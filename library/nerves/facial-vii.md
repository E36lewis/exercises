The facial nerve, also known as the seventh cranial nerve, cranial nerve VII, or simply CN VII, is a cranial nerve that emerges
 from the pons of the brainstem, controls the muscles of facial expression, 
 and functions in the conveyance of taste sensations from the anterior two-thirds of the tongue.
