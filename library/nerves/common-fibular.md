The common fibular nerve (also known as the common peroneal nerve, external popliteal nerve, or lateral popliteal nerve) is a 
nerve in the lower leg that provides sensation over the posterolateral part of the leg and the knee joint. It divides at the knee into 
two terminal branches: the superficial fibular nerve and deep fibular nerve, which innervate the muscles of the lateral and anterior 
compartments of the leg respectively. When the common fibular nerve is damaged or compressed, foot drop can ensue.
