The inferior gluteal nerve is the main motor neuron that innervates the gluteus maximus muscle. It is responsible 
for the movement of the gluteus maximus in activities requiring the hip to extend the thigh, such as climbing stairs. Injury 
to this nerve is rare but often occurs as a complication of posterior approach to the hip during hip replacement. When damaged, 
one would develop gluteus maximus lurch, which is a gait abnormality which causes the individual to 'lurch' backwards to compensate 
lack in hip extension.
