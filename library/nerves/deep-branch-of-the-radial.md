The deep branch of the radial nerve is a branch of the radial nerve that supplies motor innervation to the muscles in the posterior compartment of the forearm. 
It arises from the radial nerve in the cubital fossa and winds around the lateral side of the radius between the two heads of the supinator muscle. 
It then descends between the superficial and deep layers of muscles in the posterior compartment of the forearm.
