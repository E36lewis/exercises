The nerve to the piriformis is a branch of the sacral plexus that provides innervation to the piriformis muscle. It arises from the 
ventral rami of the first and second sacral spinal nerves. It enters the anterior surface of the piriformis muscle. This nerve may be double. 
This nerve is not to be confused with the inferior gluteal nerve, which also arises from posterior divisions of the first and second sacral 
ventral rami (L5, S1, S2)
