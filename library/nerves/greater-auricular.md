The great auricular nerve is a cutaneous nerve of the head. It provides sensory nerve supply 
to the skin over the parotid gland and the mastoid process of the temporal bone, and surfaces of 
the outer ear. Pain resulting from parotitis is caused by an impingement on the great auricular nerve.
