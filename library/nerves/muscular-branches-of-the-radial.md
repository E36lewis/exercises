The radial nerve is a major nerve in the upper extremity that supplies sensation to the back of the hand and forearm, and motor 
innervation to the triceps brachii, anconeus, brachioradialis, and extensor carpi radialis longus muscles.

The muscular branches of the radial nerve are:

Muscular branches to the triceps brachii, 
Muscular branches to the anconeus, 
Muscular branches to the brachioradialis, and 
Muscular branches to the extensor carpi radialis longus. 

The radial nerve gives off muscular branches to these muscles as it passes through the posterior compartment of the upper arm. The muscular 
branches to the triceps brachii supply motor innervation to the long head, medial head, and lateral head of the triceps brachii muscles. 
The muscular branches to the anconeus supply motor innervation to the anconeus muscle. The muscular branches to the brachioradialis supply motor 
innervation to the brachioradialis muscle. The muscular branches to the extensor carpi radialis longus supply motor innervation to the extensor 
carpi radialis longus muscle.
