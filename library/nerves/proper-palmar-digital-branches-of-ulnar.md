The proper palmar digital branches of the ulnar nerve are peripheral nerve branches that arise from the ulnar nerve in the hand. 
These branches provide sensory innervation to the palmar (palm) aspect of the fingers and play a crucial role in transmitting sensory information 
from the fingers to the central nervous system.

Proper Palmar Digital Branches:
The proper palmar digital branches originate from the ulnar nerve as it courses through the hand.
These branches typically arise distal to the common palmar digital branches in the region of the hypothenar eminence, located on the ulnar 
side of the palm near the base of the little finger.
The proper palmar digital branches extend distally along the palmar aspect of the fingers, branching out to supply sensory innervation.
Each branch is named according to the finger it supplies, such as the proper palmar digital branch of the ulnar nerve to the little finger.
The proper palmar digital branches join with branches of the median nerve to supply the palmar surfaces of the fingers.
The proper palmar digital branches of the ulnar nerve are responsible for transmitting sensory information from the palmar skin of the fingers. 
They play a vital role in the perception of touch, temperature, pressure, and other sensory stimuli from the palmar surfaces of the fingers.
