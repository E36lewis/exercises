 The nerve primarily consists of a single trunk that provides sensory innervation to the medial (inner) aspect of the forearm.

The medial antebrachial cutaneous nerve courses along the medial side of the forearm, parallel to the brachial artery. 
It supplies sensory innervation to the skin on the medial aspect of the forearm, extending from the elbow region down to the wrist.

The nerve is responsible for transmitting sensory information from the skin in its distribution area, allowing for the 
perception of touch, temperature, pain, and other sensory stimuli on the medial side of the forearm.
