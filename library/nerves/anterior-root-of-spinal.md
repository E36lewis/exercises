In anatomy and neurology, the ventral root of spinal nerve, anterior root, or motor root is the efferent motor root of a spinal nerve.

It contains efferent nerve fibres, which carry stimuli away from the CNS towards their target structures. The cell bodies of the anterior root 
neurons are located in the central grey matter of the spinal cord.

At its distal end, the ventral root joins with the dorsal root to form a mixed spinal nerve.

The ventral root is responsible for innervating the skeletal muscles of the body. It does this by sending motor fibers to the muscles, 
which cause them to contract. The ventral root is also responsible for controlling the reflexes of the body, such as the knee jerk reflex.
