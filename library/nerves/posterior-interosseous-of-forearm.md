The posterior interosseous nerve (or dorsal interosseous nerve) is a nerve in the forearm. It is the continuation 
of the deep branch of the radial nerve, after this has crossed the supinator muscle. It is considerably diminished in size 
compared to the deep branch of the radial nerve.
