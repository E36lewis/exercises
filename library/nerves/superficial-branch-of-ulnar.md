The superficial branch of the ulnar nerve is a terminal branch of the ulnar nerve. It supplies the palmaris brevis and the 
skin on the ulnar side of the hand. It also divides into a common palmar digital nerve and a proper palmar digital nerve.

Origin: The superficial branch of the ulnar nerve arises from the ulnar nerve just distal to the pisiform bone.

Course: The superficial branch of the ulnar nerve passes superficial to the flexor retinaculum and then divides into a common palmar digital nerve and a proper palmar digital nerve.
