The palmar branch of the median nerve is a small nerve that arises from the median nerve in the forearm. It supplies sensation to the 
skin on the palm of the hand, including the thenar eminence (the ball of the thumb) and the proximal part of the palm.

The palmar branch of the median nerve arises from the median nerve about 5 cm proximal to the wrist crease. It travels in the forearm superficial 
to the flexor retinaculum. It pierces the palmar carpal ligament and divides into three branches:

The lateral branch supplies sensation to the skin over the ball of the thumb.
The medial branch supplies sensation to the skin of the proximal part of the palm.
The intermediate branch supplies sensation to the skin between the lateral and medial branches.
