The lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve, also known as the lateral cutaneous nerve of the forearm, is a sensory nerve that arises from the 
musculocutaneous nerve. It supplies sensation to the skin of the lateral forearm.

The lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve arises from the musculocutaneous nerve just proximal to the elbow joint. It then pierces the deep 
fascia and divides into two branches:

The lateral branch supplies sensation to the skin over the lateral half of the forearm.
The medial branch supplies sensation to the skin over the radial half of the forearm.
