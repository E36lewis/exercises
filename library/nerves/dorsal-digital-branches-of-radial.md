The dorsal digital branches of the radial nerve are peripheral nerve branches that originate from the radial nerve in the hand. 
These branches provide sensory innervation to the dorsal (back) aspect of the fingers and play a crucial role in transmitting 
sensory information from the fingers to the central nervous system.

Dorsal Digital Branches:
The dorsal digital branches arise from the radial nerve as it enters the hand.
These branches typically emerge from the radial nerve in the region of the anatomical snuffbox, 
located on the back of the hand at the base of the thumb.
The dorsal digital branches extend along the dorsal aspect of the fingers.
Each branch supplies sensory innervation to the skin on the dorsal surface of its respective finger.
The branches usually divide further into smaller terminal branches that reach the fingertips.
The dorsal digital branches of the radial nerve are responsible for transmitting sensory information from the 
dorsal skin of the fingers. They play a vital role in the perception of touch, temperature, pressure, and other 
sensory stimuli from the dorsal surfaces of the fingers.
