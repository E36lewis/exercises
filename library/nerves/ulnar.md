The ulnar collateral ligament of elbow joint is in relation with the ulnar nerve. The nerve is the largest in the human 
body unprotected by muscle or bone, so injury is common. This nerve is directly connected to the little finger, and the adjacent half 
of the ring finger, innervating the palmar aspect of these fingers, including both front and back of the tips, perhaps as far back as 
the fingernail beds.
