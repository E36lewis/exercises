The long thoracic nerve innervates the serratus anterior muscle. Serratus anterior is responsible for protraction of the scapula, 
a movement that occurs when throwing a punch. Furthermore, the serratus anterior acts with the upper and lower fibers of the trapezius muscle 
to sustain upward rotation of the scapula, which allows for overhead lifting
