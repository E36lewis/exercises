The sural nerve is a sensory nerve that arises from the tibial nerve in the lower leg. It supplies sensation to the skin on the 
lateral side of the lower leg, heel, and ankle.

The sural nerve arises from the tibial nerve in the popliteal fossa, behind the knee. It then travels down 
the back of the lower leg, superficial to the gastrocnemius and soleus muscles. The sural nerve divides into two terminal branches, 
the lateral calcaneal nerve and the lateral dorsal cutaneous nerve of the foot.

The lateral calcaneal nerve supplies sensation to the skin on the lateral side of the heel. The lateral dorsal cutaneous nerve 
of the foot supplies sensation to the skin on the lateral side of the dorsum of the foot.
