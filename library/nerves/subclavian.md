The subclavian nerve is a small nerve that arises from the upper trunk of the brachial plexus. It innervates the subclavius muscle. 
The subclavian nerve is located in the posterior triangle of the neck, in front of the subclavian artery and the lower trunk of the brachial plexus. 
It is usually connected by a filament with the phrenic nerve.
