The medial brachial cutaneous nerve is a sensory nerve that arises from the medial cord of the brachial plexus. It supplies sensation to the medial 
side of the upper arm.

The medial brachial cutaneous nerve arises from the medial cord of the brachial plexus, and pierces the deep fascia of the arm about 10 cm proximal to the medial 
epicondyle of the humerus. It then divides into two branches:

The anterior branch supplies sensation to the skin of the medial side of the upper arm, as far distally as the elbow joint.
The posterior branch supplies sensation to the skin of the medial side of the upper arm, as far distally as the olecranon process of the ulna.
