The anterior cutaneous branches of the femoral nerve are two nerves that arise from the femoral nerve in the femoral triangle. 
They supply sensation to the skin of the anterior and medial thigh.

Medial femoral cutaneous nerve of the thigh
The medial femoral cutaneous nerve of the thigh arises from the femoral nerve in the femoral triangle, and pierces the fascia lata about 1 cm below the inguinal 
ligament. It then descends along the medial side of the thigh, and supplies sensation to the skin of the medial thigh as far as the knee.

Intermediate femoral cutaneous nerve of the thigh
The intermediate femoral cutaneous nerve of the thigh arises from the femoral nerve in the femoral triangle, and pierces the fascia lata about 
7.5 cm below the inguinal ligament. It then divides into two branches, which supply sensation to the skin of the anteromedial thigh.
