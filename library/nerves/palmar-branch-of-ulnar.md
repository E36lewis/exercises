The palmar branch of the ulnar nerve is a sensory nerve that supplies the skin on the palm of the hand, including the ulnar 
side of the little finger and half of the ring finger. It also supplies motor innervation to the palmaris brevis muscle in the hypothenar 
region of the hand. It arises from the ulnar nerve just distal to the pisiform bone and often divides into two palmar digital nerves.

The palmar branch of the ulnar nerve arises from the ulnar nerve just distal to the pisiform bone. It travels in the forearm superficial to 
the flexor retinaculum. It pierces the palmar carpal ligament and divides into two branches:

The superficial branch supplies sensation to the skin on the ulnar side of the little finger and half of the ring finger.
The deep branch supplies motor innervation to the palmaris brevis muscle in the hypothenar region of the hand.
