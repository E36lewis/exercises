The suboccipital nerve (first cervical dorsal ramus) is the dorsal primary ramus of the first cervical nerve (C1). 
It exits the spinal cord between the skull and the first cervical vertebra, the atlas. It lies within the suboccipital triangle along with 
he vertebral artery, where the artery enters the foramen magnum. It supplies muscles of the suboccipital triangle, the rectus capitis 
posterior major, obliquus capitis superior, and obliquus capitis inferior. The suboccipital nerve also innervates rectus capitis 
posterior minor.
