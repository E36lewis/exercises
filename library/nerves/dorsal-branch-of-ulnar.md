The dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve is a cutaneous nerve that arises from the ulnar nerve in the forearm. It supplies sensation to the skin on the back of the hand 
and the fifth finger.

The dorsal branch of the ulnar nerve arises from the ulnar nerve in the distal third of the forearm, just proximal to the wrist joint. It then pierces the deep fascia and 
divides into two branches:

The medial branch supplies sensation to the skin on the back of the hand, between the fifth finger and the ulnar side of the fourth finger.
The lateral branch supplies sensation to the skin on the back of the fifth finger.
