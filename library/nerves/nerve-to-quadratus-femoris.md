The nerve to quadratus femoris is a branch of the sacral plexus that provides innervation to the quadratus femoris and inferior 
gemellus muscles. It arises from the ventral divisions of the fourth lumbar spinal nerve, fifth lumbar spinal nerve, and first sacral spinal nerve. 
It leaves the pelvis through the greater sciatic foramen, below the piriformis muscle. It runs down in front of the sciatic nerve, the superior 
and inferior gemellus muscles, and the tendon of the obturator internus. It enters the anterior surfaces of quadratus femoris muscle and gemellus 
inferior muscle. It gives an articular branch to the hip joint.
