The pudendal nerve is the main nerve of the perineum. It carries sensation from the external genitalia of both sexes 
and the skin around the anus and perineum, as well as the motor supply to various pelvic muscles, including the male or female external 
urethral sphincter and the external anal sphincter. If damaged, most commonly by childbirth, lesions may cause sensory loss or fecal 
incontinence. The nerve may be temporarily blocked as part of an anaesthetic procedure.
