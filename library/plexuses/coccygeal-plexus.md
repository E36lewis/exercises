Coccygeal plexus originates from the ventral rami of spinal nerves S4, S5, and Co. 
    It is interconnected with the lower part of Sacral plexus. The only nerve of the plexus is the 
    coccygeal nerve, that serves sensory innervation of the skin in the coccygeal region.
