The slide board lateral lunge is a side lunge in which the straight leg slides out across a low-friction board. The standing leg bends and the sliding leg stretches. It works the inner thigh through a long range.

### How to do it
1. Place a slide board on a flat floor. Stand on the board with one foot on the sliding surface, or use a sock or a slider on one foot.
2. Hold your hands in front of you or on your hips.
3. Shift your weight onto the standing leg and bend your hip and knee.
4. Slide the other foot out to the side with a straight leg.
5. Go as far as you can control, keeping your chest up.
6. Push down through the standing foot and drag the sliding foot back in.

**Tips:** Do 3 sets of 6 to 10 reps per side, slowly. Start with a short slide and increase the range over a few weeks. Keep your knee over your toes. Stop if you feel a sharp pull in the groin. Holding a light weight at your chest makes it harder.

### In daily life
Stepping wide to the side to reach something, getting out of a car and recovering a slip all use the inner thigh in this way.

### Why it matters
Skating and field sports load the inner thigh with every push and stride. This builds strength and control there, which helps to prevent groin strains.
