The slide board reverse lunge sends one foot backward along a low-friction board while the front leg bends. The slide is smooth, like the delivery in curling. It trains control of the front leg and the hips.

### How to do it
1. Place a slide board on a flat floor. Stand with one foot on the board with a slider and the other on the floor.
2. Put your hands on your hips or hold them in front of you.
3. Slide the sliding foot backward, bending the front knee.
4. Lower until your back knee is close to the floor, or as far as you can control.
5. Push through the front foot and pull the back foot forward to the start.
6. Finish all reps, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Do 3 sets of 6 to 10 reps per side. Keep your front knee over the middle of your foot and your trunk upright. Move slowly, and use a wall or rail nearby for balance at first. Stop if you feel knee pain.

### In daily life
Stepping back from a low shelf, getting up from a low stool and stepping down stairs backward use this movement.

### Why it matters
Curling athletes slide in a deep lunge on the delivery, and skaters push in a similar line. This builds the leg control that holds those positions.
