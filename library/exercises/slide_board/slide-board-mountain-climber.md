The slide board mountain climber is a mountain climber with the feet on a slide board. In a push-up position you slide your knees in and out. The smooth slide keeps the trunk and hip flexors working continuously.

### How to do it
1. Place a slide board on the floor, or put sliders under your toes.
2. Get into a high plank position with your hands under your shoulders and feet on the sliding surface.
3. Brace your trunk and squeeze your glutes.
4. Slide one knee toward your chest, then slide it back as the other knee comes in.
5. Keep alternating in a steady rhythm.
6. Keep your hips level and stop when your form slips.

**Tips:** A usual plan is 6 to 10 rounds of 20 to 30 seconds with 30 to 40 seconds' rest. Don't let your hips bounce or your lower back sag. Make sure that your hands have a firm, non-slip grip.

### In daily life
Crawling on your hands and knees, getting down to the floor to play and pushing yourself up use this trunk position.

### Why it matters
Skaters keep a strong trunk and fast hips as they push and recover. This builds hip flexor and trunk endurance in a position similar to the skating stride.
