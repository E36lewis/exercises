A barbell curl with an overhand grip (palms down). It works the brachioradialis, the forearm muscle along the thumb side, and the brachialis and biceps in the upper arm, and it challenges your wrist extensors and grip.

### How to do it
1. Stand holding a barbell with an overhand grip, hands shoulder-width apart.
2. Keep your elbows close to your sides.
3. Brace your abdomen and keep your wrists straight.
4. Curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
5. Pause at the top.
6. Lower slowly until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Use a lighter weight than your normal curl, as the overhand grip is weaker. Don't swing your body or let your wrists bend back. Keep your upper arms still.

### In daily life
Carrying a bucket or a tray with your palms down, or lifting a pot by its lid, relies on the forearm strength built here.

### Why it matters
It can build forearm and grip strength and balance the work of regular curls, and strong forearms can support your elbows.
