The barbell incline bench press lowers and presses a bar while you lie on an angled bench. The slope emphasises the upper chest, with the front of the shoulders and the triceps helping.

### How to do it
1. Set the bench to a slight to moderate angle and lie back with your eyes under the bar.
2. Plant your feet and pull your shoulder blades back and down.
3. Grip the bar a little wider than shoulder-width and unrack it, with your arms straight.
4. Lower the bar to your upper chest in control.
5. Press it up until your arms are straight.
6. Rerack the bar when you finish your set.

**Tips:** Keep your feet flat and your hips on the bench. Don't bounce the bar off your chest. Use a spotter or safety arms for heavy sets.

### In daily life
Pressing a stuck window or pushing a loaded cart uphill draws on the same chest-and-shoulder push.

### Why it matters
A bar lets you add small amounts of load steadily, so the lift is useful for tracking progress. It can help build upper-chest and pressing strength.
