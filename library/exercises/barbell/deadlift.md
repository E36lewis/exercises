The barbell deadlift lifts a loaded bar from the floor to a standing position. It is a hip hinge that works the glutes, hamstrings and thighs, while your back and grip hold the bar and your trunk stays braced.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet.
2. Hinge at your hips and bend your knees to grip the bar just outside your legs.
3. Straighten your back, lift your chest and tighten your upper back so the slack is out of the bar.
4. Push through your feet and stand up, keeping the bar close to your legs.
5. Finish standing tall with your hips and knees straight, without leaning back.
6. Lower the bar by pushing your hips back first, then bending your knees, keeping it close until it touches the floor.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat throughout. Don't jerk the bar off the floor. Start light and add weight gradually.

### In daily life
It is the pattern for lifting a heavy box, a bag of soil or a full laundry basket from the floor.

### Why it matters
It can help build strength in the back of the body, from your calves to your upper back, and supports safe lifting habits.
