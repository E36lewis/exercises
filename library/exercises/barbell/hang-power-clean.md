The hang power clean is an Olympic-style lift in which you pull a barbell from just above the knee up to your shoulders in one fast movement, and catch it with your thighs above parallel. It trains explosive hip, leg and back power.

### How to do it
1. Hold a barbell with an overhand grip just outside your legs and stand tall, then push your hips back and lower the bar to just above your knees, with a flat back and soft knees.
2. Set your shoulders over the bar and brace your core.
3. Jump by driving through your legs and hips, then shrug your shoulders up.
4. As the bar rises, pull your elbows high and fast under the bar and catch it on your front shoulders, with your hips and knees bending slightly.
5. Stand tall, then lower the bar back to the hang position under control.

**Tips:** Keep the bar close to your body and let the legs and hips do the work, not the arms. Learn the movement with an empty bar or a light weight, ideally with a coach. Use bumper plates on a lifting platform, and drop the bar safely if you lose control.

### In daily life
It trains the fast, full-body effort of throwing a heavy object up onto a shelf or lifting it to your chest.

### Why it matters
Collision, field, court and combat sports all rely on quick force through the legs and hips. Starting from the hang makes the lift easier to learn than a full clean from the floor.
