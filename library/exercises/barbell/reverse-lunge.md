A lunge where you step backward with a barbell on your upper back. It works the quadriceps (the front of the thigh) and glutes, with the hamstrings and calves helping, and your trunk working to hold the bar steady.

### How to do it
1. Set a barbell across your upper back, resting on your shoulder muscles, and stand tall.
2. Brace your abdomen and keep your chest up.
3. Step back with one foot, landing on the ball of that foot.
4. Lower your back knee toward the floor.
5. Push through your front foot to return to standing.
6. Alternate legs, or finish one side first.

**Tips:** Use a rack with safety stops, and start with an empty bar. Keep your torso tall and your front knee over your ankle. Have a spotter for heavy loads.

### In daily life
Stepping back while carrying a load on your shoulders, like a backpack or a pole, calls for the same balance and control.

### Why it matters
The barbell allows heavier loads than dumbbells for the legs, and the step back lets you work one leg at a time.
