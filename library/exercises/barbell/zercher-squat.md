The Zercher squat is a barbell squat in which the bar sits in the crooks of your elbows, in front of your body. This front load pulls your torso forward, so the upper back and trunk work hard to stay upright. It trains the thighs, glutes and hamstrings, along with the core and upper back.

### How to do it
1. Set a barbell in a rack at about waist height, with safety bars set below your squat depth.
2. Place the bar in the crooks of both elbows, hold your forearms together or clasp your hands, and stand up to lift it off the rack.
3. Step back, and set your feet about shoulder-width apart with your toes turned slightly out.
4. Brace your abs, keep your chest up and sit down and back into a squat.
5. Lower until your thighs are about parallel to the floor, or as low as you can keep a tall torso.
6. Press through your whole foot to stand up.

**Tips:** Wrap a pad or towel around the bar, because it can bruise the inside of your elbows. Keep your elbows close to your ribs. Use a rack with safeties, and start with a light bar.

### In daily life
It resembles lifting and carrying a heavy box or a bag of soil held against your stomach and standing up with it.

### Why it matters
Holding the load in front trains the upper back and trunk to resist being pulled forward, which makes it a useful way to practise staying upright under load.
