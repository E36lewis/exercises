The close grip bench press is a bench press with your hands closer together, about shoulder-width. It shifts more of the work onto the triceps and the inner chest, while the shoulders help.

### How to do it
1. Lie on a bench with your eyes under the bar and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Grip the bar with your hands about shoulder-width apart.
3. Lift the bar off the rack and hold it over your chest with your arms straight.
4. Lower it under control to your lower chest, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
5. Press the bar back up until your arms are straight, then rack it.

**Tips:** Do not grip so narrowly that your wrists bend. Keep your shoulder blades pulled back and your elbows tucked. Use a spotter or set the rack's safety bars, and use collars on the bar.

### In daily life
It trains the push used to get up from the floor or to push a heavy door.

### Why it matters
Collision and combat sports need strong triceps and chest for pushing, blocking and punching.
