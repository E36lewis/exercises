The clean and jerk is a barbell lift in two parts: you pull the bar from the floor to your shoulders (the clean), then drive it overhead (the jerk). It uses nearly the whole body: legs, hips, back, shoulders and arms, all timed together.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over your mid-foot. Grip it just outside your knees, with a flat back and your chest up.
2. Push through your feet and stand up, keeping the bar close as it passes your knees.
3. Extend your hips and shrug hard, then pull yourself under the bar and catch it on your shoulders with your elbows forward.
4. Stand up fully, then dip a short way by bending your knees, keeping your torso upright.
5. Drive up quickly and press the bar overhead, dropping slightly under it or splitting your feet.
6. Stand with the bar over your shoulders and hips, then lower it to your shoulders and to the floor with control.

**Tips:** Keep the bar close to your body. At the top, move your head slightly through your arms. Learn the movement with a light bar in a clear space where you can safely drop the weight. A coach can help you learn it.

### In daily life
It matches getting a heavy, awkward object from the floor to overhead, such as loading a roof box.

### Why it matters
It can help build full-body strength, speed and timing, which supports running, jumping and throwing.
