The drag curl is a barbell biceps curl in which the bar slides up along your body while your elbows move back. It works the biceps, with the forearm muscles helping you hold on.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart, holding the bar with an underhand grip, arms hanging.
2. Keep the bar against your thighs.
3. Pull your elbows back behind you as you lift the bar.
4. Let the bar drag up your thighs and stomach.
5. Squeeze at the top, with the bar near your chest and your elbows behind your body.
6. Lower the bar along the same path.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders still and your torso upright. Avoid swinging with your back. Use a weight you can lift without leaning.

### In daily life
It copies pulling a heavy item close to your body, such as lifting a bag of groceries from a counter to your chest.

### Why it matters
It can help build biceps strength. Keeping the elbows back moves the work away from the front of the shoulder, which some people find more comfortable.
