The front squat is a squat with a barbell resting across the front of your shoulders. It works the thighs and glutes, and the upright position also trains the upper back and trunk.

### How to do it
1. Rest the bar across your front shoulders, elbows high.
2. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Sit down, keeping your chest tall.
5. Lower until your thighs are about parallel to the floor.
6. Push through your feet to stand.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows up. Don't let your knees collapse inwards. Use safety bars or a rack.

### In daily life
It matches standing up from a low seat while holding a load at chest height.

### Why it matters
It can help build leg strength and an upright posture, since the bar in front of you makes it hard to lean forward.
