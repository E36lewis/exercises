The power snatch is a snatch in which you catch the bar overhead in a quarter squat, with your thighs above parallel. It is simpler to learn than the full snatch and builds fast, full-body power.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet, with a wide overhand grip.
2. Set your back flat, chest up and shoulders just over the bar.
3. Push the floor away to lift the bar past your knees, then jump and shrug as it passes your hips.
4. Pull yourself under the bar and catch it overhead with locked arms in a quarter squat.
5. Stand fully upright, then lower the bar to the floor under control.

**Tips:** Learn it with a broomstick, then an empty bar, ideally with a coach. Use a platform with bumper plates and collars, and drop the bar in front of you if you miss. Keep the bar close to your body.

### In daily life
It trains the fast lift and balance used to hoist something heavy and overhead.

### Why it matters
Collision and field sports rely on quick, powerful hips and legs. The power snatch builds this with a smaller catch position than the full snatch.
