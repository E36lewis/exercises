The barbell sumo deadlift is a deadlift with a wide stance, toes turned out and a narrow grip inside your knees. The wide stance keeps the torso more upright than a conventional deadlift. It works the glutes, inner thighs, thighs and hamstrings, with the back and core holding firm.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wide, toes turned out, and the bar over your mid-foot.
2. Bend down and grip the bar with your hands inside your knees.
3. Push your knees out over your toes and lift your chest.
4. Brace your core and flatten your back.
5. Push the floor apart with your feet as you stand, keeping the bar close to your legs.
6. Stand tall, squeeze your glutes, then lower the bar the same way.

**Tips:** Do not let your back round. Make sure your hips and chest rise together rather than your hips shooting up first. Use a weight you can lift with a flat back. Chalk can help your grip.

### In daily life
It trains lifting a heavy object from the ground with a wide stance, such as a box, a bag of soil or a car seat.

### Why it matters
The upright torso and wide stance can feel kinder on the lower back for some lifters, and it builds hip and leg strength.
