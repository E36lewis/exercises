The barbell hack squat holds the bar behind your legs, so you squat with the load hanging down behind you. It works the thighs and glutes, and the position keeps your torso fairly upright.

### How to do it
1. Place a loaded barbell on the floor behind you, along the line of your heels.
2. Stand with your heels just in front of the bar and your feet about hip-width apart.
3. Squat down and reach back to grip the bar with both hands, palms facing away from you.
4. Brace your abdomen, lift your chest and keep your back flat.
5. Push through your feet to stand, keeping the bar close to the backs of your legs.
6. Stand tall, then bend your knees to lower the bar back to the floor in control.

**Tips:** Keep your chest up and your back flat. Start with a light bar. Keep your grip firm.

### In daily life
It mirrors picking up a suitcase or a bag that sits behind you while you keep your torso up and let your legs do the lifting.

### Why it matters
Because the load hangs behind you and your back stays upright, the thighs do much of the work. That can help build leg strength and practise a strong, upright lift from the floor.
