A barbell row where the bar starts on the floor each rep and your torso stays roughly parallel to the ground. It works the latissimus dorsi (lats) and mid-back muscles, with the arms and lower back helping.

### How to do it
1. Stand with the bar over your mid-foot, feet hip-width apart.
2. Hinge forward with a flat back until your torso is roughly parallel to the floor.
3. Grip the bar slightly wider than your shoulders.
4. Brace your core.
5. Pull the bar quickly to your lower chest.
6. Lower it under control back to the floor and reset between reps.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and don't use body swing. Let the bar settle on the floor each rep. Use a weight that lets you keep a straight spine.

### In daily life
Pulling a load from the ground while your trunk stays braced is how you drag a heavy bag out of a boot or yank a stuck door open.

### Why it matters
Because every rep restarts from a dead stop, it trains a strong, fast pull and can support posture and shoulder health.
