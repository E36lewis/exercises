A sit-up where you hold a light barbell straight above you the whole way up and down. The abdominals and hip flexors do the sit-up, while the shoulders and arms hold the bar overhead.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat, with your feet anchored if you need to.
2. Hold a light barbell above your chest with your arms straight.
3. Brace your abdomen and keep your arms locked.
4. Sit up, curling your trunk forward until the bar is over your shoulders and then your hips.
5. Pause at the top with your ribs stacked over your hips.
6. Lower yourself slowly, keeping the bar overhead, until your back is on the floor.

**Tips:** Start with a very light bar, or just a broomstick. Don't pull on your neck or drop the bar behind you. Stop if you feel any pain in your lower back or shoulders.

### In daily life
Sitting up from lying down while holding something over your head, such as passing a package up a ladder or lifting a child from a bed, uses this pattern.

### Why it matters
It can help build a strong trunk and steady shoulders in one movement, so your arms stay stable while your core works.
