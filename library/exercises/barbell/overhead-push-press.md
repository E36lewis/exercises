A barbell press that uses a short dip and drive from your legs to help send the bar overhead. It works the shoulders, triceps and upper chest, while the legs and core pass force up to the bar.

### How to do it
1. Hold the bar at shoulder height with your hands just outside your shoulders.
2. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart.
3. Bend your knees slightly, keeping your torso upright.
4. Drive up quickly through your legs.
5. Use that momentum to press the bar overhead until your arms are straight.
6. Lower the bar to your shoulders and bend your knees to absorb it.

**Tips:** The dip is short and vertical. Keep your core braced and your ribs down. Use a weight you can control, as the extra speed makes mistakes more costly.

### In daily life
Using your legs to start a lift is how you heave a heavy bag or box up onto a high shelf without relying only on your arms.

### Why it matters
It trains power transfer from the legs to the arms, and it lets you move more weight overhead than a strict press can.
