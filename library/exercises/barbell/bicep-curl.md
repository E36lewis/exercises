The barbell bicep curl is a basic arm exercise. It builds the biceps at the front of the upper arm, with help from the deeper elbow flexors and the forearm. Using both hands on one bar lets you lift a fairly heavy load.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart, holding a barbell with an underhand grip about shoulder-width.
2. Let the bar hang in front of your thighs with your arms straight and your elbows by your sides.
3. Bend your elbows and curl the bar up toward your shoulders.
4. Squeeze your biceps briefly at the top.
5. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight.
6. Repeat without swinging.

**Tips:** Do not rock your body or lean back. Keep your wrists straight. Lower the bar with control, and pick a weight you can lift without cheating.

### In daily life
It trains the bend at the elbow you use to lift a box or a bag from your waist up to your chest.

### Why it matters
It can help build arm and grip strength, and it is an easy exercise to load up in small steps.
