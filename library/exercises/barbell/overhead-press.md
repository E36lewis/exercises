A standing barbell lift where you press the bar from your shoulders to overhead. It works the deltoids (shoulders), triceps and upper back, and your abdomen and glutes keep your trunk upright.

### How to do it
1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding the bar at shoulder height, hands just outside your shoulders.
2. Squeeze your glutes and brace your abdomen.
3. Tuck your chin slightly as the bar passes your face.
4. Press the bar straight up until your arms are straight overhead.
5. Move your head forward under the bar.
6. Lower the bar slowly back to your shoulders.

**Tips:** Don't lean back or arch your lower back. Keep your ribs down and the bar over your mid-foot. Choose a weight you can control, and use safety stops if you are in a rack.

### In daily life
Pressing a weight straight up is what you do when you put a suitcase into an overhead locker or slide a box onto a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It can build shoulder and arm strength, and a strong overhead position supports stable shoulders.
