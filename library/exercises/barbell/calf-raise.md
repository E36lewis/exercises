The barbell calf raise is a standing exercise that lifts your heels with a bar across your upper back. The gastrocnemius and the soleus (the two main calf muscles) do the lifting, and the bar adds load.

### How to do it
1. Set a barbell on a rack at upper-chest height and place it across your upper back.
2. Step back and stand with your feet hip-width apart, toes forward.
3. Keep your knees soft and your body upright.
4. Press through the balls of your feet to lift your heels as high as you can.
5. Pause briefly at the top.
6. Lower your heels slowly, then return the bar to the rack when done.

**Tips:** Keep your body upright and do not bounce. Move slowly. Use a rack and a manageable weight, and keep a firm hold on the bar.

### In daily life
It strengthens the ankle push-off you use to walk uphill and to reach something on a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It can help build calf strength, which supports the ankle and helps with walking and running.
