The barbell hip thrust drives a loaded bar upward by extending your hips while your upper back rests on a bench. It is one of the most direct ways to train the gluteus maximus with heavy load.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your upper back against the edge of a bench and a padded barbell across your hips.
2. Plant your feet flat on the floor, about hip-width apart, knees bent.
3. Brace your abdomen and tuck your chin slightly.
4. Drive through your heels and lift your hips until your torso is level with the floor.
5. Squeeze your glutes at the top and keep your ribs down.
6. Lower your hips in control until just above the floor, then repeat.

**Tips:** Use a pad or mat on the bar. Keep your eyes forward and your chin tucked. Avoid arching your lower back at the top.

### In daily life
Standing up from a low seat or climbing a steep hill relies on the same hip extension.

### Why it matters
The movement loads the glutes most when they are fully contracted at the top. That can help build glute strength, and the lift is stable and easy to progress in small steps.
