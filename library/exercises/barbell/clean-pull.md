The clean pull is the first half of a clean. You lift a barbell from the floor with a fast leg and hip drive and finish with a shrug, but you do not catch the bar. It builds explosive power without the technical demand of the catch.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet, gripping it just outside your legs.
2. Set your back flat, chest up and shoulders slightly over the bar.
3. Push the floor away to bring the bar to your knees, then drive your hips forward powerfully.
4. Shrug your shoulders up and rise onto your toes, with your elbows pointing out.
5. Lower the bar under control to the floor and reset.

**Tips:** Keep the bar close to your legs and your arms straight until the shrug. Use a lifting platform with bumper plates and collars. Start with light weights to learn the timing.

### In daily life
It builds the explosive drive of the legs and hips for lifting a heavy load.

### Why it matters
Collision and field sports need power to sprint, tackle and jump. The clean pull builds this with less technical learning than the full clean.
