This is an overhead press with a barbell, done sitting down on a bench with back support. You press the bar from upper-chest height until your arms are straight. It mainly works the shoulders (deltoids), with the triceps, upper back and core helping.

### How to do it
1. Set a bench upright and sit with your feet flat and your back supported.
2. Hold the barbell just wider than your shoulders, at upper-chest level.
3. Brace your core.
4. Press the bar straight overhead until your arms are straight.
5. Pause briefly at the top.
6. Lower the bar slowly to your upper chest.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and avoid arching your back. Tilt your head back slightly as the bar passes your face, then bring it forward again, so the bar travels in a straight line. Use safety stops or a spotter when pressing heavy loads.

### In daily life
Lifting a heavy box onto a high shelf and heaving a bag into a roof rack both use this straight overhead push.

### Why it matters
Back support lets you focus on pressing, so you can build strength and size in the shoulders. It also builds stability for overhead lifting.
