The push jerk is a fast overhead lift. You dip your knees slightly, drive the barbell up from your shoulders with your legs and press yourself under it, then stand. It builds overhead power and speed.

### How to do it
1. Take a barbell on a rack at shoulder height, and hold it on the front of your shoulders with your elbows slightly forward.
2. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and brace your core.
3. Dip a few inches by bending your knees, keeping your torso upright.
4. Drive up explosively and press the bar overhead, bending your knees slightly again as you catch it with locked arms.
5. Stand fully upright, then lower the bar to your shoulders.

**Tips:** Keep your torso upright through the dip and drive. Lock your arms fully at the catch. Use a rack with safety bars, or bumper plates and a clear space, and drop the bar in front of you if you fail.

### In daily life
It trains the fast push of lifting something heavy onto a high shelf.

### Why it matters
Collision and combat sports need fast, powerful extension from the ground up through the arms for blocking, shoving and striking.
