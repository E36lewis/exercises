The skull crusher is a lying barbell triceps exercise. Only your elbows bend, so the triceps at the back of the upper arm do almost all the work. It is also called a lying triceps extension.

### How to do it
1. Lie on a flat bench with your feet on the floor, holding a barbell with an overhand grip, hands about shoulder-width apart.
2. Press the bar up over your chest, then tilt your arms back slightly so they point a little past vertical.
3. Keep your upper arms still. Bend only at the elbows and lower the bar toward your forehead or just behind your head.
4. Stop when the bar is a short distance from your head.
5. Straighten your elbows to return the bar to the start.
6. When you finish, bring the bar to your chest and rack it.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows pointing at the ceiling rather than flaring out. Choose a weight you can control, because the bar travels close to your face. A spotter or an EZ-bar can make heavy sets feel more secure.

### In daily life
Straightening the elbow is what you do to push a stuck drawer shut or to reach up and slide a bag onto a high shelf.

### Why it matters
The triceps are the main elbow-extending muscles. Strengthening them can help you finish presses such as the bench press and overhead press, and it supports the elbow when you push against load.
