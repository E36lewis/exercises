The snatch is an Olympic weightlifting movement that takes a barbell from the floor to overhead in one continuous pull, catching it in a deep squat. It demands power, speed, mobility and balance, and trains the whole body.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet. Grip the bar with a wide overhand grip.
2. Set your back flat, your chest up and your shoulders just over the bar.
3. Push the floor away to bring the bar up past your knees, then jump and shrug as it passes your hips.
4. Pull yourself under the bar, catching it overhead with straight arms in a deep squat.
5. Stand up with the bar overhead. Then lower it with control to the floor, using bumper plates.

**Tips:** Work with a coach and learn with a broomstick, then an empty bar. Train on a lifting platform with bumper plates and collars, and drop the bar in front of you if you lose it. Keep the bar close and your arms straight overhead.

### In daily life
It trains reaching overhead with good balance and strength, and picking up and lifting something awkward to a high place.

### Why it matters
Collision, field, gymnastics and climbing sports need full-body power and shoulder and hip mobility. The snatch trains all of these together.
