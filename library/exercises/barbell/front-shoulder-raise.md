The front shoulder raise lifts a barbell straight in front of you to shoulder height. It works the front of the shoulder, and the grip muscles of the forearm hold the bar.

### How to do it
1. Stand holding a barbell with an overhand grip, hands shoulder-width apart.
2. Let the bar rest against your thighs.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Lift the bar to shoulder height, arms nearly straight.
5. Pause.
6. Lower slowly.

**Tips:** Don't lean back. Keep the weight light. Keep your wrists straight.

### In daily life
It matches lifting something wide and light in front of you, such as a tray or a long box.

### Why it matters
It can help build shoulder strength, with a steady two-handed hold that keeps the load even.
