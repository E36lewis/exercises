This is a hip-hinge lift with a barbell. You push your hips back to lower the bar along your thighs, then drive them forward to stand. It works the glutes and hamstrings together, while the back muscles and upper back hold your position and the thighs help.

### How to do it
1. Hold a barbell with an overhand grip just outside your thighs and stand tall, feet hip-width apart.
2. Soften your knees, pull your shoulders back and brace your core.
3. Push your hips back and slide the bar down your thighs.
4. Keep your back flat and your head in line with your spine.
5. Lower until you feel a deep stretch in your hamstrings, around mid-shin or just below the knees.
6. Squeeze your glutes and drive your hips forward to return to standing.

**Tips:** Keep the bar close to your body the whole way. Don't round your back or let the bar drift forward. Keep the load manageable so your form stays strict.

### In daily life
It trains the move you use to heave a suitcase or a sack of potting soil off the ground. It also helps you stand up smoothly after leaning over a low table.

### Why it matters
It builds strong hips and hamstrings and a tough back. A strong hinge can help with sprinting and jumping, and it supports good technique in other barbell lifts.
