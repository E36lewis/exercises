This is a lifting exercise with a barbell. You hold the bar in front of your thighs and raise your shoulders straight up towards your ears. It works the upper trapezius, the muscle between your neck and shoulders, along with the levator scapulae, while your grip holds the bar.

### How to do it
1. Hold a barbell with an overhand grip, about shoulder-width, and stand tall with the bar in front of your thighs.
2. Keep your arms straight and your core braced.
3. Lift your shoulders straight up towards your ears.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower them slowly.

**Tips:** Move straight up and down; don't roll your shoulders. Keep your arms straight and don't bend your elbows. Keep your neck long and use a weight you can control.

### In daily life
Carrying heavy shopping, a suitcase or a toolbox is hard on the muscles that keep your shoulders from dropping. Shrugs train that holding strength.

### Why it matters
It builds the muscles that hold your shoulders up, which supports carrying strength. It can also help the look and feel of neck-and-shoulder posture.
