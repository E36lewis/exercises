The jump shrug is a barbell exercise where you jump while holding the bar at your thighs and shrug your shoulders at the top. It teaches the triple extension of the ankles, knees and hips that drives sprinting and jumping.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and hold a barbell at mid-thigh with an overhand grip, arms straight.
2. Lower the bar slightly by bending your hips and knees a few centimetres.
3. Jump up as high as you can, extending your ankles, knees and hips.
4. Shrug your shoulders up at the top.
5. Land softly with bent knees, then reset.

**Tips:** Keep your arms straight and do not pull with them. Land softly with your knees bent, on a firm surface. Use a light load and bumper plates, and let the bar go if needed.

### In daily life
It trains a jump for stepping up onto a high ledge or jumping a stream.

### Why it matters
Collision, field and court sports reward fast jumping and sprinting. The jump shrug practices that explosive extension.
