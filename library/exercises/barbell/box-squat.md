The box squat is a barbell back squat in which you sit back onto a box and then stand up. The box shows you how far to go and teaches you to push your hips back. It builds strength and power out of the bottom of the squat.

### How to do it
1. Set a box or bench at a height that puts your thighs about parallel when you sit, and place a barbell on a rack at upper-chest height.
2. Step under the bar, rest it across your upper back, lift it off the rack and step back with the box behind you.
3. Set your feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, brace your core and sit your hips back to the box.
4. Sit with control, keeping your shins near vertical and your back flat.
5. Push your feet into the floor and drive up to stand, then rack the bar.

**Tips:** Do not drop onto the box or relax your trunk when you sit. Keep your chest up and your back flat. Use a stable box, and set the rack's safety bars just below the box height in case you fail.

### In daily life
It is the same movement as sitting down on a chair and standing up without using your hands.

### Why it matters
Field, collision, combat and court sports need explosive hips and legs for sprinting, jumping and tackling. The box makes the bottom position the start of the lift, which builds strength where it is hardest.
