The overhead squat is a squat with a barbell held above your head on locked arms. It demands shoulder, trunk and hip mobility and stability together, and shows any weakness in those areas.

### How to do it
1. Take a barbell on a rack and, with a wide grip, press or jerk it overhead until your arms are locked out.
2. Stand with your feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, toes turned out slightly.
3. Brace your core and push the bar slightly back over your head.
4. Squat down as deep as you can with your chest up and arms locked.
5. Drive up to stand, then lower the bar to the rack or your shoulders.

**Tips:** Start with a broomstick or an empty bar. Keep your arms locked and your ribs down. If you lose the bar, push it back and step away; do not try to save it. Set the rack's safety bars and use collars.

### In daily life
It trains the balance and mobility for reaching overhead while bending down, such as picking up something while carrying a load.

### Why it matters
Gymnastics, climbing, combat and running sports benefit from the shoulder, trunk and hip control this movement builds.
