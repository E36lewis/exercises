The barbell clean is a power lift that brings the bar from the floor to your shoulders in one fast movement. It uses the legs, hips, back and shoulders together and needs good technique.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over your mid-foot.
2. Bend down, grip the bar just outside your knees and keep your back flat.
3. Push through the floor to lift the bar past your knees.
4. Extend your hips and shrug your shoulders to speed it up.
5. Pull your elbows around and under the bar.
6. Catch the bar on the front of your shoulders in a quarter squat, then stand tall.
7. Lower the bar with control.

**Tips:** Keep the bar close and your back flat. Start with light weights or a broomstick to learn the technique. Learn from a coach and use bumper plates on a platform so you can drop the bar safely.

### In daily life
It trains the fast, full-body effort you use to lift something heavy from the ground to chest height.

### Why it matters
It can help build power, speed and coordination across the whole body.
