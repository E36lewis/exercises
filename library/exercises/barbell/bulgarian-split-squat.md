The barbell Bulgarian split squat is a single-leg squat with a bar on your upper back and your rear foot on a bench. It loads the thighs and glutes heavily and asks your trunk to stay steady under the bar.

### How to do it
1. Set a barbell on a rack at upper-chest height and place it across your upper back.
2. Step back and stand about two steps in front of a bench.
3. Place the top of one foot on the bench behind you.
4. Lower your hips straight down until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor.
5. Press through your front foot to stand, keeping your torso steady.
6. Finish your reps, return the bar to the rack, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your front knee over your toes. Do not let the bar roll. Practise without a load first, and use a rack with safety bars. Dumbbells are a gentler option if you are unsure.

### In daily life
It supports climbing stairs or stepping up while carrying a load, such as a heavy backpack.

### Why it matters
It can help build strong, stable legs and checks that both sides are carrying their share.
