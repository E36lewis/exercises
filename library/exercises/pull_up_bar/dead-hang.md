The dead hang is simply hanging from a bar with straight arms for time. The fingers and forearms hold your whole body weight. It builds grip and gently decompresses the shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Set a pull up bar high enough that your feet can hang free. Use a box to step up if needed.
2. Grip the bar with an overhand grip, hands about shoulder-width apart.
3. Step off the box and hang with your arms straight.
4. Keep your shoulders active by gently pulling them away from your ears, and your body still.
5. Hold for the planned time, then step back down onto the box.

**Tips:** Typical sets are 3 to 5 holds of 20 to 60 seconds, with 1 to 2 minutes' rest. Stop before your grip fails completely and step down to a box rather than dropping. Build up time slowly, because the finger tendons adapt slower than the muscles.

### In daily life
Hanging from a monkey bar, carrying heavy bags in each hand and holding on to a strap on a moving bus all test your grip like this.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and rock climbers hang from their fingers for long periods, and a strong grip with healthy shoulders keeps them safe. A dead hang is a simple way to build that grip and shoulder endurance.
