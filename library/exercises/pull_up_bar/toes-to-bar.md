Toes to bar raises your straight legs all the way up until your feet touch the bar you are hanging from. It trains the abdominals and hip flexors through a large range, and it takes strong shoulders and grip.

### How to do it
1. Hang from a pull up bar with an overhand grip, hands just wider than your shoulders.
2. Pull your shoulders down and brace your trunk.
3. Start with a small backward swing of the legs.
4. Lift your straight legs, driving your toes up toward the bar.
5. Touch the bar with your feet, then lower them with control.
6. Hang still for a moment before the next rep.

**Tips:** This is advanced. Build up through hanging knee raises and straight leg raises first. Do 3 sets of 3 to 8 reps. Control your swing, and don't let your legs drop quickly, which strains the shoulders. Stop if your grip is failing.

### In daily life
Lifting your legs to climb into a high seat and swinging yourself over an obstacle use the same hip flexion.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and rock climbers need to bring the feet to the bar or the wall and to control the body swing. Toes to bar builds that range and trunk strength.
