The neutral grip pull-up is done with the palms facing each other on parallel handles. Your wrists and elbows sit in a natural position, so it is often easier on the elbows than an overhand pull-up.

### How to do it
1. Use a pull up bar with parallel handles. Grip them with your palms facing each other.
2. Hang with your arms straight and your shoulders pulled gently down.
3. Pull your elbows down and in toward your ribs.
4. Lift your chin over the handles.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight again.

**Tips:** Do 3 to 4 sets of 5 to 10 reps, or add weight once 10 reps are easy. Start from a full hang and avoid swinging. If your elbows were bothered by other grips, this one is often the most comfortable. Stop if you feel sharp pain.

### In daily life
Pulling yourself up over a wall or onto a ledge, with your hands in a natural position, uses this grip.

### Why it matters
Gymnastics, rock-climbing and water athletes pull a lot. The neutral grip lets them do high volumes of pulling with less stress on the elbows.
