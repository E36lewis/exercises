The lock off pull up is a pull-up with a pause at the top, or at a chosen elbow angle. You hold with one or both arms bent. It builds the strength to hold a bent-arm position, as in climbing.

### How to do it
1. Hang from a pull up bar with an overhand grip.
2. Pull yourself up until your chin is over the bar.
3. Hold at the top for 3 to 5 seconds, with your elbows bent and your shoulders down.
4. Lower yourself slowly.
5. For a harder version, hold at 90 degrees or with one arm bent for time.

**Tips:** Do 3 to 5 sets of 1 to 3 reps with pauses of 3 to 10 seconds, with at least 2 minutes' rest. Don't let your shoulders shrug up to your ears. Use a box to get to the top if you need to, and build the time slowly. Stop if you feel pain in the elbows.

### In daily life
Holding yourself on a ledge, pulling up onto a platform and hanging onto a rail rely on bent-arm strength.

### Why it matters
Rock climbers must lock off an arm to reach for the next hold, and gymnasts need to hold positions on the bar and rings. This builds that strength.
