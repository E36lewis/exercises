A bodyweight exercise where you hang from a bar and pull your chin above it. It works the latissimus dorsi and the upper back, with the biceps, forearms and shoulders helping.

### How to do it
1. Hang from a bar with an overhand grip, hands a little wider than your shoulders.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down, away from your ears.
3. Brace your abdomen and cross your ankles if you like.
4. Pull your elbows down toward your ribs, driving your chest toward the bar.
5. Raise your chin above the bar.
6. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are straight again.

**Tips:** Avoid swinging or kicking to get up. Control the way down, as it builds strength too. If you can't yet do a full rep, use a resistance band or lower yourself slowly from the top.

### In daily life
Pulling yourself up over a wall, climbing out of a pool, or hauling yourself onto a ledge uses this same pull.

### Why it matters
It is one of the best tests of relative strength, since you lift your own body weight. It can build the pulling strength that balances all the pushing we do in daily life.
