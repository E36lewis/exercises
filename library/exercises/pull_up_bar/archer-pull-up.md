The archer pull up is a wide-grip pull-up in which you pull towards one hand while the other arm stays nearly straight. It is a step toward the one-arm pull-up.

### How to do it
1. Hang from a pull up bar with a wide overhand grip, well outside shoulder width.
2. Pull your body up toward your right hand, bending that elbow.
3. Keep the left arm straight, sliding along the bar if you can.
4. Aim your chin toward the right hand.
5. Lower with control, then repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Build up through wide pull-ups first. Do 3 sets of 3 to 6 reps per side with at least 2 minutes' rest. Don't swing or shrug. Take it easy at the bottom of each rep to protect the elbow. Stop if you feel pain in the elbow or shoulder.

### In daily life
Pulling yourself up with one hand to reach a high point, such as when climbing, uses unequal arm loading.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and rock climbers often pull harder with one arm. The archer pull up builds one-sided strength as a progression toward a one-arm pull-up.
