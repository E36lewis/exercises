A dumbbell exercise where you lift your arms out to the sides while bent forward. It works the rear of the shoulders (rear deltoids) and the upper back, mainly the rhomboids and middle trapezius.

### How to do it
1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand and hinge forward at the hips with a flat back.
2. Let your arms hang below your chest, palms facing each other.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Lift the weights out to the sides with a slight bend in your elbows.
5. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top, with your arms near shoulder height.
6. Lower slowly.

**Tips:** Use light weights, as the rear shoulder muscles are small. Don't swing or shrug. Keep your neck in line with your spine.

### In daily life
Opening your arms wide and drawing the shoulder blades back, as when you open a large curtain or pull a heavy door, uses these muscles.

### Why it matters
These muscles balance the chest and front of the shoulders, so training them can support upright posture and shoulder health.
