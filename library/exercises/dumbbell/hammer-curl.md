The hammer curl is a dumbbell curl with your palms facing each other, as if you were holding a hammer. That grip shifts more of the work to the brachialis and brachioradialis, the muscles on the front and thumb side of your elbow and forearm.

### How to do it
1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, palms facing your thighs.
2. Keep your elbows close to your ribs and your shoulders back.
3. Bend your elbows and lift the weights toward your shoulders, keeping your palms facing in.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight again.
6. Repeat, or alternate arms.

**Tips:** Don't swing your body to get the weights up. Keep your wrists straight. Choose a weight you can control all the way down.

### In daily life
Carrying shopping bags or a bucket by the handle puts your hand in this same neutral position, with the load pulling your elbow straight.

### Why it matters
A strong brachialis and brachioradialis can help you carry loads and grip with confidence. It also adds thickness to the upper arm and supports your forearm.
