The incline flye opens your arms out to the sides like a hug in reverse, while you lie on an angled bench. It stretches and works the pectoral muscles, especially the upper chest, with only a small bend at the elbow.

### How to do it
1. Set a bench to a slight incline and lie back with a dumbbell in each hand.
2. Hold the dumbbells above your chest with your palms facing each other.
3. Soften your elbows into a slight, fixed bend.
4. Lower the weights out and down in a wide arc until you feel a stretch across your chest.
5. Bring them back along the same arc, squeezing your chest as they meet.
6. Repeat.

**Tips:** Use light weights, because the arms are long levers. Keep your elbows soft but don't turn it into a press. Stop when you feel a comfortable stretch and don't go deeper.

### In daily life
Pulling your arms together to hug someone or to gather a wide load is the same action as the lift at the top of the flye.

### Why it matters
The flye isolates the chest more than a press. It can help build chest strength and the flexibility to open your arms and shoulders.
