The dumbbell squat is a squat with a weight in each hand, hanging at your sides. It is an easy way to load the movement when you have no rack. Your thighs and glutes do most of the work, and your grip and upper back hold the dumbbells.

### How to do it
1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with your arms hanging at your sides and your palms facing in.
2. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart and your toes slightly out.
3. Brace your core and push your hips back as you bend your knees.
4. Lower until your thighs are about parallel to the floor, with the dumbbells staying near your outer thighs.
5. Press down through your feet to stand tall again.
6. Reset your stance and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders back so the weights do not pull you forward. Start with a weight you can hold securely, because your grip may give out before your legs do. Look straight ahead and keep your chest lifted.

### In daily life
Squatting with weights at your sides is like crouching to lift two full grocery bags and standing up with them.

### Why it matters
It is a simple way to build leg strength, and holding the weights challenges your grip and posture too, which can help carry that strength over to daily tasks.
