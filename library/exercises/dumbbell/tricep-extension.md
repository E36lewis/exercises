The dumbbell triceps extension isolates the triceps, the muscle on the back of the upper arm that straightens the elbow. This version is done overhead with both hands holding one dumbbell, which also stretches the long head of the triceps.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall and hold one end of a dumbbell with both hands, cupping the top plate.
2. Raise it overhead until your arms are straight, with your biceps near your ears.
3. Keep your elbows pointing forward and bend them to lower the dumbbell behind your head.
4. Stop when you feel a stretch along the back of your arms.
5. Straighten your elbows to bring the weight back up.

**Tips:** Only your forearms should move; keep your upper arms still. Brace your abs so your ribs do not flare and your lower back does not arch. Choose a weight you can control, because it travels close to your head.

### In daily life
It builds the strength for straightening your arms above your head, as when you put a bag in an overhead locker or hang a coat on a high hook.

### Why it matters
Triceps make up most of the upper arm and do much of the work in any pressing exercise. Training them directly can help your bench press, push-up and overhead press, and keep your elbows working well.
