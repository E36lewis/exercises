The dumbbell wrist curl with your palm facing down, also called a wrist extension, works the extensor muscles on the top of the forearm. These muscles lift the back of your hand up, and they work as a balance to the stronger flexors on the underside.

### How to do it
1. Sit on a bench and rest your forearms on your thighs, palms down, with your wrists just past your knees.
2. Hold a light dumbbell in each hand with an overhand grip.
3. Let your wrists bend down so the weights hang slightly.
4. Raise your knuckles up toward the ceiling by extending your wrists.
5. Pause briefly at the top.
6. Lower the dumbbells slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Use a very light weight, because the extensors are small and easy to strain. Keep your forearms on your legs and move only your wrists. Take your time, particularly on the lowering phase.

### In daily life
It works the muscles that hold your wrist steady when you use a screwdriver, a mouse or a racket, and when you carry a bag.

### Why it matters
Balanced forearm strength between the flexors and extensors can help keep the wrist and the outer elbow comfortable in gripping and typing tasks.
