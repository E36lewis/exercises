The dumbbell good morning is a home version of the good morning. You hold a dumbbell against your chest and hinge forward from the hips, then stand back up. It trains the hamstrings and the muscles along your spine.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart, holding one dumbbell upright against your chest with both hands.
2. Soften your knees and brace your core.
3. Push your hips straight back and lean your chest forward with a flat back.
4. Lower until your torso is near parallel to the floor, or as far as you can keep your back flat.
5. Drive your hips forward to stand tall.

**Tips:** Keep the movement a hinge, not a squat. Keep your back flat from your head to your tailbone and your knees soft. Start light.

### In daily life
It is the hinge for picking something up from the floor or leaning over a sink without rounding your back.

### Why it matters
Runners and wheelchair athletes need strong hamstrings and a strong lower back. This version only needs a single dumbbell.
