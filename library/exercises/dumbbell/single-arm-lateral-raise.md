This is a one-arm dumbbell raise out to the side. It mainly works the side of your shoulder (deltoid), and the rotator cuff helps hold the joint steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in one hand at your side.
2. Brace your core and keep a slight bend in your elbow.
3. Raise your arm out to the side until it reaches about shoulder height.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly, taking about as long as the lift.
6. Finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Don't swing or shrug your shoulder. Lead with your elbow, not your hand. Use a light weight, since the shoulder is a small muscle.

### In daily life
You lift your arm out to the side to put on a jacket or reach for a door handle beside you.

### Why it matters
It builds the rounded width of the shoulders, and the single-arm version lets you hold on to a wall with your other hand for balance.
