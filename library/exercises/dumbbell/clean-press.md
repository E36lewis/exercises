The dumbbell clean press lifts dumbbells from your thighs to your shoulders (the clean) and then presses them overhead. The legs and hips start the lift, and the shoulders and upper back finish it.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart, a dumbbell in each hand, hanging in front of your thighs.
2. Push your hips back and bend your knees slightly, keeping your back flat.
3. Stand up fast and shrug, pulling your elbows up and out so the weights rise close to your body.
4. Turn your wrists and catch the dumbbells at your shoulders, palms facing each other or forward.
5. Bend your knees a little, then straighten them as you press the weights overhead until your arms are straight.
6. Lower the weights to your shoulders, then to your thighs, and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and don't lean back as you press. Let the weights land softly on your shoulders. Use a weight you can press without arching your back.

### In daily life
It matches lifting a suitcase from low down and putting it on a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It can help build shoulder strength and teach your legs and arms to work as one chain, which supports lifting and throwing.
