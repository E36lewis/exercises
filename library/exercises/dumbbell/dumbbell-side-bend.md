The dumbbell side bend is a standing exercise in which you hold a dumbbell in one hand and bend your trunk sideways. It trains the obliques and the muscles at the side of your spine.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart, holding a dumbbell in one hand at your side.
2. Place your other hand on your hip or behind your head.
3. Bend sideways at your waist, lowering the dumbbell toward your knee.
4. Use the muscles on the opposite side of your trunk to pull back to upright.
5. Finish all repetitions, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Keep your chest facing forward and do not lean forwards or backwards. Start light and move slowly. Do not use a heavy weight in both hands, which cancels the work.

### In daily life
It strengthens the sides of your trunk for carrying a bag on one side or reaching sideways.

### Why it matters
Combat and collision sports need a strong trunk to resist and produce side bending during tackles and grappling.
