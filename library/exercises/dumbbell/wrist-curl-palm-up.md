The dumbbell wrist curl with your palm facing up works the flexor muscles on the underside of the forearm. These muscles bend the wrist and the fingers, so they play a large part in your grip.

### How to do it
1. Sit on a bench and rest your forearms on your thighs, palms up, with your wrists just past your knees.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with an underhand grip.
3. Let the weights roll down toward your fingertips, opening your hands.
4. Close your fingers and curl your wrists up toward the ceiling.
5. Pause briefly at the top.
6. Lower slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Pick a light weight you can lift for the full range. Keep your forearms still on your thighs. Do not let the weight drop quickly at the bottom, and avoid pushing through any wrist pain.

### In daily life
It strengthens the muscles you use to carry shopping bags, hold a suitcase handle or wring out a cloth.

### Why it matters
Strong forearm flexors give you a firmer grip, which can help in pulling exercises such as rows and deadlifts, and in sports like climbing.
