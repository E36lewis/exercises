The dumbbell alternating arm press is an overhead press done one arm at a time, so each shoulder works on its own while the other rests. It works the shoulders and triceps, with your core keeping you upright.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward or toward each other.
2. Brace your abdominals and keep your knees slightly soft.
3. Press one dumbbell straight overhead until your arm is almost straight.
4. Lower it slowly back to your shoulder.
5. Press the other dumbbell up, then lower it.
6. Continue alternating sides.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and do not lean back to push the weight up. Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows. Use a weight you can control, and do not lock your elbows hard at the top.

### In daily life
Pushing up with one hand at a time is how you slide a suitcase onto a wardrobe, change a ceiling light bulb or put a cup on the top shelf.

### Why it matters
Because each arm lifts independently, a weaker side cannot hide behind the stronger one. That can help even out strength between your left and right shoulders.
