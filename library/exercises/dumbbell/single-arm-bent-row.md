This is a one-arm dumbbell row with your other hand on a support. It mainly works the lats, with the upper back and arms helping.

### How to do it
1. Put one hand and the same-side knee on a bench, or rest one hand on a sturdy support with a flat back.
2. Hold a dumbbell in your other hand, arm hanging straight down.
3. Pull the dumbbell towards your hip, leading with your elbow.
4. Pause for a moment, drawing your shoulder blade back.
5. Lower the weight slowly until your arm is straight.
6. Finish your reps, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and your shoulders square to the floor. Pull with your back instead of jerking the weight. Choose a weight you can control all the way down.

### In daily life
You use this pull to haul a heavy bag onto a shelf or drag a suitcase off a conveyor belt.

### Why it matters
It builds back strength that supports an upright posture. The supported hand lets you work hard without loading your lower back.
