The dumbbell Bulgarian split squat is a single-leg squat with your rear foot raised on a bench. The front thigh does most of the work, with the glutes and the inner thigh helping, and you have to balance as well.

### How to do it
1. Stand about two steps in front of a bench, holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
2. Place the top of one foot on the bench behind you.
3. Lower your hips straight down until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor.
4. Keep your torso tall or leaning slightly forward from the hips.
5. Press through your front foot to stand.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your front knee tracking over your toes. Do not let your front heel lift. Start with body weight to find your balance and use a stable bench.

### In daily life
It resembles stepping up onto a curb, climbing stairs while carrying bags, and standing from a low seat on one leg.

### Why it matters
It can help build leg strength and balance, and it shows differences between your two sides.
