A plank with a dumbbell in each hand where you row one dumbbell at a time. It works the back (lats, trapezius and rhomboids), the shoulders and the biceps, while your core resists twisting and your support arm holds you up.

### How to do it
1. Start in a plank position on your hands, gripping dumbbells, with your feet wide apart.
2. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
3. Row one dumbbell toward your ribs while pressing the other into the floor.
4. Keep your hips level and square to the floor.
5. Lower the dumbbell under control.
6. Row the other side.

**Tips:** Use hexagonal dumbbells that won't roll, and start with light weights. A wider foot stance is more stable. Stop if your hips twist or your lower back sags.

### In daily life
Holding your body steady while one arm moves, as when you lift a bag from the floor while supporting yourself with the other hand, uses the same skill.

### Why it matters
It trains pulling and anti-rotation (resisting twist) together, which can build back strength and trunk stability at once.
