The Arnold press is a dumbbell shoulder press that adds a half turn of the hands as you press. It works all three heads of the deltoid, with the triceps and upper chest helping.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your chest, palms facing you and elbows bent.
2. Brace your abdominals.
3. Press upward while rotating your palms to face forward.
4. Finish with your arms nearly straight above your shoulders.
5. Lower the weights while rotating your palms back to face you.
6. Return to the start position.

**Tips:** Move slowly through the rotation. Keep your ribs down and avoid arching your back. Use a lighter weight than you would for a standard press, and keep the movement within a comfortable range.

### In daily life
The rotating motion resembles lifting a dish from your chest and turning it to slide onto a high cupboard shelf.

### Why it matters
Turning the hands as you press keeps the front and side of the shoulder working through a longer path than a standard press, which can help build shoulder strength and control.
