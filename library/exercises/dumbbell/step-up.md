The dumbbell step-up is a single-leg lower-body exercise. You step onto a box while holding weights. The working leg's glutes and thighs lift you, and the calves and inner thighs help.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a sturdy box or bench, with a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
2. Place one foot fully on the box.
3. Press through that heel and stand tall on the box.
4. Bring your other foot up beside it, or lift your knee.
5. Step the trailing foot down first, then the working foot, under control.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Choose a box height where your knee bends to about 90 degrees. Push with the top leg, not by springing off the bottom foot. Make sure the box cannot slide. Keep your torso upright.

### In daily life
It is the movement of climbing a flight of stairs, a curb or a step into a bus with groceries in your hands.

### Why it matters
Working one leg at a time shows up any strength difference between sides and builds the balance you need for walking and climbing.
