A dumbbell exercise lying on a bench, arcing the weight from over your chest to behind your head and back. It works the latissimus dorsi (the large muscles of your back), with the triceps and chest muscles helping.

### How to do it
1. Lie on a bench with your upper back supported and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold one dumbbell with both hands above your chest, your palms under the top plate.
3. Keep a slight bend in your elbows.
4. Lower the dumbbell in an arc behind your head until you feel a stretch under your arms.
5. Keep your ribs down so your lower back doesn't arch.
6. Pull the weight back over your chest in the same arc.

**Tips:** Hold the dumbbell firmly, and choose a light one so you stay in control behind your head. Keep the bend in your elbows the same throughout. Stop if you feel pinching in your shoulder.

### In daily life
The reach back and the pull forward again resemble taking a heavy item down from a high shelf or pulling a rope toward you.

### Why it matters
Both the arms and the trunk move around the shoulder here. It can build the back and chest muscles that control the shoulder through a long range.
