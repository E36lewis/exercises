The dumbbell deadlift lifts a pair of dumbbells from the floor to standing. It works the hamstrings and glutes, with the calves, inner thighs and grip helping, and the abdominal and lower back muscles keeping your trunk steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and a dumbbell beside each foot, or in front of your shins.
2. Push your hips back and bend your knees until you can grip the weights, keeping your back flat.
3. Lift your chest and brace your abdomen.
4. Push through your feet and stand up, keeping the dumbbells close to your legs.
5. Finish tall, squeezing your glutes, without leaning back.
6. Lower the weights by pushing your hips back and bending your knees, and set them down.

**Tips:** Keep your arms straight and your back flat. Let the weights slide along your legs. Choose a weight you can lift without rounding your back.

### In daily life
It matches picking up shopping bags, a suitcase or a toddler's toys from the floor.

### Why it matters
It can help build hip and leg strength. Dumbbells at your sides are often easier to learn than a long bar, so this can be a good first step toward deadlifting.
