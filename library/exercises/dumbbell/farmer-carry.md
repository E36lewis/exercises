The dumbbell farmer carry is a walk while holding a dumbbell in each hand. Your forearms and grip hold the weights, while your shoulders, upper back and trunk keep you upright and level.

### How to do it
1. Stand between two dumbbells and hinge to pick them up, with a flat back.
2. Stand tall with the weights at your sides, shoulders down and back.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Walk with short, steady steps.
5. Keep the weights from swinging.
6. Set them down by hinging at your hips.

**Tips:** Squeeze the handles firmly. Don't lean to one side. Use a clear path.

### In daily life
It matches carrying two bags of groceries in from the car in one trip.

### Why it matters
It can help build grip strength and a tall posture under load, which supports everyday carrying. Dumbbells are easy to scale: pick a pair that suits you.
