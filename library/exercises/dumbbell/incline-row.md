The incline row is a dumbbell row done lying chest down on an angled bench. The bench holds your trunk up, so your back muscles and shoulder blades do the pulling without your lower back having to work to hold you.

### How to do it
1. Set a bench to a slight incline and lie face down with your chest against the pad.
2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight down.
3. Pull your elbows up and back, leading with your elbows.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top.
5. Pause briefly.
6. Lower the weights slowly until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Keep your chest on the bench the entire time. Don't shrug your shoulders toward your ears. Control the lowering.

### In daily life
Pulling a mower cord, a car door or a heavy drawer toward you uses the same back muscles.

### Why it matters
Because the bench supports you, it is easy to feel the upper back working. It can help posture by strengthening the muscles that draw the shoulder blades together.
