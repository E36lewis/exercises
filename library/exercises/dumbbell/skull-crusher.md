This is a lying triceps exercise with dumbbells. It works the triceps at the back of your upper arm, and the small shoulder muscles keep your arms steady.

### How to do it
1. Lie on a bench holding dumbbells above your chest with your arms straight.
2. Keep your upper arms still and pointing up.
3. Bend your elbows, lowering the weights towards your head.
4. Stop when your forearms are roughly parallel to the floor.
5. Straighten your arms to lift the weights back up.
6. Repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows pointing up and not flared. Use a light weight and have a spotter for heavier loads. Lower the weight to the sides of your head, not onto your face.

### In daily life
You straighten your arms against resistance like this to push yourself up from a low chair or to fully extend your arms overhead.

### Why it matters
It builds the triceps, which supports elbow strength and pressing.
