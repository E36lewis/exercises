This is a one-arm dumbbell raise that lifts the weight to shoulder height. It works the deltoid, and the chest and shoulder-blade muscles help.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in one hand.
2. Brace your core and keep a slight bend in your elbow.
3. Raise your arm in front of you or out to your side to shoulder height.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly, resisting the drop.
6. Finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Don't swing the weight or lean back. Keep your shoulder relaxed away from your ear. Use a light weight you can lift smoothly.

### In daily life
You lift your arm to shoulder height to reach for a cup in a cupboard or pass an item to someone across a table.

### Why it matters
It builds strength in the shoulder, and training one arm at a time can help you control your arm's path through the lift.
