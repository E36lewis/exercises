A dumbbell exercise that lifts your arms out to the sides to train the deltoids (the shoulder muscles), especially the middle part that gives the shoulder its width. The upper back helps hold your shoulder blades steady as you lift.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms by your sides, palms facing in.
2. Soften your knees and brace your abdomen.
3. With a slight bend in your elbows, lift both arms out to the sides.
4. Stop when your hands are about shoulder height.
5. Pause briefly.
6. Lower the weights slowly to your sides.

**Tips:** Lead with the elbows, not the hands, and keep your shoulders down away from your ears. Don't swing your body to get the weights up. Choose a light weight, because this small muscle tires quickly.

### In daily life
Holding your arms out and away from your body is what you do when you carry a full bag or tray out to one side, or steady yourself on a bus.

### Why it matters
Training the side of the shoulder can help balance the work done by the front of the shoulder in pushing exercises, and supports even shoulder shape and posture.
