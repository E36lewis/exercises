This is a one-arm overhead press with a dumbbell. It works your shoulders and the muscles around your shoulder blade, with the triceps finishing the press.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with a dumbbell at shoulder height and your palm facing forward or inward.
2. Brace your core and keep your ribs down.
3. Press the dumbbell straight overhead until your arm is straight.
4. Pause for a moment.
5. Lower it back to your shoulder with control.
6. Finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Don't lean to the side or arch your lower back. Keep your wrist straight over your elbow. Choose a weight you can control overhead.

### In daily life
You press one-handed to slide a bag into the overhead rack on a train or place a plate on a high cupboard shelf.

### Why it matters
It builds shoulder strength, and holding the weight on one side makes your core work to keep you upright without leaning.
