The Cuban press is a shoulder exercise that combines an upright row, an external rotation and a press in one smooth movement. It trains the rotator cuff and the deltoids together with light weights.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a light dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs, palms facing you.
2. Pull the dumbbells up to chest height with your elbows high, like an upright row.
3. Rotate your forearms upward so the dumbbells point to the ceiling, with your elbows at shoulder height.
4. Press the dumbbells straight overhead.
5. Reverse the steps slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Use very light weights and do not rush. Keep your elbows high in the rotation and your trunk upright. Do not shrug and avoid arching your lower back.

### In daily life
It trains reaching overhead and putting something on a high shelf with a stable shoulder.

### Why it matters
Baseball, softball and swimming need shoulder strength and a strong rotator cuff. The Cuban press trains both in a single movement.
