This is a one-leg hip hinge holding dumbbells. It works the glutes and hamstrings, and your hips and core keep you balanced.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg, holding dumbbells in front of your thighs.
2. Keep a slight bend in your standing knee.
3. Hinge at your hip and extend your other leg straight behind you.
4. Lower the weights along your leg until your torso is close to parallel to the floor.
5. Squeeze your glutes to bring your torso back to standing.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your hips square to the floor and your back flat. Stay close to a wall in case you wobble. Start with light weights.

### In daily life
You balance like this when you bend to pick up a toy on one foot or stand on a bus lurching around a corner.

### Why it matters
It builds strength and balance in one leg at a time, which can help you notice if one side is weaker and support your hips on uneven ground.
