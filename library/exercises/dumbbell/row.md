This is a bent-over pulling exercise with a dumbbell in each hand. You hinge at the hips and pull the weights towards your ribs. It mainly works the upper back (lats, rhomboids and rear shoulders), with the biceps and other arm muscles assisting.

### How to do it
1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand and hinge forward at the hips, with a flat back and soft knees.
2. Let your arms hang straight down under your shoulders.
3. Pull your shoulder blades back and together.
4. Bend your elbows and pull the dumbbells towards your lower ribs.
5. Pause briefly at the top.
6. Lower the weights slowly until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and your neck in line with your spine. Don't jerk the weights up with your body. Start light so your lower back stays comfortable.

### In daily life
Hauling a grocery bag out of the boot, yanking open a stuck door or starting a lawn mower all use this pull. So does lifting a child up from the floor.

### Why it matters
A strong upper back balances all the pushing we do in training and daily life. It supports good posture and can help protect the shoulders and spine.
