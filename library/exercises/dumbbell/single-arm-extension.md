This is a one-arm overhead triceps extension with a dumbbell. It works the triceps at the back of your upper arm, and the shoulder muscles keep your arm overhead.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall, holding a dumbbell in one hand.
2. Raise your arm overhead so your elbow points at the ceiling.
3. Keep your upper arm still and bend your elbow, lowering the weight behind your head.
4. Stop when your forearm is about parallel to the floor or when you feel a stretch.
5. Straighten your arm to lift the weight back up.
6. Finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your elbow close to your head and your ribs down so your back doesn't arch. Use a light weight and steady your elbow with your other hand if needed.

### In daily life
You straighten your arm like this when you reach up to put a box on a high shelf or push off a chair arm.

### Why it matters
Stronger triceps can help you press, push up and lock out your elbow, and working one side at a time can show if one arm lags.
