Scaption is a shoulder raise in which you lift light dumbbells diagonally, halfway between forward and sideways, in the plane of your shoulder blades. It trains the deltoid and the supraspinatus in the position where the shoulder joint works best.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a light dumbbell in each hand, arms by your sides and thumbs pointing forward.
2. Brace your core lightly.
3. Raise your arms in a V shape about 30 degrees in front of your body, up to shoulder height.
4. Pause briefly at the top.
5. Lower slowly back to your sides.

**Tips:** Use a light weight and keep your shoulders down. Do not shrug or lean back. Raise your thumbs up for a fuller turn of the shoulder, or keep them forward if a pinch occurs.

### In daily life
It is the motion of reaching out in front of and slightly to the side, such as for a jar on a shelf.

### Why it matters
Batting, racket and swimming sports use the shoulder in this plane. It builds the strength that keeps the shoulder joint steady.
