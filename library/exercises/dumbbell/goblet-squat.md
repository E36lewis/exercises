The dumbbell goblet squat holds one dumbbell vertically at your chest. It works the thighs and glutes, and the abdomen steadies your trunk against the weight in front of you.

### How to do it
1. Stand a dumbbell on one end and cup the top end with both hands, holding it against your breastbone.
2. Set your feet a little wider than your shoulders, toes turned slightly out.
3. Brace your abdomen and keep your elbows pointing down.
4. Push your hips back and bend your knees to sit between your legs.
5. Lower to a depth where your back stays flat and your heels stay on the floor.
6. Keep your chest up the whole way.
7. Drive up through your feet to stand.

**Tips:** Aim your knees in line with your toes. Keep your heels down. Don't let your lower back round at the bottom.

### In daily life
Lifting a box off the ground, or crouching to pick up a child, shares this squat: a load held close and an upright body.

### Why it matters
The dumbbell is easy to find and to increase in small steps, so it suits learning the squat and building leg strength. Holding it in front can help you practise staying upright.
