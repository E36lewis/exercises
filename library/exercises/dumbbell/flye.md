The dumbbell flye is a chest exercise in which you open and close your arms like wings while lying on a flat bench. It works the chest (pectoralis major), with the front of the shoulders and the arms steadying the weights.

### How to do it
1. Lie on a bench with a dumbbell in each hand, arms straight above your chest.
2. Turn your palms to face each other.
3. Bend your elbows slightly and keep that bend.
4. Lower the weights out to the sides in an arc until you feel a stretch across your chest.
5. Keep your shoulder blades tucked against the bench.
6. Bring the weights back together over your chest, as if hugging a barrel.

**Tips:** Use light weights. Don't go lower than is comfortable for your shoulders. Keep the same elbow bend throughout.

### In daily life
It matches the hugging movement of bringing both arms together in front of you.

### Why it matters
It can help build chest strength and shoulder flexibility, and it trains the chest by itself, without the triceps doing the lifting as in a press.
