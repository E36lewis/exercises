The dumbbell bicep curl is a basic arm exercise that targets the biceps, with help from the brachialis and brachioradialis. Because each arm holds its own dumbbell, each side has to work on its own.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, arms at your sides and palms facing forward.
2. Keep your elbows close to your ribs.
3. Bend one elbow and curl the dumbbell toward your shoulder.
4. Pause at the top and squeeze.
5. Lower it slowly until your arm is straight.
6. Repeat with the other arm, or curl both together.

**Tips:** Do not swing your body or let your elbows drift forward. Keep your wrists straight. Pick a weight you can control for the whole movement.

### In daily life
It trains the one-handed lift you use to carry a shopping bag or a suitcase and bring it up to your side.

### Why it matters
It can help build arm and grip strength, and working each arm alone can show you if one side is weaker.
