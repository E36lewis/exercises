The front arm raise lifts a dumbbell straight out in front of you to shoulder height. It works the front of the shoulder (the anterior deltoid), with the shoulder-blade muscles and trunk steadying you.

### How to do it
1. Stand with a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs.
2. Brace your abdomen.
3. Keep your arms nearly straight.
4. Lift the weights to shoulder height.
5. Pause briefly.
6. Lower slowly.

**Tips:** Don't swing. Keep your shoulders down. Use a light weight.

### In daily life
It matches reaching forward to lift a light object off a shelf.

### Why it matters
It can help build shoulder strength, and it is an easy way to train the front of the shoulder on its own.
