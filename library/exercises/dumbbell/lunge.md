A forward lunge holding a dumbbell in each hand. The extra weight makes the thighs, glutes and hamstrings work harder, and your hips and trunk work to keep you upright.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides.
2. Brace your core and keep your shoulders back.
3. Take a long step forward with one foot.
4. Lower your back knee toward the floor until your front thigh is roughly level with the floor.
5. Keep your front knee over your ankle.
6. Push through your front foot to return to standing, and repeat on the other leg.

**Tips:** Keep your torso upright and the dumbbells steady at your sides. Don't let the front knee cave in. Start with light dumbbells and check the floor isn't slippery.

### In daily life
The loaded step is like walking up a hill with grocery bags in both hands, or lunging forward to pick up a heavy item from the ground.

### Why it matters
Adding load to a single-leg pattern can build leg strength and hip stability while still challenging balance.
