The dumbbell incline bench press presses two weights upward while you lie back on an angled bench. The slope shifts more work to the upper part of the chest and the front of the shoulders, and the triceps help straighten your arms.

### How to do it
1. Set a bench to a slight to moderate angle, often about 30 degrees.
2. Sit with a dumbbell on each thigh, then lie back and bring the weights to your shoulders.
3. Plant your feet on the floor and keep your shoulder blades pinched back.
4. Press the dumbbells up until your arms are straight above your chest.
5. Pause at the top.
6. Lower them slowly to the sides of your chest.
7. Repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your feet planted. Keep your elbows at about 45 degrees to your body and don't flare them. Use a spotter for heavy sets.

### In daily life
Pushing a heavy door open or pushing yourself up from a reclined seat shares the upward and forward push.

### Why it matters
Dumbbells let each arm work on its own, which can show and balance strength differences. The incline adds upper-chest and shoulder work to a pressing routine.
