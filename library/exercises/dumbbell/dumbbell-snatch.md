The dumbbell snatch is a one-arm power lift. You swing a dumbbell from the floor to overhead in one fast, smooth movement. The legs and hips create the power, and the shoulder and trunk steady the weight overhead.

### How to do it
1. Place a dumbbell on the floor between your feet, then hinge and grip it with one hand, with your back flat.
2. Drive through your legs and hips, standing up fast.
3. As the dumbbell rises, shrug and pull your elbow high, then punch your hand through the bottom of the dumbbell to catch it overhead.
4. Stand tall with your arm locked out and the dumbbell over your shoulder.
5. Lower the dumbbell to your shoulder, then to the floor with a hinge. Repeat all repetitions, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Learn it with a light dumbbell. Keep the weight close to your body and your back flat. Clear space around you and do not catch the weight in front of you. If you fail, let the dumbbell drop to the side, away from your body.

### In daily life
It is the motion of lifting something from the floor and putting it up on a high shelf in one smooth action.

### Why it matters
Combat, collision and field sports need powerful, coordinated movement from the ground up. The snatch trains that in one arm at a time.
