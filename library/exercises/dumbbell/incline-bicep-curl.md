The incline curl is a dumbbell curl done sitting back on an angled bench, with your arms hanging behind your body. That position stretches the long head of the biceps before each curl and lets you do it with less swing.

### How to do it
1. Set a bench to a steep incline, about 45 to 60 degrees, and sit back with your head and back supported.
2. Let your arms hang straight down by your sides, holding a dumbbell in each hand, palms forward.
3. Keep your upper arms still and curl the weights toward your shoulders.
4. Squeeze your biceps at the top.
5. Lower slowly until your arms are straight again, feeling the stretch.
6. Repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders back against the bench. Use lighter weights than a standing curl. Don't swing your body.

### In daily life
Lifting a heavy bag or a child from low down, where the elbow bends from a stretched position, uses this arm path.

### Why it matters
The long stretch makes the biceps work through a long range. That can help build biceps strength and good elbow control.
