The dumbbell serratus punch is a lying exercise in which you punch a dumbbell straight up toward the ceiling by pushing your shoulder blade forward. It trains the serratus anterior, the muscle that holds your shoulder blade against your ribs and moves it as you punch.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back on a bench or the floor and hold a light dumbbell straight above your shoulder, palm facing in.
2. Keep your arm straight and your shoulder blade resting on the bench.
3. Push the dumbbell up toward the ceiling by pushing your shoulder blade forward, without bending your elbow.
4. Pause at the top.
5. Lower slowly back to the start. Repeat for all repetitions, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Use a very light weight. Keep your elbow straight and move only your shoulder blade. Do not let your shoulder shrug up toward your ear.

### In daily life
It helps you push a heavy door or reach forward without your shoulder blade lifting off your back.

### Why it matters
Boxers and fighters punch with the serratus. Collision and swimming sports also rely on a stable shoulder blade as the arm reaches forward.
