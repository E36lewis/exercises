This is an overhead press with a dumbbell in each hand. You push both weights from shoulder height until your arms are straight above you. It mainly works the shoulders (deltoids), with the triceps and upper back helping.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall with a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing forward.
2. Brace your core and keep your ribs down.
3. Press both dumbbells overhead until your arms are straight.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Lower slowly back to shoulder height.

**Tips:** Don't arch your lower back. Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows. Choose a weight you can press without leaning back.

### In daily life
Putting luggage on a rack, lifting a child up to see a parade and reaching a cereal box on the top shelf all use this overhead press.

### Why it matters
Because each arm lifts its own weight, the stronger side can't cover for the weaker one. The rotator cuff and shoulder-blade muscles work to keep the joint steady.
