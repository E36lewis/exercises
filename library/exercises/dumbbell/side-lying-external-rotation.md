Side lying external rotation is a basic rotator cuff exercise. You lie on your side with a light dumbbell and rotate your forearm upward while your elbow stays against your body. It strengthens the small muscles at the back of the shoulder.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with your head supported and hold a very light dumbbell in your top hand.
2. Tuck your elbow against your side with a small towel between your elbow and ribs, and bend it to 90 degrees with your forearm across your stomach.
3. Keeping your elbow against your body, slowly rotate your forearm upward until it points toward the ceiling.
4. Pause, then lower the weight slowly.
5. Repeat for all repetitions, then roll over and do the other arm.

**Tips:** Use a very light weight, often 1 to 3 kg. Keep the elbow pinned to your side and the movement slow. Do not roll your trunk back to lift the dumbbell.

### In daily life
It supports reaching behind you or into a back seat without a sharp shoulder pinch.

### Why it matters
Throwing, racket, swimming and collision sports stress the shoulder with fast, repeated movements. Rotator cuff strength helps keep the joint centered, and this exercise is a common starting point in shoulder rehabilitation.
