This is a seated rotating core exercise with a dumbbell. You lean back slightly and turn your torso side to side, moving the weight with you. It mainly works the obliques, the muscles at the sides of your waist, while the hip flexors and deep trunk muscles help.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent and your heels on the ground.
2. Hold a dumbbell with both hands in front of your chest.
3. Lean back until you feel your abs working, keeping your back straight.
4. Rotate your torso to one side, moving the dumbbell with you.
5. Return through the centre and rotate to the other side.
6. Keep a steady, controlled pace.

**Tips:** Turn from your ribs, not just your arms. Don't round your back. If your lower back hurts, keep your feet down and use a lighter weight.

### In daily life
Twisting to reach a bag on the back seat, swinging a golf club or racquet and turning with a heavy bag all call for this kind of controlled rotation.

### Why it matters
A strong core can help you control rotation and transfer force from your hips to your arms. It supports spine stability and everyday movement.
