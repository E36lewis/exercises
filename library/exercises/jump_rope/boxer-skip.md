The boxer skip is a relaxed rope-jumping step that shifts your weight from one foot to the other, as if skipping. The rope turns at a steady pace, and you stay light on your feet. It builds the rhythm and calf endurance boxers use for rounds.

### How to do it
1. Pick a rope that reaches your armpits when you stand on the middle.
2. Hold the handles at hip height with elbows close.
3. Turn the rope with your wrists.
4. Hop lightly from one foot to the other with each turn, as if jogging on the spot.
5. Keep the jumps small, and your weight on the balls of your feet.
6. Keep your shoulders relaxed and breathe steadily.

**Tips:** Boxers skip in rounds of 3 minutes with 1 minute's rest, for 3 to 6 rounds. Beginners can start with 1 minute rounds. Use a flat, firm surface, and stop if you feel pain in the shins or Achilles tendon.

### In daily life
Jogging on the spot to warm up, skipping with a child and light running steps use the same bounce.

### Why it matters
Boxing and other combat sports use this for footwork, rhythm and calf and heart conditioning across a three-minute round.
