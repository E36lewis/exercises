The criss cross is a rope jump in which you cross your arms in front of you on one pass of the rope and uncross them on the next. It adds coordination to basic rope jumping.

### How to do it
1. Start jumping rope in a basic bounce, with a steady rhythm.
2. On one pass, cross your forearms in front of your body so the rope forms a loop around you.
3. Jump through the loop with both feet.
4. Open your arms again on the next pass.
5. Alternate crossed and open arms, keeping your jumps low.
6. Keep your wrists doing the work and your elbows close.

**Tips:** Master the basic bounce first. Do 5 to 8 rounds of 30 to 60 seconds, with 30 seconds' rest. If you trip, slow down and cross just a little. Use a lighter rope so your wrists can turn it quickly.

### In daily life
Coordinating two sides of the body at once, such as playing a drum or juggling, uses similar timing.

### Why it matters
Gymnastics and rock-climbing athletes use rope work for rhythm, coordination and light feet. The criss cross adds variety and trains the arms and legs to work together.
