Single leg jump rope is the basic rope jump done on one foot at a time. It makes the calf and ankle stiffer and stronger, and trains balance.

### How to do it
1. Pick a rope that reaches your armpits when you stand on the middle.
2. Stand on one foot with the other lifted slightly off the floor.
3. Turn the rope with your wrists and jump on the standing foot.
4. Land softly on the ball of the foot and keep your jumps small.
5. After 10 to 20 jumps, switch legs.

**Tips:** Do 3 to 5 sets of 20 to 30 jumps per leg. Build up slowly, because the load on the calf and Achilles tendon doubles. Stop if you feel pain in the lower leg. Make sure you have a clear space and a flat, firm surface.

### In daily life
Hopping on one foot to put on a shoe, or to cross a stream, uses the same single-leg bounce.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and rock climbers need stiff, springy ankles and good single-leg balance. This drill builds that and helps prepare the lower leg for landings and foot work.
