The double under is a jump in which the rope passes under your feet twice for each jump. It needs a faster wrist turn and a slightly higher jump. It is an advanced rope skill.

### How to do it
1. Use a lightweight speed rope that reaches your armpits when you stand on its middle.
2. Stand tall with your elbows at your sides and hands slightly out from your hips.
3. Jump a little higher than normal, about 5 cm, and turn the rope fast with your wrists.
4. Let the rope pass twice under your feet.
5. Land softly on the balls of your feet and go straight into the next jump.
6. Keep your body straight and avoid kicking your feet back.

**Tips:** Practise by alternating a double under with a few single jumps. Do 5 to 10 rounds of 20 to 40 seconds, or a set of 20 to 50 reps. Build up slowly because the landings are harder on the calves and Achilles tendons. Use a good surface and shoes.

### In daily life
A quick double hop to clear an obstacle uses a similar spring.

### Why it matters
Combat, gymnastics and rock-climbing athletes use double unders to push the limit of coordination and calf conditioning. They raise the heart rate quickly.
