The stability ball V-up is a core exercise in which you hold a ball overhead, lift your arms and legs together, and pass the ball from your hands to your feet. It works the rectus abdominis and the deep core muscles, and the ball adds a coordination challenge.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your legs straight and a stability ball held in both hands overhead.
2. Brace your abs and press your lower back toward the floor.
3. Lift your arms and legs together, bringing your hands toward your feet.
4. Squeeze the ball between your ankles or feet and release it from your hands.
5. Lower your arms and legs, holding the ball with your feet.
6. Repeat, passing the ball back to your hands at the top.

**Tips:** Move slowly so the ball does not bounce away. Keep your lower back on the floor as your legs lower. Use a smaller, softer ball if you find it hard to hold between your feet.

### In daily life
It trains the coordination to lift things with your feet and hands together, as when you sit up while holding a bag or an armful of laundry.

### Why it matters
Passing the ball adds timing and trunk control to a standard V-up, which can help you keep your abs engaged through a longer range of motion.
