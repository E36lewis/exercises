The stability ball tricep dip is a bench dip with your feet resting on a stability ball. The ball is unstable, so your trunk has to work to keep you steady while the triceps do the pushing.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the edge of a sturdy bench with your hands beside your hips, fingers pointing forward.
2. Rest your heels on top of a stability ball in front of you, with your legs straight.
3. Slide your hips off the bench and straighten your arms to hold yourself up.
4. Bend your elbows to lower your hips toward the floor, keeping your back close to the bench.
5. Press back up until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Brace your abs so the ball does not roll away. Keep your shoulders down, away from your ears. Lower only as far as your shoulders feel comfortable, and place the bench against a wall if it slides.

### In daily life
It trains pushing your weight up with your arms while your legs are not on firm ground, as when you climb out of a boat or push up from a low step.

### Why it matters
Putting your feet on a ball adds a balance challenge to a simple triceps exercise, so your abs and the muscles around the shoulder blades are used as well as the arms.
