A core exercise from a push-up position with your shins on a stability ball. You pull your knees in toward your chest, rolling the ball with you. It works the abdominals and hip flexors, while your shoulders and arms hold you up.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position with your hands under your shoulders and your shins or the tops of your feet on the ball.
2. Make a straight line from head to ankles.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Pull your knees toward your chest, rolling the ball in.
5. Pause when your hips rise a little and your knees are under your hips.
6. Extend your legs back out to the straight line, slowly.

**Tips:** Keep your hips level and your arms straight. Move slowly so the ball doesn't veer sideways. Stop if your wrists, shoulders or lower back hurt.

### In daily life
Drawing your legs up while holding your upper body steady is like getting off the floor, or lifting your legs into a car while leaning on your arms.

### Why it matters
It joins the abdominals, hip flexors and shoulder stabilisers into one task and can build core strength with an unsteady surface.
