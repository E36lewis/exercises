The stability ball chest press is a dumbbell press done with your upper back on a ball. It works the chest, front of the shoulders and triceps, and the ball makes your core and hips work to keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the ball with a dumbbell in each hand and walk your feet forward until your upper back rests on the ball.
2. Lift your hips so your body forms a flat line from knees to shoulders.
3. Hold the dumbbells over your chest with your palms facing forward.
4. Lower them slowly until your elbows are just below shoulder height.
5. Press them back up until your arms are straight.
6. Keep your feet wide and your hips lifted throughout.

**Tips:** Keep your hips up and your feet wide for stability. Do not let your wrists bend back. Use lighter weights than on a bench and a non-slip floor.

### In daily life
It trains the push you use to move a heavy object while keeping your balance.

### Why it matters
It can help build upper-body strength together with the core and hip stability the ball demands.
