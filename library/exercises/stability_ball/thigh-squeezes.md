Thigh squeezes use a stability ball held between your knees to work the adductors, the inner-thigh muscles that pull your legs together. It is a gentle, low-impact way to wake up the muscles that steady your hips and knees.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Place a stability ball between your knees.
3. Squeeze the ball with your thighs, as if you were trying to bring your knees together.
4. Hold for a moment at a firm but comfortable effort.
5. Release gently and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your lower back relaxed against the floor and your belly soft. Squeeze steadily instead of jerking. Breathe out as you squeeze. Use a half-inflated ball if a full one feels too hard to hold.

### In daily life
The adductors work when you cross your legs, swing your leg into a car, or step sideways to avoid an obstacle.

### Why it matters
Strong inner thighs can help support the pelvis and knees, and they balance the outer hip muscles for steadier movement.
