This is a side-on core hold using a stability ball. You support yourself on one forearm with your body in a straight line and your feet on the ball. It mainly works the obliques, the muscles at the sides of your waist, with the shoulder-blade muscles and the deep lower-back muscle (quadratus lumborum) helping.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with your forearm on the floor and your elbow under your shoulder.
2. Rest your feet on a stability ball, one on top of the other.
3. Lift your hips so your body forms a straight line.
4. Brace your core and breathe steadily.
5. Hold, then lower with control and switch sides.

**Tips:** Keep your hips high and your body straight. Don't let your shoulder sink. Place the ball against a wall if it rolls.

### In daily life
Carrying a heavy bag on one side or being pulled sideways by a dog on a lead asks the sides of your trunk to keep you straight.

### Why it matters
It builds side-core strength, which supports spine stability and can help protect the lower back. The ball adds an unsteady surface to challenge it further.
