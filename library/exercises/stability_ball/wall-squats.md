The stability ball wall squat is a squat with a ball held between your lower back and a wall. The ball rolls as you bend and straighten your knees, giving back support. It mainly works the quadriceps, with the glutes, hamstrings and adductors helping.

### How to do it
1. Place a stability ball between the wall and your lower back, and lean into it.
2. Step your feet forward until they are about shoulder-width apart and in front of your knees.
3. Bend your knees and let the ball roll down the wall as you sit back and down.
4. Lower until your thighs are about parallel to the floor.
5. Press through your heels to straighten your legs, rolling the ball back up.

**Tips:** Keep your knees pointing in the same direction as your toes. Keep gentle pressure on the ball throughout so it does not slip away. Keep your weight on your heels and midfoot, and use a ball that is firm but not over-inflated.

### In daily life
It trains lowering yourself into a chair, a car seat or a toilet and standing back up with control.

### Why it matters
Leaning on the ball lets you learn a good squat pattern with less load on your lower back, which makes it useful for beginners and for building leg endurance.
