A core exercise with your shins or feet on a stability ball and your hands on the floor. You lift your hips into an inverted V, working the abdominals, with the shoulders and chest holding you up.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position with your shins or the tops of your feet on the ball.
2. Keep your hands under your shoulders.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Roll the ball toward your hands as your hips rise.
5. Pause when your hips are high.
6. Roll the ball back to the start with control.

**Tips:** Keep your arms straight and avoid sagging at the hips. Move slowly. Stop if you feel a wrist, shoulder or lower back strain.

### In daily life
Supporting your weight on your hands while you fold at the hips is like getting up from the floor or pushing yourself out of a low chair.

### Why it matters
The unstable ball can build core strength and shoulder stability together.
