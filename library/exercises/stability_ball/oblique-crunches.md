A crunch on a stability ball with a twist. It targets the obliques (the side abdominal muscles), with the deeper core muscles and hip flexors helping, and the ball makes your trunk work to stay balanced.

### How to do it
1. Sit on a stability ball, then walk your feet forward until your lower back is supported.
2. Place your hands lightly beside your head.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Curl up and rotate your shoulder toward the opposite knee.
5. Pause.
6. Lower back slowly and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Rotate from your ribs rather than pulling on your neck. Keep your feet planted wide for balance. Go slowly and stop if your lower back hurts.

### In daily life
The curl-and-turn is what you do to reach across a table or twist to grab a bag from the passenger seat.

### Why it matters
Stronger obliques can help stabilise your trunk and support twisting movements.
