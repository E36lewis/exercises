The stability ball bird dog has you lie face down over a ball and lift the opposite arm and leg. The ball moves under you, so your trunk muscles, back extensors and glutes must work to keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down over a stability ball with your hips on it, your hands on the floor and your toes on the floor.
2. Brace your abdomen and keep your spine long.
3. Lift one arm and the opposite leg until they are in line with your torso.
4. Hold for a moment without letting the ball roll.
5. Lower both slowly to the floor.
6. Repeat with the other arm and leg.

**Tips:** Move slowly and keep the ball still. Do not arch your lower back. Place the ball on a non-slip surface, and start with smaller lifts or just one limb.

### In daily life
It trains the trunk control you need when you reach for something on a high shelf while balancing on an uneven surface.

### Why it matters
It can help build the back and core control that supports your spine, with an unstable surface to challenge it.
