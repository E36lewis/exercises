A crunch where you curl your hips off the floor instead of lifting your shoulders, with a stability ball held between your lower legs. It works the lower part of the rectus abdominis, with the hip flexors and obliques helping.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your arms by your sides, or hold something behind you for support.
2. Squeeze a stability ball between your lower legs, with your knees bent over your hips.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Curl your hips off the floor, bringing your knees toward your chest.
5. Pause at the top.
6. Lower your hips slowly until they touch the floor.

**Tips:** Move slowly and use your abs, not momentum. Don't swing your legs or arch your lower back. Stop if your lower back hurts.

### In daily life
Rolling your hips up and tucking your knees is the motion you use to roll out of bed or turn over on the floor.

### Why it matters
It targets the lower abdominals in a way that regular crunches do not. It can help build the control that supports your pelvis.
