The deadbug is a core exercise done lying on your back, holding a stability ball between your hands and knees. Your abdominal muscles keep your spine still while your limbs move, which is the whole challenge.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your arms straight up and your knees bent over your hips.
2. Hold the stability ball between your hands and knees.
3. Press your lower back lightly into the floor and brace your abdomen.
4. Slowly straighten one arm overhead and the opposite leg out, while the other hand and knee keep the ball pinned.
5. Hold for a moment, then bring them back to the start.
6. Repeat on the other side, moving slowly.

**Tips:** Keep your lower back on the floor; stop the movement where it starts to arch. Breathe out as you extend. Slow beats fast here.

### In daily life
It trains your trunk to stay steady while an arm and a leg move, as when you reach for a shelf or step while carrying a child.

### Why it matters
It can help build core control, which supports posture and a comfortable back during everyday movement.
