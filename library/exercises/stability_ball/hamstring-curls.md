Hamstring curls use a stability ball under your heels. You bridge up and roll the ball toward your hips, so the back of your thighs pull your heels in while your glutes hold your hips up.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your arms by your sides and your heels on top of the ball, legs straight.
2. Press your heels into the ball and lift your hips so your body makes a straight line from shoulders to heels.
3. Keeping your hips high, bend your knees and roll the ball toward your glutes.
4. Pause for a moment when your feet are close.
5. Slowly straighten your legs to roll the ball back out.
6. Lower your hips to the floor when you finish.

**Tips:** Keep your hips high and level the entire time. Move slowly, because the ball does not have to roll fast. Stop if you feel cramping in the back of the thigh.

### In daily life
The hamstrings bend your knee and slow your leg when you walk, run or step down from a kerb, so they matter every time you move your feet.

### Why it matters
The ball makes this a balance task as well as a strength task, so it can help build hamstring strength and control of the hips together. That mix is useful for sport and for a healthy-feeling knee.
