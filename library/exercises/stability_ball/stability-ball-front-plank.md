The stability ball front plank is a plank done with your forearms on a stability ball. The ball moves under you, so your abdominals and deep trunk muscles have to work harder to hold you still. Your shoulders and hips help as well.

### How to do it
1. Kneel in front of a stability ball and place your forearms on it, with your elbows under your shoulders.
2. Walk your feet back until your body forms a straight line from head to heels.
3. Squeeze your abs and glutes.
4. Hold the position and try to keep the ball from rolling or wobbling.
5. Breathe steadily as you hold.
6. Lower your knees to the floor to finish.

**Tips:** Do not let your hips sag or rise. Keep your neck in line with your spine by looking at the floor. Use a wider foot stance if you wobble. Stop if you feel pain in your lower back.

### In daily life
Holding steady on an unstable surface is like keeping your body steady while leaning over a bathtub to wash it, or bracing over a counter.

### Why it matters
The unstable ball can help build core endurance and shoulder control, which supports good posture.
