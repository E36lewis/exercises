The stability ball bridge is a hip lift with your heels or upper back on a ball. The glutes lift the hips, the hamstrings help, and the unstable ball makes your core and legs work to keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your heels on top of a stability ball and your knees bent or straight, as you can manage.
2. Rest your arms on the floor at your sides.
3. Press your heels down and squeeze your glutes.
4. Lift your hips until your body is in a straight line from shoulders to knees.
5. Hold for a moment without letting the ball roll.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Do not arch your lower back at the top. Keep the ball steady. Use a non-slip floor and bend your knees more if the hamstrings cramp.

### In daily life
It trains the hip extension you use to push up from a deep chair or the floor.

### Why it matters
It can help build glute, hamstring and core strength, which supports the hips and lower back when the surface is not steady.
