Thoracic extension over a foam roller is a mobility stretch for your upper and mid back. You lie with a roller across the back of your ribs and gently bend backward over it. It opens up the stiff, rounded upper back that comes from long periods of sitting.

### How to do it
1. Place a foam roller on the floor and sit in front of it, then lie back so the roller sits across your mid back, just below your shoulder blades.
2. Support your head with your hands, elbows pointing forward, and keep your hips on the floor or slightly lifted.
3. Keeping your abdominals gently braced, lean back over the roller until you feel a stretch in your upper back.
4. Hold for a few breaths, then lift up and move the roller a little higher or lower.
5. Repeat on two or three spots along your upper back.

**Tips:** Keep your lower ribs down and do not let your lower back arch to make the movement. Avoid rolling over your lower back. Breathe out as you lean back, and stop if you feel any sharp pain.

### In daily life
It eases the stiff upper back from desk work, driving and looking at a phone.

### Why it matters
Swimmers, gymnasts, climbers, wheelchair athletes and precision sports like archery need to extend the upper back to reach overhead and hold good posture. Better thoracic mobility also takes strain off the shoulders and lower back.
