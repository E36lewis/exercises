A lunge with a trunk rotation toward the front leg. It works the thighs and glutes, and the abdominal and back muscles that rotate your trunk (the obliques and the spinal muscles).

### How to do it
1. Stand tall and hold a light weight in both hands at chest height.
2. Step forward into a lunge.
3. Lower until both knees are bent.
4. Keep your hips facing forward.
5. Rotate your upper body toward your front leg.
6. Turn back to the centre, then push up to standing and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Twist from your ribs and upper back, not from your lower back or knees. Keep your front knee steady. Keep the weight light and move slowly to protect your back.

### In daily life
Turning while you step is the motion of reaching into the back seat of a car, or turning to hand something to someone beside you as you walk.

### Why it matters
Combining a lunge with a twist trains your legs and trunk together, which can help support balance when you rotate under load.
