This is a front squat done while holding a hammer out to the side in one hand, like a flag pole. The thighs and glutes do the squat, while the shoulder, forearm and trunk fight the off-centre weight.

### How to do it
1. Hold the hammer at the end of the handle with one hand.
2. Raise it so it extends sideways from your shoulder like a flag pole.
3. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
4. Brace your abdomen.
5. Squat down, keeping the hammer steady.
6. Stand up and switch arms.

**Tips:** Use a light hammer. Keep your torso upright. Clear the space around you.

### In daily life
It trains carrying a load on one side, such as a heavy tool or a bag.

### Why it matters
It can help build grip and core stability, since the weight tries to tip you to one side.
