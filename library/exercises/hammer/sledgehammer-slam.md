The sledgehammer slam is a full-body conditioning move. You raise a sledgehammer overhead and bring it down onto a large tire. The legs, hips, back, shoulders and core all take part.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a tire with your feet about shoulder-width apart. Hold the handle with one hand near the head and the other near the end.
2. Lift the hammer up and overhead as you rise onto your legs and straighten your hips.
3. Brace your core and keep your chest up at the top.
4. Drive the hammer down in an arc. Slide your lower hand toward the head as you swing.
5. Hinge your hips and bend your knees as the hammer lands near the centre of the tire.
6. Let the hammer rebound, then lift it again in one smooth rhythm.

**Tips:** Keep the tire well away from other people and from walls. Check that the hammer head is firmly fixed to the handle. If you want to build skill first, swing a lighter hammer. Swap which hand leads to keep both sides even.

### In daily life
It matches the swing of splitting firewood, breaking up soil, or driving a fence post with a maul.

### Why it matters
The slam teaches your legs and trunk to pass force up to your arms, which can help build power and grip endurance.
