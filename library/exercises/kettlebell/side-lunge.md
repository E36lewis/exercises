This is a sideways lunge holding a kettlebell. You step wide to one side, sit back into that hip and push back up. It works the front of the thighs and the glutes and inner thighs, while your core keeps you steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet together, holding a kettlebell in front of your chest.
2. Take a big step to the side.
3. Push your hips back and bend the stepping knee, keeping the other leg straight.
4. Keep your chest up and your foot flat.
5. Push through your foot to return to standing.
6. Repeat, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Keep your knee in line with your toes. Don't let your heel lift. Use a step length you can control.

### In daily life
Stepping off a curb sideways, moving through a crowd and reaching down to a low shelf to one side all draw on this side-to-side strength.

### Why it matters
Most training moves forward and back; this works side-to-side strength and flexibility in the inner thigh, which supports hip stability and balance.
