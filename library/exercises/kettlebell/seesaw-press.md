This is an alternating overhead press with two kettlebells. One goes up as the other comes down, like a seesaw. It works the shoulders and the muscle along your ribs that moves the shoulder blade (serratus anterior), with the triceps helping.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in each hand at shoulder height.
2. Brace your core and keep your knees soft.
3. Press one kettlebell overhead until your arm is straight.
4. As you lower it, press the other one up.
5. Keep alternating, so one goes up as the other comes down.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and avoid leaning sideways. Keep your wrists straight. Control the weights so neither swings into your head.

### In daily life
Loading shelves with one hand while the other lowers a box, and keeping steady when you carry a load unevenly, use the same side-to-side balance.

### Why it matters
The alternating pattern asks your trunk to resist leaning from side to side. That builds shoulder strength together with coordination.
