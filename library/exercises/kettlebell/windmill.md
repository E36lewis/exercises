The kettlebell windmill is a core, hip and shoulder exercise. You keep a kettlebell locked out overhead, push your hips out to the side, and slide your free hand down your leg toward the floor. It stretches the hamstrings and works the obliques, glutes and the stabilising muscles of the shoulder.

### How to do it
1. Hold a kettlebell overhead in your right hand with your arm straight, and turn your feet about 45 degrees to the left.
2. Look up at the bell and keep your right arm vertical.
3. Push your right hip out to the side, keeping your right knee almost straight.
4. Lean your torso to the left and slide your left hand down the inside of your left leg.
5. Stop when you feel a stretch in the back of your right thigh, or touch your shin or foot.
6. Squeeze your glutes and return to standing, then repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Learn with no weight first, then a light bell. Keep your eyes on the kettlebell, and keep your arm locked. Move slowly, and stop if your shoulder or back feels uncomfortable.

### In daily life
It trains reaching down to the floor to the side of you while you steady an object overhead, such as carrying a tray or a tool.

### Why it matters
It combines shoulder stability, hip hinge and side bending in one movement, helping hip mobility and control.
