The kettlebell alternating arm press is a standing overhead press done one arm at a time. It works the shoulders and triceps, while your core and glutes keep you steady against the off-centre load.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
2. Clean a kettlebell to the rack position in each hand, resting at shoulder height with your elbows close to your body. (With only one bell, hold it in one hand and switch sides between sets.)
3. Tighten your abdominals and squeeze your glutes.
4. Press one kettlebell straight overhead until your arm is fully extended.
5. Lower it under control back to your shoulder.
6. Press the other side, alternating arms.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and avoid arching your lower back. Press in a straight line close to your head. Choose a weight you can control overhead and keep your wrist straight. The bell should rest on your forearm, not pull your wrist back.

### In daily life
A kettlebell is lopsided, so pressing it overhead is like lifting a bulky bag into a luggage rack or a box onto a high shelf.

### Why it matters
Pressing one side at a time can help build shoulder strength and teach your trunk to resist leaning toward the loaded side, which supports a stable spine.
