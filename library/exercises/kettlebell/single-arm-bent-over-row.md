This is a kettlebell row for one arm in a forward hinge. It mainly works the lats and teres major, with your upper back and arms helping and your legs and hips holding the position.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand.
2. Hinge forward at the hips with a flat back and soft knees.
3. Let your arm hang straight down.
4. Pull the kettlebell towards your hip, keeping your elbow close to your body.
5. Pause and squeeze your shoulder blade towards your spine.
6. Lower slowly, finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and hips level. Don't twist your shoulders as you pull. Use a weight you can control without jerking.

### In daily life
You pull the same way when you lift a grocery bag off the floor of the car or yank the cord on a lawn mower.

### Why it matters
It builds upper-back strength that supports posture, and working one arm at a time can help you notice and even out differences between sides.
