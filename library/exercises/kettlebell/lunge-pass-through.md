A lunge with a kettlebell that you pass from hand to hand under your front thigh. The legs and glutes do the lunge, while the obliques and hips control your trunk as the weight moves across.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a kettlebell in one hand.
2. Step forward into a lunge.
3. Keep your chest up and your front knee over your ankle.
4. Pass the kettlebell under your front thigh to your other hand.
5. Step back up to standing.
6. Lunge again on the other leg, passing the kettlebell back.

**Tips:** Hinge slightly at the hips to pass the weight, but keep your back flat. Use a light weight and move in a controlled way. Keep a firm grip so you don't drop it on your foot.

### In daily life
It copies reaching low with one hand and passing a heavy item to the other, such as moving a pot from a low cupboard to the counter.

### Why it matters
Moving the weight around your body while you balance on a split stance combines leg strength with trunk control, which can help you handle awkward loads.
