The arm clean is a one-arm kettlebell lift that brings the bell from the floor to your shoulder in one fluid motion. The hips, legs and glutes supply the power, and the back, shoulder and grip finish the job.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, a kettlebell on the floor between your feet.
2. Hinge at your hips, keeping your back flat, and grip the handle.
3. Drive through your legs and hips to stand tall, pulling the bell up close to your body.
4. As the bell rises, bring your elbow in and let the bell rotate to rest at your shoulder.
5. Stand tall for a moment.
6. Lower the bell along the same path, hinging at your hips.

**Tips:** Use your legs and hips to lift, not your arm. Keep the bell close so it does not slam against your forearm. Start light and practise the hip hinge first.

### In daily life
It copies picking a bag of groceries or a bucket up off the ground and bringing it to chest height without bending over it.

### Why it matters
Coordinating hips, back and shoulder in one pull can help build full-body power and grip, and gives you practice at lifting from the floor with a flat back.
