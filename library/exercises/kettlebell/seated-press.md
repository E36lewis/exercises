This is a one-arm overhead press with a kettlebell, done sitting down. You press the bell from shoulder height until your arm is straight above you. It mainly works the shoulders (deltoids) and triceps, with the upper back helping you keep the weight steady.

### How to do it
1. Sit on a bench or chair with your back upright and feet flat.
2. Hold a kettlebell by the handle at shoulder height, with your wrist straight and the bell resting against your forearm.
3. Brace your core.
4. Press the kettlebell straight overhead until your arm is straight.
5. Pause, then lower it under control to shoulder height.
6. Repeat, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and avoid arching your back. Keep your wrist straight. Choose a weight you can control, and keep the bell away from your head.

### In daily life
Sliding a case into an overhead bin, putting a jar on a high shelf and lifting a child onto a tall seat all use this overhead push.

### Why it matters
Sitting takes your legs out of the lift, so your shoulders and arms do the work. Pressing one side at a time also shows you whether one arm is weaker than the other.
