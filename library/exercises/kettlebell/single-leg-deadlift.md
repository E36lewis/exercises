This is a one-leg hip hinge holding a kettlebell. It works the glutes and hamstrings, and your back and thigh muscles keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg, holding a kettlebell in front of you with both hands or one.
2. Keep a slight bend in your standing knee.
3. Hinge at the hip and extend your other leg straight behind.
4. Lower the kettlebell towards the floor with your back flat.
5. Squeeze your glutes to stand.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your hips square and don't round your back. Use a light weight until your balance is solid. Fix your eyes on a point on the floor ahead.

### In daily life
You lean and balance like this when you reach down to take a heavy pot from a low cupboard with one foot braced.

### Why it matters
It builds single-leg strength and a firm hinge, which can help you lift things from the floor with a steady back.
