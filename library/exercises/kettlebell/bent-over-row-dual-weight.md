This is a bent-over row with a kettlebell in each hand. It works the large back muscles (latissimus dorsi and teres major), the upper back and the biceps, while your hips and lower back hold the bent position.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and a kettlebell in each hand.
2. Push your hips back and lean forward until your torso is roughly parallel to the floor, with your knees slightly bent.
3. Let your arms hang straight down with your palms facing each other.
4. Pull both kettlebells toward your lower ribs, leading with your elbows.
5. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top.
6. Lower the weights slowly until your arms are straight again.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and your neck in line with your spine. Do not jerk the weights up with your hips. Choose a load you can hold without rounding your back.

### In daily life
It trains the position and pull you use when you lift a heavy grocery bag or a pet carrier off the ground.

### Why it matters
It can help build back strength and practise holding a hip hinge under load, which supports good posture.
