The floor press is a kettlebell press done lying on the ground, one arm at a time. It works the chest, the front of the shoulders and the triceps, and the floor stops your upper arm at a set depth.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Hold a kettlebell in each hand with your elbows resting on the floor and your forearms upright.
3. Press one kettlebell straight up until your arm is extended over your shoulder.
4. Lower it slowly until your upper arm touches the floor, then pause briefly.
5. Press the other kettlebell up and lower it.
6. Continue alternating arms.

**Tips:** Keep your wrist straight and the bell steady, resting against your forearm. Do not bounce your elbow off the floor. Keep your head and back flat on the floor, and start light if you are new to pressing kettlebells.

### In daily life
Pushing from the floor is the same motion as shoving yourself away from the ground when you get up, or pushing a heavy box across the floor while lying under a bed or car.

### Why it matters
The floor gives back support and a built-in stopping point, so it can be a gentler way to build pressing strength while limiting how far the shoulder stretches back.
