This is a one-arm kettlebell press on the floor, with a small reach at the top. It works the chest, front shoulder and triceps.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and a kettlebell held above your chest.
2. Keep your wrist straight and stacked over your forearm.
3. Lower the kettlebell until your upper arm touches the floor.
4. Press up until your arm is straight.
5. Reach a little higher at the top, letting your shoulder blade slide forward.
6. Lower with control and repeat, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your elbow about 45 degrees from your body. Don't drop the bell onto your arm. Start with a light weight.

### In daily life
You push the same way to roll yourself up off the floor or to shove open a stuck window.

### Why it matters
The floor limits how far your arm drops, which can be kinder on the shoulders, and the reach at the top trains the muscle that moves the shoulder blade.
