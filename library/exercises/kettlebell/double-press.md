The double press is a kettlebell shoulder press done with two kettlebells at once. The shoulders (deltoids) do most of the pushing, with the triceps and upper back helping and the trunk holding you steady.

### How to do it
1. Clean both kettlebells to your shoulders, elbows close to your ribs and forearms upright.
2. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and brace your abdomen.
3. Press both bells straight overhead until your arms are straight.
4. At the top, your biceps should be near your ears and the bells over your shoulders.
5. Pause briefly, then lower the bells to your shoulders under control.
6. Repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down and don't arch your back. Press in a straight line, letting your head move slightly forward at the top. Use lighter bells until you can press without leaning.

### In daily life
It trains pushing two loads overhead at once, such as lifting two bags onto a high rack.

### Why it matters
It can help build shoulder strength and stability, which supports reaching overhead. Holding two bells also shows up any imbalance between your two sides.
