This is a kettlebell swing with one hand. It works the glutes, hamstrings and thighs, and your shoulder and grip hold the bell as it swings.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wider than shoulder-width, with a kettlebell in front of you.
2. Hinge at your hips and grip the handle with one hand.
3. Hike the bell back between your legs.
4. Snap your hips forward to swing the bell up to about chest height.
5. Let it fall back and hinge again, keeping your arm loose.
6. Finish your reps, then switch hands.

**Tips:** Power the swing with your hips, not your arm. Keep your back flat and your core braced. Clear the space around you in case you let go.

### In daily life
You use the same hip snap to swing a heavy suitcase up onto a bed or to throw a bag into a truck bed.

### Why it matters
It builds hip power and grip, and the single-arm version makes your trunk resist twisting.
