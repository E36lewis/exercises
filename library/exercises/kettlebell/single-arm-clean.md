This is a fast lift that brings a kettlebell from the floor to your shoulder with one hand. Your legs, hips and shoulder drive it, and your forearm and grip keep it controlled.

### How to do it
1. Stand over a kettlebell with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hinge at your hips and grip the handle with one hand.
3. Drive through your legs and hips to stand up fast.
4. As the bell rises, pull your elbow in and rotate your hand around the handle.
5. Catch the kettlebell at your shoulder, resting against your forearm.
6. Lower it back to the floor with control, then repeat.

**Tips:** Let your legs and hips power the lift, not your arm. Keep the bell close to your body so it doesn't swing out. Start with a light weight to learn the catch and avoid banging your forearm.

### In daily life
It matches lifting something heavy from the ground to your chest in one move, such as a sack of potting soil.

### Why it matters
It trains quick hip power, timing and grip together, which can help with lifting and moving with force.
