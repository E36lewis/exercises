This is a one-arm kettlebell press done while you hinge forward. The triceps do the pressing, and your lats, glutes, hamstrings and core hold your torso steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a kettlebell in one hand.
2. Hinge forward at your hips with a flat back and soft knees.
3. Bring the kettlebell to your shoulder, with your elbow bent.
4. Brace your core and keep your hips level.
5. Press the kettlebell out and back until your arm is straight.
6. Lower it with control and finish your reps, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your back flat and your hips square, and don't twist. Choose a weight you can hold in the stance first. Look at a spot on the floor to keep your neck long.

### In daily life
You may push something away from you, such as a stuck drawer, while you lean over a counter or sink.

### Why it matters
Pressing in a hinge trains your back and hips to stay still while your arm moves, which can help protect your back during one-sided pushing.
