The halo circles a kettlebell around your head like a halo. It moves and strengthens the shoulders and upper back, and your trunk has to stay still while the weight passes behind you.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Hold the kettlebell upside down by the horns, in front of your chest with both hands.
3. Brace your abdomen and keep your ribs down.
4. Slowly move the bell to one side of your head, then behind your neck.
5. Continue around to the other side and back to the front, keeping it close to your head.
6. Complete the set, then reverse the direction.

**Tips:** Keep your hips and ribs still and don't lean back. Use a light weight to begin. Keep the bell clear of your face and neck.

### In daily life
Reaching around and over your head, as when you pull on a coat or put an item on a high shelf, needs this kind of shoulder control.

### Why it matters
Circling a weight around the head can help build shoulder stability and mobility. It also teaches you to keep your trunk still while your arms move.
