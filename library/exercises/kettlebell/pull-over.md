A pull over done lying on a bench or the floor with a kettlebell held by its horns. It works the lats, with the chest, the triceps and the upper back helping, and your forearms working to keep hold of the kettlebell.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent, holding a kettlebell by the horns above your chest with both hands.
2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and lock your wrists in line with your forearms.
3. Lower the kettlebell in an arc behind your head, without letting the bell reach your head.
4. Stop at a comfortable stretch under your arms.
5. Keep your ribs down so your lower back stays flat.
6. Pull the kettlebell back over your chest.

**Tips:** Check that the floor behind you is clear. Use a light kettlebell. On the floor the range stops when your elbows touch, which can be safer for your shoulders.

### In daily life
Pulling something heavy toward you from overhead, such as an item from a high shelf, uses the same line of pull.

### Why it matters
It trains the shoulder through a large range and builds a strong grip on an awkward handle.
