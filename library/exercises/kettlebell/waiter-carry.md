The waiter carry is a walking exercise in which you hold a kettlebell overhead with a straight arm, like a waiter with a tray, and walk. The shoulder muscles keep the weight steady while the trunk resists leaning.

### How to do it
1. Clean or press a kettlebell overhead with one arm and lock out your elbow, with your wrist straight.
2. Stand tall with your ribs down and your core braced.
3. Walk slowly in a straight line for the set distance or time without leaning.
4. Keep your eyes forward and your bicep near your ear.
5. Lower the kettlebell with control and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Do not let the bell drift forward or your wrist bend back. Start with a light bell and keep a clear path. If your arm tires, lower the bell rather than risk dropping it overhead.

### In daily life
It is the same as carrying a tray, a bag or a child on your shoulder through a crowd.

### Why it matters
Bat, racket, collision, gymnastics and climbing sports need a stable shoulder that holds up under load. The walk adds an element of balance as well.
