The suitcase carry is a one-sided walking exercise. You carry a heavy kettlebell in one hand at your side and walk without leaning. The obliques and the muscles beside your spine work to stop your trunk bending toward the weight, which is called anti-lateral-flexion. Your grip works hard too.

### How to do it
1. Place a kettlebell on the floor beside one foot.
2. Hinge your hips back, bend your knees and pick it up with one hand, then stand tall.
3. Keep your shoulders level and your ribs stacked over your pelvis.
4. Walk slowly in a straight line for the set distance or time without leaning.
5. Put the kettlebell down with control and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Do not tilt toward the weight or shrug the loaded shoulder. Take small, steady steps. Do the same distance on each side.

### In daily life
It is exactly like carrying a heavy shopping bag or suitcase from the car to the house.

### Why it matters
A strong, level trunk and a firm grip help in every sport that involves carrying, tackling, climbing or changing direction under load.
