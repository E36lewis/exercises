The kettlebell sumo high pull is a hip-driven lift. From a wide stance, you stand and pull the kettlebell up to your chest or chin. It works the glutes, thighs, upper back and shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wide and toes turned out, with a kettlebell on the floor between your feet.
2. Hinge your hips back, bend your knees and grab the handle with both hands.
3. Keep your back flat and your chest up.
4. Drive your hips and knees forward to stand.
5. As you rise, let the legs power the bell upward and pull it to chest height with your elbows high and out.
6. Lower the bell with control and repeat.

**Tips:** Let your legs and hips do the work, with your arms guiding the pull. Keep the bell close to your body on the way up. Do not round your back or shrug your shoulders to your ears.

### In daily life
It copies lifting a heavy item off the ground and bringing it up to chest level, such as a water jug or a box to a countertop.

### Why it matters
It joins leg drive with upper back and shoulder work, which can help build power and conditioning with one movement.
