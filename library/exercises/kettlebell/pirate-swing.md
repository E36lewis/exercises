A kettlebell exercise where you swing the weight in a diagonal arc from one hip toward the opposite side. It works the obliques, the muscles along the spine and the hips as they control the rotation.

### How to do it
1. Stand with feet wider than your hips, holding a kettlebell in both hands.
2. Hinge slightly at the hips with a flat back.
3. Swing the kettlebell diagonally up toward one shoulder.
4. Let your torso follow the movement.
5. Swing it back down and across to the other side.
6. Continue smoothly side to side.

**Tips:** Keep your hips stable and your back flat. Use a light weight first. Make sure there is clear space around you.

### In daily life
The diagonal swing is like hauling a bag of soil or a bucket up from one side and tipping it over to the other.

### Why it matters
It can help build rotational strength and trunk control, which supports sports and work that involve swinging.
