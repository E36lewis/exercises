The kettlebell bottoms up press is a one-arm overhead press with the bell held upside down, bottom up. The bell wants to tip, so the rotator cuff and the muscles of the forearm and grip must keep it steady.

### How to do it
1. Clean a kettlebell to the rack position and turn it so the bell is above your hand, balanced upside down.
2. Squeeze the handle hard, keeping your wrist straight and your elbow near your ribs.
3. Brace your core and press the kettlebell straight overhead, keeping the bell balanced.
4. Lock out your arm, then lower the bell with control to the rack.
5. Repeat for all repetitions, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Use a light kettlebell at first. Keep the wrist straight and the grip tight. Make sure you have space around you, since a bell may fall. Do not tilt your trunk.

### In daily life
It builds the grip and shoulder control to hold something unstable overhead, like a full cup or a tray.

### Why it matters
Bat, gymnastics and climbing sports call for a firm grip and a steady shoulder. The upside-down hold trains both.
