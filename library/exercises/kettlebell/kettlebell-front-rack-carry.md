The kettlebell front rack carry is a walking exercise in which you hold one or two kettlebells at your chest in the rack position and walk. The weight in front pulls you forward, so the trunk muscles and shoulders must keep you upright.

### How to do it
1. Clean one or two kettlebells to the rack position, with the bell resting on your forearm and your elbow tucked near your ribs.
2. Stand tall with your ribs down and your core braced.
3. Walk slowly in a straight line for the set distance or time without leaning back.
4. Breathe steadily and keep your wrist straight.
5. Set the bells down safely with a hinge at the hips. If you carry one bell, repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Do not lean back or flare your ribs. Keep the elbow close to your body and the shoulder down. Start with a light bell and work up. A single bell also trains the trunk to resist side bending.

### In daily life
It copies carrying a heavy box or a child held in front of your chest.

### Why it matters
Collision and combat sports need a trunk and shoulders that stay strong while you move with a load in front, such as holding or grappling an opponent.
