The kettlebell farmer carry is a walk while holding a kettlebell in each hand. The forearms and grip work hardest, with the shoulders, upper back and trunk holding you tall and still.

### How to do it
1. Stand between two kettlebells and hinge to pick them up, as in a deadlift.
2. Stand tall with the bells at your sides, shoulders down and back.
3. Brace your abdomen.
4. Walk with short, steady steps.
5. Keep the bells from swinging against your legs.
6. Set them down by hinging at your hips.

**Tips:** Don't lean sideways. Look ahead. Pick up and put down with a flat back.

### In daily life
It matches carrying heavy buckets, bags or luggage, where the handles are thick and your grip tires first.

### Why it matters
It can help build grip strength and an upright carry. The kettlebell handle is thick, so it can challenge your hands in a different way than a dumbbell.
