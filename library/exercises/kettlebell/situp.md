This is a sit-up holding a kettlebell against your chest. It works the abs and obliques, and the grip and upper body help hold the weight.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat.
2. Hold a kettlebell against your chest with both hands.
3. Brace your core.
4. Curl up until you are sitting.
5. Lower slowly with control.

**Tips:** Don't pull on your neck. Keep your feet on the floor, and anchor them if you need to. Start with a light weight.

### In daily life
You use this to sit up in bed when you're holding something, such as a baby or a heavy blanket.

### Why it matters
The weight makes your abs work harder than a body-weight sit-up, which can build abdominal strength and trunk control.
