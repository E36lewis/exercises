The pro agility drill, also called the 5-10-5 or short shuttle, is a standard test of changing direction. You sprint 5 yards one way, 10 the other and 5 back to the start. It trains quick stops and re-accelerations.

### How to do it
1. Place three cones in a line, 5 yards (about 4.5 m) apart. The middle cone is the start.
2. Straddle the middle cone in a low stance with a hand on the ground.
3. Sprint 5 yards to one side and touch the line with your hand.
4. Turn and sprint 10 yards to the far cone, touch the line again.
5. Turn once more and sprint 5 yards back through the middle cone.
6. Walk back and rest.

**Tips:** Do 4 to 6 runs, alternating the starting direction, with 1 to 2 minutes' rest. Plant on the outside foot, drop your hips and push off hard. Warm up thoroughly. Use a grippy surface, because the sharp turns put load on the knees and ankles.

### In daily life
Chasing a child who changes direction, dodging a car and reacting quickly in a game of tag use the same stop and go.

### Why it matters
Field, collision and court sports are decided in short, sharp changes of direction. This drill is a standard measure of that and a repeatable way to train it.
