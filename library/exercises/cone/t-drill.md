The T drill uses four cones in a T shape. You sprint forward, shuffle sideways, shuffle back and backpedal to the start. It trains forward, lateral and backward movement in one run.

### How to do it
1. Place one cone at the start, a second 10 yards (about 9 m) ahead and two more 5 yards (about 4.5 m) to each side of the second cone.
2. Start at the first cone in an athletic stance.
3. Sprint forward to the second cone and touch it.
4. Shuffle sideways to the left cone and touch it, without crossing your feet.
5. Shuffle to the right cone and touch it, then back to the middle cone.
6. Backpedal to the start.

**Tips:** Do 4 to 6 runs with 1 to 2 minutes' rest, alternating the direction of the first shuffle. Stay low, keep your toes forward and keep your hips square. Warm up well and use a non-slip surface.

### In daily life
Walking forward, sidestepping and backing away from something in quick succession are all part of everyday movement.

### Why it matters
Field, court and racket sports require all three directions of movement, often in a few seconds. The T drill is a standard way to measure and train that.
