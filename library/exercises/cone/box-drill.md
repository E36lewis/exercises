The box drill uses four cones in a square. You sprint forward, shuffle sideways, backpedal and shuffle back to the start. It trains movement in four directions with sharp transitions.

### How to do it
1. Place four cones in a square, 5 yards (about 4.5 m) apart.
2. Start at one corner in an athletic stance.
3. Sprint forward to the next cone.
4. Shuffle sideways to the third cone without crossing your feet.
5. Backpedal to the fourth cone, then shuffle back to the start.
6. Rest, then run the square in the opposite direction.

**Tips:** Do 4 to 6 runs in each direction with 1 minute's rest. Stay low and keep your chest over your knees at each cone. Use a grippy surface and warm up first, because the transitions load the knees and ankles.

### In daily life
Moving forward, sideways and backward around a busy kitchen or a shop floor uses the same transitions.

### Why it matters
Field and racket sports need quick transitions between forward, sideways and backward movement. The box drill is a simple pattern to train that footwork.
