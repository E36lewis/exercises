The tricep pushdown is a cable exercise for the back of the upper arm. You stand at a high cable and push a bar or rope down by straightening your elbows. It trains the triceps brachii.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley high with a straight bar or rope and stand facing it with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Grip the handle with your elbows tucked at your sides and bent to about 90 degrees.
3. Lean forward very slightly and brace your core.
4. Push the handle down until your arms are straight.
5. Let the handle rise slowly until your forearms are about parallel to the floor.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows pinned to your sides and do not let your shoulders roll forward. Do not lean on the bar with your body. Control the way up.

### In daily life
It trains the push for straightening your arm to push off a table or close a heavy door.

### Why it matters
Pole planting in snow sports, pushing off in gymnastics and climbing, and punching in combat sports depend on strong triceps.
