The seated cable row is a pulling exercise. You sit at a low cable and pull a handle toward your belly. It trains the muscles in the middle of your back, including the rhomboids and the lats.

### How to do it
1. Sit at the cable row with your feet on the footplates, knees slightly bent, and grip the handle.
2. Sit tall with your chest up and your arms straight.
3. Brace your core and pull the handle to your lower ribs, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
4. Pause briefly.
5. Return slowly until your arms are straight, keeping your back tall.

**Tips:** Do not lean far back or round your back as you let the weight go forward. Use your back, not your arms or body swing, to pull. Keep your shoulders down.

### In daily life
It builds the pulling strength for rowing, hauling and opening heavy doors.

### Why it matters
Swimming, snow sports, gymnastics and climbing all call for strong pulling from the back. It also balances pressing exercises.
