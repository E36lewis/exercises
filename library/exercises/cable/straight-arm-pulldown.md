The straight arm pulldown is a cable exercise that isolates the lats. You stand facing a high cable and push a bar down with straight arms, as in the pulling phase of a swimming stroke. The elbows stay almost straight, so the lats do the work.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley high with a straight bar or rope and stand facing it, a step or two back.
2. Hold the bar with your arms straight and slightly in front of you, hands shoulder-width apart.
3. Hinge slightly forward and brace your core.
4. Pull the bar down to your thighs, keeping your arms almost straight.
5. Let the bar rise slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Keep the elbow bend small and fixed. Do not use your triceps to push the bar or lean back with your trunk. Choose a weight you can control for the whole range.

### In daily life
It trains the sweeping pull you use to move a heavy load back toward your body.

### Why it matters
Swimmers pull the water with straight arms. This lift trains the lats in that pattern.
