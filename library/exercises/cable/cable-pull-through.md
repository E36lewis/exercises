The cable pull through is a hip hinge. You face away from a low cable and pull the rope between your legs by pushing your hips forward. It trains the glutes and hamstrings with little load on your lower back.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley low with a rope attachment, and stand facing away from it with the rope between your legs.
2. Hold the rope with both hands and walk forward until the cable is tight.
3. With soft knees, push your hips back until your torso is leaning forward and the rope pulls through your legs, keeping your back flat.
4. Squeeze your glutes and drive your hips forward to stand tall.
5. Let the weight move back with control, then repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your arms straight and let your hips do the work. Do not round your back or squat the weight down. Stand far enough from the machine to keep tension on the cable.

### In daily life
It practices the hinge you use to pick up something light from the floor without bending your back.

### Why it matters
Running, field and collision sports rely on strong hips for sprinting and driving through contact. This lift teaches the hinge with a light, safe load.
