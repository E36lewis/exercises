The cable chop is a rotational core exercise. You pull a cable handle diagonally from high to low across your body, as if chopping wood. The obliques turn and brace your trunk while your arms guide the handle.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley high and stand side-on to the machine with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Grip the handle with both hands, arms reaching up toward the pulley.
3. Brace your core and pull the handle diagonally down across your body to the opposite hip, turning your trunk and pivoting your back foot.
4. Pause briefly, then let the handle travel back up slowly with control.
5. Finish all repetitions, then turn around and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Move from your trunk and hips, not by just swinging your arms. Keep your hips and chest facing the same way as the handle moves and avoid leaning back. Use a weight light enough to control the return.

### In daily life
It copies the twist you use to put a bag in the back seat or to swing an axe or a heavy tool.

### Why it matters
Racket, bat, golf, combat and field sports all start a swing or throw from the hips and trunk. The cable chop trains that diagonal pattern under steady tension, so you can generate and control rotation.
