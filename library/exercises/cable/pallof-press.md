The Pallof press is an anti-rotation core exercise. You stand side-on to a cable and press the handle away from your chest, while your trunk stops the cable from twisting you. The obliques and deep abdominals work to hold you still.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley at chest height and stand side-on to it, far enough away to feel tension.
2. Hold the handle with both hands at the center of your chest, with your feet shoulder-width apart and knees soft.
3. Brace your core and press the handle straight out in front of you until your arms are straight.
4. Hold for a second or two while the cable tries to turn you.
5. Bring the handle back to your chest. Finish all repetitions, then turn around and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your hips and shoulders square to the front and do not let the cable twist you. Stand tall and do not lean away. Start light, because the goal is control, not weight.

### In daily life
It trains you to stay steady when something pulls you sideways, such as a dog on a lead.

### Why it matters
Throwing, swinging, kicking and punching sports all need a trunk that resists unwanted twisting so force can pass cleanly from the legs to the arms. The Pallof press builds that stability.
