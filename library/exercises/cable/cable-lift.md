The cable lift is the reverse of the chop. You pull a cable handle diagonally from low to high across your body. The obliques and hips drive the turn while the shoulders guide the handle overhead.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley low and stand side-on to the machine with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Grip the handle with both hands near the outside hip, arms mostly straight.
3. Brace your core and lift the handle diagonally up and across your body, turning your trunk and pivoting your back foot.
4. Finish with the handle above the opposite shoulder, arms extended.
5. Lower slowly along the same path. Complete all repetitions, then repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Rotate through the hips and trunk and keep your arms mostly straight. Do not lean back or shrug your shoulders up. Control the downward phase, because the cable pulls you back.

### In daily life
It mirrors lifting a bag or a child onto a shelf or into a car on one side of the body.

### Why it matters
Throwing, swinging and striking sports power a lot of movement from low to high, such as a tennis forehand or a baseball swing. This lift trains the trunk to produce and then control that rotation.
