The cable face pull is a pulling exercise for the rear shoulders and upper back. You pull a rope toward your face with your elbows high. It strengthens the rear deltoids, the rotator cuff and the muscles between your shoulder blades.

### How to do it
1. Set a cable pulley at about face height with a rope attachment and hold one end of the rope in each hand with your palms facing in.
2. Step back until your arms are straight and the cable is tight, and stand tall with your core braced.
3. Pull the rope toward your face, spreading the ends apart and keeping your elbows high.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together and turn your hands outward at the end.
5. Return slowly until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Use a light to moderate weight and do not lean back to move it. Keep your elbows higher than your wrists. Avoid shrugging your shoulders up.

### In daily life
It counters the rounded-forward shoulders from desk work, driving and phone use.

### Why it matters
Healthy shoulders matter in every sport that uses the arms. It balances pushing exercises and trains the muscles that steady the shoulder joint.
