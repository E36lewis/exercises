The stationary bike ride is steady pedalling at an easy to moderate pace. There is no impact, so it is a gentle way to build aerobic fitness or to recover.

### How to do it
1. Adjust the seat so your knee is slightly bent at the bottom of the stroke.
2. Set the handlebars so you can sit tall without strain.
3. Start pedalling with light resistance and warm up for 5 minutes.
4. Increase the resistance until you can talk in short sentences.
5. Pedal at about 80 to 100 revolutions per minute.
6. Cool down with 3 to 5 minutes of easy pedalling.

**Tips:** This is typically done in zone 2, a pace at which you can hold a conversation, for 30 to 60 minutes. Keep your shoulders relaxed and your knees tracking straight. Wear comfortable shorts, and drink water.

### In daily life
Commuting by bike, cycling to the shops and riding with family all use the same easy cycling.

### Why it matters
Runners and cyclists build their aerobic base with long easy sessions. The bike spares the joints, so it works well on recovery days or when a running injury needs rest.
