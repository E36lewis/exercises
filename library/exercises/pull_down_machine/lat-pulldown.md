The lat pulldown is a machine exercise for the back. You sit and pull a bar down to your upper chest. It trains the latissimus dorsi, the large muscles of your back, and is a good way to build toward pull-ups.

### How to do it
1. Sit at the machine and adjust the thigh pads so your legs are held firmly.
2. Grip the bar with your hands a little wider than your shoulders, palms facing forward.
3. Lean back slightly and brace your core.
4. Pull the bar down to your upper chest, driving your elbows down and back.
5. Slowly let the bar rise until your arms are nearly straight.

**Tips:** Do not lean far back or jerk the weight with your body. Keep your chest up and your shoulders down. Do not pull the bar behind your neck.

### In daily life
It helps with pulling movements such as pulling yourself up or opening a heavy door.

### Why it matters
Swimmers, gymnasts and climbers rely on a strong pull from the back. This is a way to build that strength for people who cannot yet do pull-ups.
