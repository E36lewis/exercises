The band farmer carry is a walk in which you stand on a resistance band and hold its handles at your sides. The band pulls your hands down, so your forearms and grip work to hold on while your shoulders and trunk keep you upright.

### How to do it
1. Stand on the middle of the band with both feet.
2. Hold a handle in each hand, arms by your sides.
3. Stand tall and pull your shoulders down and back.
4. Walk with short, controlled steps.
5. Keep the band tight and your hands steady at your sides.
6. Stop, turn and walk back, or set the handles down.

**Tips:** Don't let your shoulders shrug or hunch. Keep your ribs down. Check the band for wear before use, as a worn band can snap.

### In daily life
It copies carrying shopping or luggage for a distance, but with a load you can adjust by choosing a lighter or heavier band.

### Why it matters
It can help build grip and posture. A band is easy to pack, so it suits travel or small spaces.
