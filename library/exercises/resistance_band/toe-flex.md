The resistance band toe flex trains the muscles under the foot and in the lower leg that curl your toes down. You curl your toes against the tension of a band.

### How to do it
1. Sit with one leg straight in front of you and loop a resistance band around the ball of your foot.
2. Hold the ends of the band in your hands, with the band taut.
3. Curl your toes down against the pull of the band.
4. Pause for a moment.
5. Return slowly to the start and repeat.
6. Switch feet after the set.

**Tips:** Move slowly and keep the movement small and controlled. Keep your ankle still so the toes do the work. Choose a light band first. Stop if you feel a cramp.

### In daily life
The toes grip the ground as you push off, and in tight shoes, they curl to help you stay upright on a slope.

### Why it matters
Stronger toe flexors can help your feet grip the floor and push off, which supports balance and walking.
