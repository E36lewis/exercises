The banded Pallof press is the home version of the anti-rotation press. You press a resistance band away from your chest while your trunk stops the band from twisting you. The obliques and deep abdominals hold you still.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a resistance band at chest height on a sturdy post or door anchor and stand side-on to it, far enough away to feel tension.
2. Hold the band with both hands at the center of your chest, with feet shoulder-width apart and knees soft.
3. Brace your core and press your hands straight out until your arms are straight.
4. Hold for a second or two while the band tries to turn you.
5. Bring your hands back to your chest. Finish all repetitions, then turn around and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your hips and shoulders square to the front and do not let the band twist you. Check the band for nicks and make sure the anchor is secure. Step further from the anchor for more tension.

### In daily life
It trains you to stay steady when something tugs you to one side, like carrying shopping with a bag that swings.

### Why it matters
Sports with throwing, swinging, kicking and punching need a trunk that resists unwanted twist. This version needs only a band, so it is easy to do at home or on the road.
