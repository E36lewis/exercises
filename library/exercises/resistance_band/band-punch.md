The band punch is a punching exercise with a resistance band. You stand facing away from an anchor and punch forward against the band. It builds punching speed and the muscles that drive a punch, with a light load.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a resistance band at chest height behind you and hold one end in each hand, or one in one hand.
2. Stand with your feet staggered in a fighting stance and your elbows bent.
3. Brace your core and punch straight forward, turning your hips and shoulder into the punch.
4. Return your fist to your chin quickly.
5. Repeat for all repetitions, then switch sides or stance.

**Tips:** Use a light band and keep your elbows from locking hard at the end. Rotate your hips and trunk. Check that the band is secure and not frayed.

### In daily life
It trains the push and reach of getting something away from your body quickly.

### Why it matters
Boxing and other combat sports depend on fast punching. This drill builds speed and endurance with a light load.
