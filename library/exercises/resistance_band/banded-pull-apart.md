The banded pull apart is a band exercise for the upper back and rear shoulders. You hold a resistance band in front of you and pull it apart by moving your hands out to the sides. The rear deltoids, rhomboids and middle trapezius do most of the work, with the rotator cuff helping.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a resistance band in front of you at chest height with your arms straight and hands shoulder-width apart.
2. Brace your abdominals lightly.
3. Pull the band apart by moving your hands out to the sides.
4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end.
5. Slowly bring your hands back together.

**Tips:** Keep your arms level and your shoulders down away from your ears. Do not arch your lower back. Choose a band that lets you move smoothly and hold it firmly so it does not snap back.

### In daily life
It counteracts the hunched, rounded-forward posture that comes from desk work, driving and scrolling on a phone.

### Why it matters
Strengthening the muscles between the shoulder blades can help balance the chest and front of the shoulders, which supports good posture and healthy shoulders. It is also a common warm-up before pressing.
