This is a shoulder exercise with a resistance band. You turn your forearm inward across your belly while your elbow stays at your side. It works the internal rotators, mainly the subscapularis on the front of the shoulder blade, with teres major helping.

### How to do it
1. Attach a resistance band to a fixed point at elbow height.
2. Stand sideways, with the anchor on the same side as your working arm.
3. Hold the band with your elbow tucked against your side and bent to about 90 degrees, forearm pointing forward.
4. Keeping the elbow still, rotate your forearm inward across your belly.
5. Pause, then return slowly to the start.
6. Repeat, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulder down and relaxed, with your elbow pinned to your side. Don't swing your body. Keep the resistance light and the movement smooth.

### In daily life
Reaching behind your back to tuck in a shirt, pulling a door closed towards you and wiping a counter across your body all turn the arm inward.

### Why it matters
Training the internal rotators balances the external rotation exercise, so the front and back of the shoulder stay evenly strong. That supports a stable, well-controlled shoulder in pressing, pulling and overhead activities.
