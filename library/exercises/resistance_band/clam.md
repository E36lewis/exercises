The clam is a side-lying exercise with a resistance band around your thighs. You open your top knee like a clam shell, which works the side of your hip, mainly the gluteus medius, and the small hip rotators.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with a band just above your knees and your knees bent at about 45 degrees.
2. Stack your hips and shoulders and rest your head on your arm.
3. Keep your feet together and brace your abdomen.
4. Lift your top knee as far as you can without rolling your hips back.
5. Pause briefly.
6. Lower slowly and repeat before switching sides.

**Tips:** Do not roll your hips backward. Move only from the hip. Keep the movement small and controlled, and stop if you feel a pinch in the hip.

### In daily life
It strengthens the hip muscles that keep your pelvis level when you walk and stand on one leg to put on a shoe.

### Why it matters
It can help build hip stability, which supports the knees and pelvis.
