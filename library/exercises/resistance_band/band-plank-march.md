The band plank march is a plank with a resistance band around your legs in which you lift one foot at a time. It works the abdominals, shoulders and hips, with the band adding extra work for the outer hips and glutes.

### How to do it
1. Place a resistance band around your thighs, just above your knees, or around your feet.
2. Get into a forearm or high plank, with your body in a straight line from head to heels.
3. Brace your abdominals and squeeze your glutes.
4. Lift one foot a short distance off the floor, keeping your hips level.
5. Place it down and lift the other foot.
6. Continue marching in a steady rhythm.

**Tips:** Do not let your hips sway or sag. Keep your neck neutral. Keep the band tension light, and stop if your lower back starts to ache.

### In daily life
Staying rigid while one foot lifts is like holding yourself steady while you step on a ladder or carry a heavy tray across a room.

### Why it matters
Marching in a plank can help build core and hip stability, and the band helps the glutes switch on, which supports a steady spine and pelvis.
