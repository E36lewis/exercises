Ankle inversion with a resistance band turns the sole of your foot inward against light resistance. It trains the tibialis posterior and tibialis anterior, which support the arch of the foot. It balances the ankle after eversion work.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your legs straight and anchor a resistance band to a fixed object on the outside of your foot.
2. Loop the band around the front of the working foot.
3. Keeping your leg still, turn the sole of your foot inward against the band.
4. Pause at the end of the range.
5. Slowly return. Repeat for all repetitions, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Move at the ankle only. Keep the knee still and the pace slow. Use a light band and train both inversion and eversion so the two sides of the ankle stay balanced.

### In daily life
It helps keep the arch steady when you walk on uneven ground or stand all day.

### Why it matters
Court, field, running, skating, dancing, gymnastics and climbing sports all put the ankle through twisting forces. Balanced strength on both sides of the ankle helps protect it.
