Ankle dorsiflexion with a resistance band pulls your toes and foot up toward your shin against light resistance. It trains the muscle on the front of your shin, the tibialis anterior, which lifts your foot and controls it as it lands.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your legs out in front and anchor a resistance band to a fixed object in front of you.
2. Loop the other end around the top of your foot.
3. Keep your leg still and pull your toes and foot up toward your shin.
4. Pause at the top for a moment.
5. Slowly let your foot move back. Repeat for all repetitions, then switch sides.

**Tips:** Move at the ankle only, keep the knee still, and take the return slowly. Pull the foot straight up and do not let it twist in or out. Use a light band to start.

### In daily life
It helps stop your toes from catching on a curb or stair, and it controls your foot slap when you walk downhill.

### Why it matters
Running, hiking, court and skating sports load the shins and the front of the ankle with every step. Strong shin muscles help prevent shin splints and help control the foot during landings and downhill walking.
