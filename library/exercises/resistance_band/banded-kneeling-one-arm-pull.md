This is a one-arm pull with a resistance band, done from a kneeling position. It works the latissimus dorsi, the back of the shoulder and the biceps, and your trunk works to keep you from twisting.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a band at about chest height.
2. Kneel facing the anchor with a tall posture, and hold the band in one hand with your arm extended.
3. Brace your abdominals and keep your hips level.
4. Pull your elbow back toward your ribs, squeezing your shoulder blade toward your spine.
5. Slowly return your arm forward.
6. Complete your repetitions and switch arms.

**Tips:** Do not twist or lean back. Keep your shoulder down away from your ear. Make sure the band is anchored firmly, and choose a band you can pull without shrugging.

### In daily life
Pulling one arm back is the pattern of starting a lawn mower, pulling a bag toward you or opening a heavy door.

### Why it matters
Kneeling removes your legs from the pull, so your back and trunk have to do the work. That can help build back strength and trunk stability, supporting good posture.
