The band draw hold copies drawing a bow. You hold one end of a resistance band out in front of you and pull the other back toward your shoulder, then hold the position. It trains the back and rear shoulder muscles in a steady hold.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a resistance band at shoulder height and stand side-on to it.
2. Hold the band in the hand nearest the anchor with that arm straight in front of you, and pinch the band with your other hand.
3. Pull the band back by drawing your elbow back and squeezing your shoulder blade toward your spine.
4. Hold the draw for the set time, keeping your shoulders level.
5. Release slowly and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders down and level, and do not twist your trunk. Check the band and anchor for wear. Release the band under control.

### In daily life
It trains the shoulder blade muscles that keep your posture steady when you hold a position.

### Why it matters
Archery and other precision sports hold a draw while aiming. Back strength and shoulder steadiness help you hold that position.
