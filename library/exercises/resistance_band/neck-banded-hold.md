An isometric neck exercise, meaning you hold still against resistance without moving. A resistance band pulls on your head, and the muscles at the back and sides of your neck and upper back work to keep it in place.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a light resistance band at head height.
2. Stand or sit tall, with the band around the back of your head, and step away until there is light tension.
3. Tuck your chin slightly and keep your gaze level.
4. Brace your trunk.
5. Hold your head still against the pull of the band.
6. Release slowly.

**Tips:** Use very light tension; your head should not move. Keep your shoulders relaxed and your breathing steady. Stop if you feel pain, tingling or dizziness.

### In daily life
Holding your head level against a pull is what the neck does when you look at a screen, ride in a bumpy car or carry something on your head or shoulders.

### Why it matters
Stronger neck muscles can support head posture and steadiness, and holding still keeps the load gentle on the joints.
