Finger extensions use a resistance band around the fingers to train opening the hand. They work the finger and wrist extensors on the back of the forearm, which balance the many muscles used to grip.

### How to do it
1. Sit with your forearm resting on a table, palm down, hand over the edge.
2. Place a resistance band or loop around your fingers and thumb.
3. Start with your fingers together and your hand relaxed.
4. Open your fingers against the band.
5. Hold for a moment.
6. Slowly close your fingers again.

**Tips:** Keep your wrist neutral. Use a light band. Stop if you feel pain in your fingers or elbow.

### In daily life
It trains opening your hand to release a grip, as when you let go of a jar lid or put down a bag.

### Why it matters
It can help balance grip strength with the muscles that open the hand, which supports comfortable wrists and elbows, especially for people who climb, lift or type a lot.
