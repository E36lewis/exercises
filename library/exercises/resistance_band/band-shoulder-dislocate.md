The band shoulder dislocate is a mobility exercise. You hold a resistance band with straight arms and move it from in front of your thighs, over your head, to behind your back and back again. It opens the chest and takes the shoulders through their full range.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall holding a long resistance band with a wide overhand grip, arms straight and the band in front of your thighs.
2. Brace your core lightly.
3. Lift the band up and over your head, keeping your arms straight.
4. Continue moving the band behind your back as far as you comfortably can.
5. Reverse the path slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Hold the band wide enough that the movement is easy and smooth, then narrow your grip over time. Do not arch your lower back or force it. Stop if you feel a pinch in the shoulder.

### In daily life
It loosens your shoulders for reaching overhead, getting dressed and fastening a seat belt.

### Why it matters
Swimmers, gymnasts and climbers need a full overhead range at the shoulder. It makes a good warm-up before overhead work.
