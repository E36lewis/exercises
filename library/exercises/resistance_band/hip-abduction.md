Hip abduction with a resistance band moves your leg out to the side against the band. It works the muscles on the outside of the hip, mainly gluteus medius and minimus and the tensor fasciae latae.

### How to do it
1. Loop a resistance band around both legs, just above your knees or around your ankles.
2. Stand tall, holding a wall or chair for balance.
3. Keep your toes pointing forward and your trunk upright.
4. Lift one leg out to the side against the band.
5. Pause when your leg has moved a short way.
6. Lower slowly, keeping some tension in the band.
7. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Don't lean away to cheat. Hold on for balance. Keep the band tight at the bottom.

### In daily life
Stepping sideways out of a car or over a low barrier uses these muscles, and so does keeping your pelvis level while you walk.

### Why it matters
Strong side-hip muscles can help your hips and knees stay in line on every step, so they support walking, running and single-leg balance.
