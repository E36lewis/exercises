Hip adduction with a resistance band pulls your leg across toward the middle, against the band. It works the inner thigh, mainly the adductor group.

### How to do it
1. Attach a band to a low, secure anchor and loop the other end around the ankle nearest it.
2. Stand sideways to the anchor, holding a wall or chair for support.
3. Stand tall, with the working leg slightly out to the side.
4. Pull your working leg across in front of your standing leg.
5. Pause when you feel the inner thigh tighten.
6. Return slowly to the start, keeping tension on the band.
7. Complete your reps, then turn around to train the other leg.

**Tips:** Don't lean away from the anchor. Keep your standing knee soft. Control the return.

### In daily life
Squeezing your knees together to hold a bag, or steadying yourself on a bicycle or horse, uses the inner thigh.

### Why it matters
The adductors balance the outer hip muscles and help stabilise your pelvis when you step. That can help hip stability and support the groin.
