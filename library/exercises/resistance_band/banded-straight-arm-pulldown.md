The banded straight arm pulldown is a home version of the straight arm pulldown. You anchor a band overhead and pull it down to your thighs with straight arms. It trains the lats in the same pattern as the pull of a swimming stroke.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a resistance band high on a door anchor or pull-up bar and stand facing it, a step or two back.
2. Hold the ends of the band with your arms straight and slightly in front of you.
3. Hinge slightly forward and brace your core.
4. Pull the band down to your thighs, keeping your arms almost straight.
5. Let the band rise slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Keep the elbow bend small and fixed. Do not lean back. Check the anchor is secure and the band is not frayed.

### In daily life
It trains the sweeping pull you use to move a load toward your body.

### Why it matters
Swimmers pull with straight arms through the water. This version needs only a band, so it is easy to do at home.
