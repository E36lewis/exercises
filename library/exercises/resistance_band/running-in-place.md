This is a cardio drill performed without moving forward. You lift your knees alternately while a resistance band adds tension around your thighs. It works the hip flexors and the front and back of the thighs, while the glutes keep your pelvis steady.

### How to do it
1. Place a light resistance band around both thighs, just above the knees.
2. Stand tall with your core braced.
3. Lift one knee towards hip height while the opposite arm swings forward.
4. Quickly lower it and lift the other knee.
5. Keep up a steady rhythm on the balls of your feet.
6. Press your knees gently outward against the band and keep them pointing straight ahead.

**Tips:** Stay tall and don't lean back. Land softly through your feet. Keep your knees from caving inward, and slow down if you lose balance.

### In daily life
A brisk walk to the station, taking stairs two at a time and keeping warm at a cold bus stop all use this quick knee lift.

### Why it matters
It raises your heart rate in a small space and builds hip and leg endurance. The band gives your outer hips extra work, which can help them stay stable.
