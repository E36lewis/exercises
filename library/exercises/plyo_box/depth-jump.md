A depth jump starts with a step off a box and ends with a jump straight up as soon as you land. It trains the legs to use the stretch from landing to jump higher, which is called reactive strength. It is an advanced exercise.

### How to do it
1. Place a plyo box at 20 to 40 cm. Start low.
2. Stand on the edge of the box with your toes near the edge.
3. Step off, don't jump, and land on both feet at the same time.
4. As soon as you land, jump as high as you can, spending as little time on the ground as possible.
5. Land softly with bent knees, then step back onto the box and reset.

**Tips:** This is a high-stress drill. Do 3 to 4 sets of 3 to 5 jumps with full rest, and not on tired legs. Only do it after you can land from a box and jump confidently. Never jump off the box; step off. Stop if your landings become heavy.

### In daily life
Bouncing off a curb into a quick sprint, hopping off a step and straight up again all use this stretch and rebound.

### Why it matters
Court, field, collision and snow sports need quick, springy take-offs from the ground. Depth jumps train the legs to react quickly and are a classic tool for jumpers and sprinters.
