The lateral box jump is a jump from beside a box to the top of the box, sideways. You push off from the side of both feet and land on top. It builds sideways power over a safe, low-impact height.

### How to do it
1. Set a stable plyo box at 20 to 40 cm and stand beside it, close to its side.
2. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
3. Swing your arms and bend your knees slightly.
4. Jump up and sideways, and land on top of the box with both feet.
5. Land softly with bent knees, then stand up straight.
6. Step down, don't jump down. Repeat, or turn around and jump the other way.

**Tips:** Do 3 to 4 sets of 3 to 5 jumps on each side with full rest. Start with a low box. Step down every time, because repeated drops onto the floor add impact and risk. Check that the box doesn't slide and wear flat shoes.

### In daily life
Stepping up sideways onto a high curb, hopping onto a platform and moving sideways onto a ledge use this movement.

### Why it matters
Court, snow and skate sports involve pushing sideways with power. The lateral box jump builds that push, with the low landing keeping the load on the joints small.
