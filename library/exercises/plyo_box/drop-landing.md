The drop landing teaches you to land softly and with good alignment. You step off a low box, land on both feet and absorb the impact by bending your hips and knees. It is a core drill for lowering the risk of knee ligament injuries.

### How to do it
1. Set a plyo box at a low height, 20 to 30 cm.
2. Stand on the box with your toes near the edge and feet hip-width apart.
3. Step off, don't jump, and land on both feet at once.
4. Land on the whole foot, bend your hips and knees together and sit back slightly.
5. Keep your knees in line with your toes and don't let them cave in.
6. Hold the landing for two seconds, then stand up and step back onto the box.

**Tips:** Do 3 sets of 5 landings with full rest between sets. Raise the box only when your knees stay in line and the landing is quiet. Look for a soft, silent landing, a straight trunk and even weight on both feet. Stop if your landing becomes noisy or sloppy.

### In daily life
Stepping off a curb you didn't see, hopping off a truck bed or jumping off the bottom step of a ladder all call for this soft landing.

### Why it matters
Court, field, snow, gymnastics and rock-climbing athletes all land from jumps or falls. This drill trains the legs to absorb force with the hips and knees aligned. It is a core part of programs that help prevent ACL injuries.
