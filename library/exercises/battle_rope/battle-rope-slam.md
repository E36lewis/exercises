The battle rope slam lifts a heavy rope overhead with both hands and slams it into the floor in front of you. The whole body works together: the legs and hips power the throw. It is short, intense conditioning.

### How to do it
1. Anchor a battle rope, with equal lengths on each side, and stand facing the anchor with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Hold one end in each hand.
3. Squat slightly and raise both arms overhead, extending your hips.
4. Slam the ropes down to the floor with force, bending your knees and hinging your hips.
5. Let the ropes rebound and lift them again.
6. Keep a steady rhythm.

**Tips:** A usual plan is 8 to 10 rounds of 15 to 20 seconds with 40 seconds' rest. Hinge at the hips, not your back, and don't let your lower back round. Keep the area clear and check that the anchor is secure.

### In daily life
Slamming a heavy mat, tossing a load into a skip and swinging a sledgehammer use the same overhead-to-floor motion.

### Why it matters
Combat, gymnastics and rock-climbing athletes use explosive full-body conditioning. The slam builds power and muscle endurance in short bursts.
