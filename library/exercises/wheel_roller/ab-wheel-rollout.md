The ab wheel rollout is a core exercise in which you roll a small wheel forward on the floor and back again. The rectus abdominis and the deeper trunk muscles work hard to stop your lower back from sagging. This is called anti-extension strength.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on a mat and hold the handles of the wheel with the wheel on the floor under your shoulders.
2. Tuck your pelvis under and brace your abdominals as if you were about to be poked in the stomach.
3. Roll the wheel slowly forward, keeping your arms straight and your hips and shoulders moving together.
4. Go only as far as you can with your back flat.
5. Pull the wheel back toward your knees by tightening your abs, not by pushing your hips back.

**Tips:** Do not let your lower back arch or your hips drop as you roll out. Start with a short range, for example rolling to a wall, and add distance over time. Stop the set when your back starts to sag.

### In daily life
It builds the trunk strength that keeps your back safe when you reach out to lift something heavy or push a loaded cart.

### Why it matters
A strong, braced trunk transfers force between the legs and arms, so this exercise helps in almost every sport. It is especially useful for sports where you must hold your body stiff while moving, such as gymnastics, climbing, swimming and combat sports.
