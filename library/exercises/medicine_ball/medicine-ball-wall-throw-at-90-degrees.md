The medicine ball wall throw at 90 degrees is a fast throwing drill. You stand facing a wall with your arm raised to shoulder height and elbow bent, and throw and catch a light medicine ball against the wall. It trains the rotator cuff to work quickly, as in a baseball throw.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a solid wall about an arm's length away, holding a light medicine ball in one hand.
2. Lift your elbow to shoulder height and bend it to 90 degrees with the ball behind your hand.
3. Throw the ball against the wall with a quick rotation of the forearm.
4. Catch the ball as it bounces back and let your arm absorb it.
5. Immediately throw again for the set number of repetitions, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Use a light ball, about 1 to 2 kg, and a wall that can take the ball. Keep your elbow up and your trunk still. Stop if you feel a pinch in your shoulder.

### In daily life
It trains the quick catch and throw you use in games of catch.

### Why it matters
Baseball and softball pitchers and fielders throw at high speed, and the rotator cuff must handle the load. Fast plyometric work like this prepares the shoulder for it.
