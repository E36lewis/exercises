The reverse throw is a medicine ball throw behind you. You swing a ball between your legs and throw it up and back over your head. It trains the muscles at the back of the shoulder to slow the throwing arm and power the movement.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a medicine ball in both hands in front of you.
2. Bend your knees and hinge your hips, swinging the ball down between your legs.
3. Drive your hips and legs up and swing the ball up over your head.
4. Release the ball up and behind you.
5. Collect the ball, or have a partner retrieve it, and repeat.

**Tips:** Check that the space behind you is clear. Use a light ball and a soft landing area. Start the throw from the legs and hips, and do not arch your lower back.

### In daily life
It trains the lift and throw of tossing a bag over your shoulder.

### Why it matters
Racket and bat sports rely on slowing the arm after a swing. The reverse throw trains the back of the shoulder and hips to produce and control that motion.
