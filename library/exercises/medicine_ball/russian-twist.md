This is a seated rotating core exercise with a medicine ball. You lean back and swing the ball from beside one hip to beside the other. It mainly works the obliques, the muscles at the sides of your waist, with the hip flexors and deep trunk muscles helping.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent and your heels on the ground.
2. Hold a medicine ball in both hands in front of your chest.
3. Lean back until you feel your abs working, keeping your back straight.
4. Rotate your torso to one side, moving the ball beside your hip.
5. Return through the centre and rotate to the other side.
6. Keep the pace steady.

**Tips:** Turn your ribs and chest with the ball, not only your arms. Don't hunch your back. Use a ball you can control, and keep your feet down if you feel strain.

### In daily life
Passing a box to someone beside you, turning with a child in your arms and throwing a ball sideways all use rotation like this.

### Why it matters
Moving a ball from side to side trains you to control rotating force. That supports trunk stability in sports and in everyday twisting movements.
