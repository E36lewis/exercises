The cross hop is a jumping drill in which you hop diagonally across a line while holding a medicine ball. It trains the thighs and glutes to push off, while the hips and trunk keep you steady on each landing.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg beside a line on the floor, holding the medicine ball at your chest.
2. Bend your knee a little and sit back into your hip.
3. Push off your standing foot and hop diagonally forward across the line.
4. Land softly on your other foot, bending your knee and hip to absorb the impact.
5. Keep your chest up and the ball close, and pause to steady yourself.
6. Hop back or carry on across the line, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Land quietly, with your knee over your foot and not caving inwards. Keep the hops small until you can control the landing. Use a non-slip surface.

### In daily life
It copies hopping over a puddle or a kerb, where you leave the ground on one foot and must land on the other without wobbling.

### Why it matters
It can help build leg power and single-leg stability, which supports quick changes of direction in sport.
