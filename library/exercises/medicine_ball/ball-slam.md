The ball slam is a full-body power exercise: you lift a slam ball overhead and throw it hard into the floor. It works the shoulders, lats, abdominals and legs in one fast movement.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a slam ball with both hands.
2. Raise the ball overhead, rising onto your toes and reaching tall.
3. Brace your abdominals.
4. Throw the ball down with force in front of your feet, bending at your hips and knees.
5. Catch it on the bounce or pick it up with a flat back.
6. Reset and repeat.

**Tips:** Use a ball made for slamming, as other balls may bounce back hard. Keep your back flat when picking it up. Make sure there is clear space around you.

### In daily life
The overhead-to-floor drive is the same motion as chopping wood, driving in a tent stake or slamming a stuck lid shut.

### Why it matters
Throwing with maximum effort for short bursts can help build power and conditioning, and many people find it a good way to burn off a stressful day.
