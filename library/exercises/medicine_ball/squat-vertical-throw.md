The squat vertical throw is a medicine ball power exercise. You squat down, then drive upward and throw the ball straight overhead. The legs and hips supply most of the power, and the shoulders and arms finish the throw.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a medicine ball at your chest.
2. Squat down with your chest up and the ball close to you.
3. Drive up fast through your legs.
4. Extend your hips and knees fully, then release the ball straight up above you.
5. Step aside and let it land, or catch it with soft knees and absorb it into the next squat.
6. Reset, then repeat.

**Tips:** Throw the ball straight up, not forward, and check that nothing is overhead. Pick a ball light enough to throw quickly. Do not throw if your wrists or shoulders hurt, and keep a firm grip with fingers spread behind the ball.

### In daily life
It copies the explosive lift you use to heave a bag onto a high shelf or toss something up to a person standing on a ladder.

### Why it matters
Throwing a ball upward teaches the legs and arms to produce force together and quickly, which can help build explosive power.
