The Ali shuffle is a ladder drill named after Muhammad Ali's footwork. You alternate in and out of each square with quick, scissor-like steps. It trains the fast feet of a boxer.

### How to do it
1. Lay a flat agility ladder on the floor.
2. Stand at one end facing the ladder, on the balls of your feet.
3. Step into the first square with your left foot, then your right.
4. Step out to the side with your left foot, then your right, so you move forward one square.
5. Keep going down the ladder with quick, scissor-like steps.
6. Keep your hands up as if guarding, and your head up.

**Tips:** Start slowly and speed up as the steps become clean. Do 4 to 6 passes, or 15 to 20 seconds of work with 40 seconds' rest. Stay light, never stamping, and keep your eyes up.

### In daily life
Quick, light steps to weave through a crowd or to keep your balance on slippery ground use similar footwork.

### Why it matters
Boxers and other combat athletes depend on quick, balanced feet. The Ali shuffle trains the rhythm and speed of that footwork.
