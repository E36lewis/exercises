The ladder in and out drill moves both feet into a square and then out to the sides as you travel along the ladder. It trains quick, rhythmic foot contacts with a sideways emphasis.

### How to do it
1. Lay a flat agility ladder on the floor. A 4 to 5 m ladder is typical.
2. Stand at one end facing along the ladder, with both feet outside the first square.
3. Step both feet into the first square one after the other.
4. Step back out to the sides, one foot at a time, so you are outside the square again, one square further on.
5. Repeat down the ladder, keeping a steady beat.
6. Walk back and go again.

**Tips:** Take quick, small steps on the balls of your feet and keep your head up. Do 3 to 5 passes. Add speed only when each step is clean. For intervals, use 20 seconds of work and 40 seconds of rest for 4 to 6 rounds. Keep the floor clear.

### In daily life
Stepping quickly through a gate, hopping through a crowd or navigating a busy hallway uses fast, small steps like these.

### Why it matters
Court, field and racket sports rely on lateral foot speed to get to the ball or react to an opponent. This drill builds that speed and foot coordination.
