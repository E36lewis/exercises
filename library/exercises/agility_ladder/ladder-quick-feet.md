Quick feet is a drill in which you run through each square of an agility ladder, placing one foot in each square and moving as fast as you can control. It trains fast foot contacts.

### How to do it
1. Lay an agility ladder flat on the floor.
2. Stand at the start facing along the ladder, on the balls of your feet.
3. Run forward with small steps, placing one foot in each square.
4. Keep your knees up slightly, your arms bent and swinging, and your head up.
5. Go the whole length, then jog or walk back.
6. Repeat, increasing speed each time while keeping steps clean.

**Tips:** Do 4 to 6 passes, or work for 10 to 20 seconds and rest 30 to 40 seconds. Land softly and keep your feet inside the squares. Quality counts more than speed, so slow down when you start to catch the ladder.

### In daily life
Running up a flight of stairs two at a time, dodging a puddle and any quick jog on uneven ground need fast foot contacts.

### Why it matters
Court, field, combat and racket sports all depend on the feet making many fast contacts. Quick feet trains that skill, and it also works as a warm-up before sprints and cutting drills.
