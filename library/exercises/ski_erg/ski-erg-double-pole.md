The ski erg double pole copies the double-poling of cross-country skiing. You pull both handles down together and fold through the hips. It conditions the back, arms and trunk.

### How to do it
1. Set the damper to 4 to 6 and stand facing the machine, with a handle in each hand.
2. Reach up with both arms and rise slightly onto your toes.
3. Pull the handles down toward your thighs while hinging at the hips and bending your knees.
4. Finish with your arms by your thighs and your trunk folded.
5. Return up smoothly with your arms and hips and repeat.

**Tips:** A usual session is 20 to 40 minutes at an easy pace, or 5 x 4 minutes hard with 2 minutes easy. Aim for 30 to 40 strokes per minute. Keep your elbows soft and your back flat, and keep the handles under control as they return.

### In daily life
Pulling a heavy rope down, closing a roll-up garage door and pushing a heavy cart with both arms use the same downward drive.

### Why it matters
Cross-country skiers drive with the arms, back and trunk on every stride. This trains that movement and its endurance directly, indoors and all year.
