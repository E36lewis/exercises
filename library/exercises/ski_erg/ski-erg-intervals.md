Ski erg intervals alternate hard pulling on a ski erg with easy recovery. You pull two handles down in a double-pole movement while folding at the hips. It conditions the whole body, mostly the back, arms and trunk.

### How to do it
1. Set the damper to 4 to 6 and stand facing the machine with the handles in your hands.
2. Start with your arms up and your knees slightly bent.
3. Pull the handles down toward your thighs as you hinge at the hips and bend your knees.
4. Let your arms and trunk finish with a slight crunch.
5. Return to the top by straightening your trunk and reaching up.
6. Repeat in a steady rhythm.

**Tips:** A usual plan is 8 to 10 rounds of 30 seconds hard and 30 seconds easy, or 6 x 500 m with 1 minute's rest. Keep your arms fairly straight at the start and use your hips, not just your arms. Hold the handles firmly to prevent them swinging back.

### In daily life
Pulling down a rope, hauling in a line or pushing a heavy door shut use the same downward pull.

### Why it matters
Snow and water sports use the double-pole and pull. The ski erg trains the muscle endurance and the heart and lungs for cross-country skiing and paddling.
