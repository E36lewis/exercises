From a plank with straight arms, you let your chest sink between your shoulder blades, then push the floor away. Only the shoulder blades move. It trains the serratus anterior to control the shoulder blade.

### How to do it
1. Start in a high plank with your arms straight and your hands under your shoulders.
2. Keep your elbows locked.
3. Let your chest sink down as your shoulder blades pull together.
4. Push the floor away until your shoulder blades spread and your upper back rounds slightly.
5. Pause for a moment.
6. Repeat in a smooth rhythm.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows straight the whole time. Move only through the shoulder blades. Don't let your hips sag. Do it on your knees to start.

### In daily life
It is the shoulder motion of pushing a heavy cart or reaching forward for something far away.

### Why it matters
A shoulder blade that moves well and stays steady is the base for all pushing, pulling and overhead work. It is useful in every sport that uses the arms, so it is recommended for everyone.
