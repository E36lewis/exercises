The lateral leg swing is a dynamic warm-up in which you swing a leg from side to side across your body. It warms and loosens the inner thighs and the hip joint.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a wall or post, with both hands on it for support.
2. Shift your weight onto one leg with a soft knee.
3. Swing the other leg out to the side, then across the front of the standing leg.
4. Keep the swing smooth, and let it rise a little each time.
5. Do 10 to 15 swings, then change legs.

**Tips:** Keep your hips square to the wall and don't twist. Stand tall and don't lean. Let the leg swing freely without forcing the end range.

### In daily life
Stepping sideways out of a car or over a low barrier takes the same side range of the hip.

### Why it matters
It prepares the adductors and the hips for lateral cutting, kicks and wide stances in field, court, combat and skating sports. It can help guard against groin strains.
