The levator scapulae stretch eases the muscle running from the neck to the top of the shoulder blade. It relieves the tight side of the neck that comes from sitting or driving.

### How to do it
1. Sit tall on a chair, with one hand holding the seat for an anchor.
2. Turn your head about 45 degrees toward the opposite shoulder, as if you were looking into your armpit.
3. Tilt your chin down.
4. Place the other hand on the back of your head and add a gentle pull.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then change sides.

**Tips:** Pull softly. Keep the shoulder you hold down. Stop at any tingling in the arm.

### In daily life
Holding a phone or a steering wheel can tighten this muscle.

### Why it matters
Cyclists, wheelchair users, shooters and riders keep the head forward for long periods. The stretch eases a stiff neck and helps looking over the shoulder.
