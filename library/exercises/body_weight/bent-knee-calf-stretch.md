The bent knee calf stretch lengthens the soleus, the deep calf muscle under the gastrocnemius. Bending the knee lets the stretch reach the lower calf and the Achilles.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a wall with your hands on it.
2. Step one foot back a short way, heel down and toes forward.
3. Bend both knees and shift your hips toward the wall until you feel a stretch low in the back calf.
4. Keep the back heel down.
5. Hold for 30 seconds, then change sides.

**Tips:** Keep the heel down and the foot pointing forward. Do not bounce. The pull is felt lower in the calf than the straight-leg stretch.

### In daily life
Tight calves make squatting and walking uphill feel stiff.

### Why it matters
Runners, skiers, court and field players need ankle bend. A supple soleus supports landing from jumps and may ease Achilles strain.
