A squat in which you lean back with your knees traveling far forward and your heels lifted. It puts a strong stretch and load on the quadriceps. It is an advanced, optional exercise.

### How to do it
1. Stand holding a post or rail with one hand and your feet hip-width apart.
2. Rise onto the balls of your feet.
3. Bend your knees forward and lean your torso back in one straight line.
4. Lower as far as you can control, keeping your hips forward.
5. Squeeze your thighs to push back up.
6. Repeat slowly.

**Tips:** Hold a rail for balance. Go only as deep as you can without knee pain. Do it only if your knees are healthy and you have built up strength. Stop at once if the front of the knee hurts.

### In daily life
It trains the thighs through a deep knee bend, as in kneeling down.

### Why it matters
Snow and court sports need strong quadriceps for deep knee bends and landings. This is an optional move for building them.
