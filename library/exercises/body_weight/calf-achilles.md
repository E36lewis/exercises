The calf/Achilles stretch lengthens the calf muscles and the Achilles tendon, the thick cord at the back of the ankle. It is often done on a step or against a wall.

### How to do it
1. Stand on the edge of a step, holding a rail for balance, with the balls of your feet on the step.
2. Let your heels drop below the step, or step one foot back if you are using a wall.
3. Keep your leg straight to feel the stretch high in the calf.
4. Bend your knee slightly to feel it lower, near the tendon.
5. Hold for about 20 to 30 seconds.
6. Rise back up and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your heel on the floor and your foot straight. Do not bounce. Ease off if you feel pain near the tendon, especially if you have had an Achilles injury.

### In daily life
It can ease tight calves after a day in heels or a long walk.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the ankle mobile and the calf-tendon unit supple, which supports running and walking.
