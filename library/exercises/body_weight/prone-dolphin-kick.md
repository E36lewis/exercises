You lie face down and lift and lower your legs together in a wave from your hips. It copies the dolphin kick of swimming on dry land.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your arms stretched overhead and your legs together.
2. Squeeze your glutes and lift your legs a few inches.
3. Lower them while your chest lifts slightly.
4. Make a smooth wave through your hips and trunk.
5. Repeat rhythmically.
6. Rest by lying flat.

**Tips:** Keep the motion small and smooth. Keep your neck long. Stop if your lower back pinches.

### In daily life
These muscles help you straighten your hips and back when you stand up or lift from a low position.

### Why it matters
Swimmers use a dolphin kick for starts, turns and butterfly. The dry-land drill builds the pattern and the back and glute endurance.
