The V-up is a body-weight core exercise in which you fold your body into a V by lifting your straight legs and torso toward each other. It mainly works the rectus abdominis (the "six-pack" muscle), with the hip flexors and obliques helping.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your legs straight and your arms stretched overhead.
2. Brace your abs and press your lower back toward the floor.
3. Lift your legs and your upper body at the same time, reaching your hands toward your toes.
4. Balance briefly on your sit bones, with your body in a V shape.
5. Lower your arms and legs slowly with control, without letting them drop.

**Tips:** Keep your movement smooth rather than swinging. Bend your knees a little, or lift one leg at a time, if the full version is too hard. Stop if your lower back arches or your neck strains.

### In daily life
It builds the abdominal strength for sitting up straight from a flat position and for bringing your legs up at the same time, as when you get into a car or onto a high bed.

### Why it matters
The V-up links the abs and hip flexors in one movement, which builds the trunk strength that supports good posture and body control.
