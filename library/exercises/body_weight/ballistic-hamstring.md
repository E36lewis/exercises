The ballistic hamstring stretch uses a gentle, controlled swing of the leg to reach the back of the thigh. It targets the hamstrings, with the hip flexors and thigh muscles working to swing the leg.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall next to a wall or support and hold on with one hand.
2. Shift your weight onto one leg.
3. Swing your other leg forward, keeping it fairly straight, to a comfortable height.
4. Let it swing back down and slightly behind you.
5. Repeat the swing, gradually letting it go a little higher.
6. Switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep the swing smooth and controlled and your trunk upright. Never force or bounce into pain. Warm up first, as ballistic stretching increases the risk of pulled muscles if done cold.

### In daily life
Supple hamstrings help you bend forward to tie shoes, reach for a dropped key and take long strides up a hill.

### Why it matters
Swinging the leg through a growing range can prepare the hamstrings for kicking or sprinting, and supports hip mobility. Skip it if you have had a recent hamstring strain.
