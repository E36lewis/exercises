The wide chest stretch opens the chest and the front of the shoulders by holding your arms out wide and pressing them slightly back. It stretches the pectoral muscles, the front of the deltoid, and the biceps when your elbows are straight.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your ribs down.
2. Lift your arms out to the sides at about shoulder height, with straight elbows.
3. Turn your palms forward, or thumbs up and back, to open the chest.
4. Gently press your arms slightly back until you feel a stretch across your chest.
5. Hold for several slow breaths, then relax.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders away from your ears and your neck relaxed. Do not push your ribs forward or arch your lower back. Reduce how far back you press if you feel pinching in the front of the shoulder.

### In daily life
It counters the rounded shoulders that come from typing, driving or looking at a phone, and makes it easier to reach out wide, as when you open a large door.

### Why it matters
A stretch across the front of the chest can help you keep your shoulders back and your posture upright after long periods hunched forward.
