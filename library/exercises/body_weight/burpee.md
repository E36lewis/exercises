The burpee is a whole-body conditioning move. You drop into a squat, kick back to a plank, jump the feet in and then jump up, working the legs, chest, shoulders and trunk together.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Squat and place your hands on the floor.
3. Kick your feet back into a plank, with a straight line from head to heels.
4. Lower the chest to the floor or do a push-up if you want a harder version.
5. Jump the feet back to your hands.
6. Explode upward into a jump with your hands overhead.
7. Work 30 to 45 seconds on and 15 to 30 seconds off, for 4 to 6 rounds, or 10 to 20 reps.

**Tips:** Keep your trunk braced in the plank so the hips don't sag. Land softly from the jump. Step rather than jump if you need to lower the impact.

### In daily life
Getting down to the floor and back up quickly, as with a child, is the pattern.

### Why it matters
Fighters, runners and court players use burpees to build stamina and the ability to get up and go again after being on the floor.
