Arm circles are a simple mobility drill in which you draw circles in the air with your outstretched arms. They warm up the shoulder joint and the muscles around the shoulder blades, led by the deltoids.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Raise both arms out to the sides at shoulder height, palms down.
3. Make small circles, moving from the shoulder.
4. Gradually make the circles bigger.
5. After several circles, reverse the direction.
6. Lower your arms and shake them out.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders down away from your ears and your chest lifted. Do not shrug. Move smoothly and stop if anything pinches. A light, relaxed tempo is better than speed.

### In daily life
Loose shoulders make it easier to reach behind to pull a seatbelt, wash your back or put on a coat.

### Why it matters
Moving the joint through its range with light effort can help raise warmth in the shoulders before throwing, swimming or lifting overhead, and supports a comfortable range of motion.
