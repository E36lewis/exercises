The half split is a kneeling stretch with one leg extended in front. It lengthens the back of the thigh and builds hamstring flexibility.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on one knee with the other leg straight in front, heel on the floor and toes up.
2. Stack the back knee under the hip.
3. Place your hands on the floor on each side of the front leg.
4. Push your hips back toward your back heel, keeping the back long.
5. Fold forward a little until you feel a pull behind the thigh.
6. Hold for 30 to 60 seconds, then change legs.

**Tips:** Hinge from the hips and don't round the back. Keep the front leg straight but not locked. Pad the kneeling knee.

### In daily life
Flexible hamstrings let you bend to tie a shoe without rounding the back.

### Why it matters
Dancers, gymnasts, climbers and fighters kick and reach high. Open hamstrings support those ranges and may reduce strains.
