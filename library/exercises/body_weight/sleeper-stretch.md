The sleeper stretch is done lying on your side. You turn your forearm toward the floor to stretch the back of the shoulder, mainly the rotator cuff muscles that slow the arm in throwing.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with the shoulder to stretch underneath, the upper arm straight out from your body at a right angle.
2. Bend that elbow to 90 degrees, with the forearm pointing to the ceiling.
3. Use your top hand to press the wrist gently toward the floor.
4. Stop when you feel a stretch at the back of the shoulder.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then change sides.

**Tips:** Keep the pull gentle. A strong stretch can pinch the front of the shoulder. Keep your shoulder blade flat and do not roll forward. Stop if you feel a pinch.

### In daily life
It helps with sleeping on your side and reaching behind to fasten a seat belt or a bra strap.

### Why it matters
Throwers, batters, swimmers and racket players often lose rotation in the back of the shoulder. A looser shoulder helps keep range for the arm and may protect it.
