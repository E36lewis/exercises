The bodyweight hyperextension lifts your upper body while you lie face down on an angled bench or machine. It works the muscles along your spine and the glutes and hamstrings.

### How to do it
1. Set the pad so it sits just below your hip bones and hook your heels under the roller.
2. Lie face down and cross your arms over your chest or hold them by your temples.
3. Keeping your back flat, lower your torso toward the floor from the hips.
4. Lift your torso until your body is in a straight line from head to heels.
5. Squeeze your glutes at the top, and stop there.
6. Lower under control and repeat.

**Tips:** Don't lift above a straight line or overarch. Move slowly. Stop if you feel any back pain.

### In daily life
Straightening up after bending to tie a shoe or work in the garden uses the same spine and hip extensors.

### Why it matters
It builds endurance in the muscles that hold your back upright, which can help with posture and with bending and lifting safely.
