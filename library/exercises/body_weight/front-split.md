The front split places one leg forward and one back to open the hamstrings of the front leg and the hip flexors of the back leg. It is an advanced flexibility goal.

### How to do it
1. Warm up with a lunge and a few leg swings.
2. Start in a low lunge with your hands on the floor or on blocks.
3. Slide the front heel forward and the back knee back, keeping the hips square.
4. Lower as far as is comfortable, using your hands or blocks for support.
5. Hold for 30 to 60 seconds, breathing slowly.
6. Come out slowly and change legs.

**Tips:** Never bounce. Keep the hips square and the back long. Work up gradually over weeks, and stay at a stretch you can breathe through.

### In daily life
The range is useful for deep lunges and long strides on uneven ground.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, dancers, martial artists and climbers value a deep split for kicks, high steps and positions.
