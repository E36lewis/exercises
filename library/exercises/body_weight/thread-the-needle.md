Thread the needle is a stretch on hands and knees. One arm slides under your body, turning the upper back and opening the shoulder blade area.

### How to do it
1. Start on hands and knees, hands under the shoulders and knees under the hips.
2. Lift one hand and slide that arm along the floor under your chest, palm up.
3. Lower the shoulder and the side of your head to the floor.
4. Hold and breathe for 20 to 30 seconds.
5. Unwind by pressing back through the support hand and reaching that arm up toward the ceiling.
6. Do 6 to 8 reps, or hold, then change sides.

**Tips:** Keep the hips above the knees and move slowly. Stop if you feel a pinch in the shoulder. Don't push hard into the neck.

### In daily life
Reaching across a desk or a bench, and twisting to look back, uses the same movement.

### Why it matters
It frees the upper back for rotating sports and for overhead reaching, which is useful in racket, bat, water and climbing sports.
