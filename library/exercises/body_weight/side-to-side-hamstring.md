This is a seated stretch with your legs wide, reaching towards one foot and then the other. It stretches the back of your thighs, and your inner thighs and glutes feel it too.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your legs open in a wide V, knees pointing up.
2. Sit tall, with your hands on the floor beside you.
3. Turn your chest towards your right leg.
4. Hinge forward from your hips and reach your hands along your leg.
5. Hold when you feel a stretch behind your thigh, then sit tall again.
6. Repeat towards the left leg.

**Tips:** Lead with your chest, not your head, and keep your back long. Don't bounce. Bend your knees a little or sit on a folded towel if your back rounds.

### In daily life
You can reach sideways towards a shoe or a dropped item without feeling a tug behind your leg.

### Why it matters
Working each leg in turn can help you notice differences between sides. Longer hamstrings can make hip hinges, such as bending to lift, feel easier.
