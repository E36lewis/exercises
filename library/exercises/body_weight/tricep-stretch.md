The tricep stretch is a standing or seated stretch for the back of the upper arm. Bending the elbow fully and lifting the arm overhead lengthens the triceps, including the long head that crosses the shoulder.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall with your ribs down and your neck relaxed.
2. Raise one arm straight up, then bend the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, between your shoulder blades.
3. Take hold of that elbow with your other hand.
4. Gently guide the elbow back and slightly toward the other side until you feel a stretch along the back of the arm.
5. Hold for several slow breaths, then switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your head upright rather than pushing it forward. Do not arch your lower back as the arm goes back. Use a gentle pull and ease off if your shoulder feels pinched.

### In daily life
It eases the arm after typing, painting a ceiling or carrying with bent elbows, and makes it easier to scratch your back or reach a zip between your shoulder blades.

### Why it matters
Stretching the triceps after pressing work can help you keep a full range of motion in the elbow and shoulder, so overhead positions stay comfortable.
