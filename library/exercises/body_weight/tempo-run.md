A tempo run is a steady run at a comfortably hard pace, near the point where breathing gets heavy but you can keep going. It builds your aerobic endurance.

### How to do it
1. Warm up with 10 minutes of easy running.
2. Settle into a pace you could hold for about an hour, hard but controlled.
3. Hold that pace for 20 to 30 minutes, or 3 x 10 minutes with 2 minutes of jogging between.
4. Keep your breath rhythmic and your form relaxed.
5. Cool down with 10 minutes of easy running.

**Tips:** Hold an even pace. Don't start too fast. You should be able to speak only a few words.

### In daily life
Steady aerobic fitness makes brisk walks, hills and stairs easier.

### Why it matters
Distance runners use tempo runs to raise the pace they can maintain in races, and field players use them for their base fitness.
