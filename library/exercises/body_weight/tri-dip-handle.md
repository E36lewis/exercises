The tri dip handle is a body-weight dip done between two parallel handles, so you hold your whole weight on straight arms and lower yourself between them. It mainly works the triceps at the back of the upper arm, with the chest, shoulders and upper back helping.

### How to do it
1. Take a handle in each hand, palms facing in, and press up until your arms are straight.
2. Keep your chest lifted, your shoulders down, and your legs bent behind you or crossed at the ankles.
3. Bend your elbows slowly and let your body sink between the handles.
4. Lower until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor.
5. Press through your palms until your arms are straight again, without shrugging.

**Tips:** Keep your elbows close to your sides to keep the work on the triceps. Stop lowering earlier if the front of your shoulder feels strained. Make sure the handles are fixed and stable before you put your weight on them.

### In daily life
This is the same push you use to lift yourself out of a swimming pool or onto a high ledge using your arms alone.

### Why it matters
Moving your full body weight on parallel handles builds triceps strength without equipment beyond the handles, and it trains the shoulder blades to stay steady while the arms push.
