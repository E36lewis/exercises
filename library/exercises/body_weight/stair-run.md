A stair run is a fast climb of a staircase or a stadium step. It is hill work for towns, building leg power and heart and lung fitness.

### How to do it
1. Pick a safe, dry staircase of 10 to 30 steps with a handrail close by.
2. Warm up with an easy walk and a few steps.
3. Run up on the balls of your feet, one step at a time or two at a time for more power.
4. Lean slightly forward and pump your arms.
5. Walk down carefully to recover.
6. Do 6 to 10 climbs, or 20 to 30 second efforts.

**Tips:** Walk down, never run down. Land the whole forefoot on each step. Keep your hand near the rail on a slippery day.

### In daily life
Climbing stairs is part of everyday life and gets easier.

### Why it matters
Runners use stairs for hill-like power and fitness where there are no hills.
