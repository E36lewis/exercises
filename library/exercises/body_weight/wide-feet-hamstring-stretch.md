The wide feet hamstring stretch is a standing forward fold with your feet set wide apart. It stretches the hamstrings at the back of the thighs and the adductors on the inner thighs, and the calves and glutes also feel it.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet about a leg's length apart, toes pointing forward or slightly in.
2. Place your hands on your hips and lift your chest.
3. Hinge forward at the hips with a long spine, keeping your knees straight but not locked.
4. Lower your hands toward the floor, or rest them on your shins or a block.
5. Hold for several slow breaths, letting your head hang.
6. Press into your feet and rise slowly, leading with your chest.

**Tips:** Fold from your hips rather than rounding your lower back. Keep your weight over the middle of your feet. Do not bounce, and soften your knees slightly if the stretch is too intense.

### In daily life
It helps when you bend to pick something off the floor or reach into a low cupboard.

### Why it matters
Longer hamstrings and more flexible inner thighs can help you bend forward and spread your legs with less strain on your lower back.
