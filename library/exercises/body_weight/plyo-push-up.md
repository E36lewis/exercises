A push-up in which you push so hard that your hands leave the floor. It builds explosive upper-body power.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position with your hands slightly wider than your shoulders.
2. Lower your chest toward the floor under control.
3. Push up as fast as you can so your hands leave the floor.
4. Land with soft, slightly bent elbows.
5. Go straight into the next rep.
6. Stop the set when the push slows.

**Tips:** Do it only after you can do many clean push-ups. Land with bent elbows, never locked. Keep your body straight. Start with a small hop, or push from your knees. Stop if your wrists or shoulders hurt.

### In daily life
It's the quick push you use to shove yourself up fast after a fall.

### Why it matters
Combat, collision and court sports need fast pushes, such as striking, shoving a blocker or pushing off. Plyometric push-ups train the arms to produce force quickly.
