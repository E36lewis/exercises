The mirror drill is a reactive agility game with a partner. One person leads with side-to-side and forward and back movements, and the other copies them as if they were a mirror.

### How to do it
1. Mark a space about 3 to 5 meters (about 3 to 5 yards) wide and face your partner about 2 meters apart.
2. The leader moves in any direction, quickly.
3. The follower mirrors each move and tries to stay at the same distance.
4. Keep a low athletic stance and eyes on your partner's hips.
5. Work for 15 to 30 seconds and swap roles. Do 4 to 6 rounds each.

**Tips:** Stay low and on the balls of your feet. Watch the hips, not the feet. Make the leader's moves sharp, without deception that risks a fall.

### In daily life
Dodging someone coming toward you in a hall is a mirror in miniature.

### Why it matters
Racket, combat and court sports reward reaction and short bursts of movement. The drill trains the reaction needed to follow an opponent.
