The B skip adds a leg extension to the A skip. After the knee lifts, the lower leg kicks forward and then paws back down. It trains the hamstrings to pull the foot under the body.

### How to do it
1. Mark 20 to 30 meters (about 20 to 30 yards) of flat ground.
2. Skip forward as in an A skip, lifting one knee to hip height.
3. Extend the lower leg forward from the knee.
4. Pull the foot down and back under the hips with a sweeping action, striking the ground with the ball of the foot.
5. Alternate legs with a skip hop.
6. Walk back to recover. Do 3 to 5 passes.

**Tips:** Keep your trunk tall and your hips forward. Do not swing the foot out far. The pull-down comes from the hamstrings.

### In daily life
The pulling motion is the same as the downward step that follows a stride.

### Why it matters
It teaches the hamstring pull in sprinting for run and field sports and may help build resilience in the muscle that is often strained at speed.
