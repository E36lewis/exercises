The movement a surfer uses to jump from lying on the board to standing. You press up from the chest, then jump your feet under you into a low stance. You can practice it on the floor.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your hands under your chest and your feet apart.
2. Push up quickly to straighten your arms, lifting your chest.
3. Bring your back foot up under your hips and your front foot between your hands as you jump.
4. Land in a low, wide surf stance with your knees bent and your arms out.
5. Stand tall, then lie back down.
6. Practise with the other foot forward too.

**Tips:** Land softly on a mat or sand, not a hard floor. Keep your eyes forward, not down. Start slowly and speed up as your technique improves. Learn it dry before you try it on a board.

### In daily life
It resembles getting up fast from the floor, a move that helps you recover from a fall.

### Why it matters
Surfers need to stand within a second to catch a wave. Practising on land builds the arm push, leg drive and timing, so you waste less energy and stay on the wave.
