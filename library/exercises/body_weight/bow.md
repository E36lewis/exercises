The bow is a stretch based on the yoga bow pose. You lie face down, hold your ankles and lift your chest and thighs. It stretches the front of the thighs, the hip flexors, the abdomen and the chest and shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down on a mat with your arms by your sides.
2. Bend your knees and reach back to hold your ankles or the tops of your feet.
3. Breathe in and press your feet into your hands to lift your chest and thighs off the floor.
4. Look forward and keep your neck long.
5. Hold for a few breaths, rocking gently if it feels comfortable.
6. Lower slowly and rest.

**Tips:** Do not force your range; loop a strap around your ankles if you cannot reach them. Keep your shoulders away from your ears. Skip or ease off if you feel pain in the lower back or knees.

### In daily life
It can undo the folded, forward-leaning shape of sitting at a desk or driving.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the front of the body mobile, which supports standing tall and a more open chest.
