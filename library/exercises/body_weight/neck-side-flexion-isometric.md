A hold where you push the side of your head into your hand and the neck doesn't move. It strengthens the muscles at the sides of the neck.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall and look straight ahead.
2. Place your right palm against the side of your head, above the ear.
3. Push your head sideways into your hand while your hand resists.
4. Build the effort to about half of your strength over two seconds.
5. Hold for about ten seconds, breathing normally.
6. Relax, then repeat on the left side.

**Tips:** Don't tilt your head or shrug your shoulder. Keep the push gentle. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or tingling in your arm.

### In daily life
These muscles keep your head level when you cradle a phone against your shoulder or take a sharp turn on a bike.

### Why it matters
Collision, combat, wheeled, mounted and water sports can jolt the head sideways. Strong side neck muscles help the head stay steady.
