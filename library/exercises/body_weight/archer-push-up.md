A wide push-up in which you press mainly with one arm while the other arm stays straight out to the side. It is a step toward the one-arm push-up.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position with your hands much wider than your shoulders.
2. Shift your weight to your right arm and bend it as you lower your chest toward your right hand.
3. Keep your left arm straight and your palm flat.
4. Lower until your chest is just above the floor.
5. Push back up and return to the middle.
6. Repeat on the left side.

**Tips:** Keep your body straight. Go through a smaller range if needed. Start with the straight arm's hand on a raised surface. Stop if you feel shoulder pain.

### In daily life
It builds the one-sided pushing you use to push yourself off the floor with one arm.

### Why it matters
Combat, gymnastics and climbing demand strong, one-arm pushing and pulling. It is a step toward bodyweight single-arm work.
