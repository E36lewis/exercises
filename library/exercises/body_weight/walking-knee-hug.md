The walking knee hug is a dynamic warm-up. With each step you pull one knee to your chest, which stretches the glute and warms the hip.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall.
2. Step forward and lift one knee toward your chest.
3. Hug the shin or knee with both hands and squeeze it close for a second.
4. Rise onto the toes of the standing foot.
5. Lower the foot and step into the next knee hug on the other side.
6. Do 8 to 10 steps per leg over about 10 to 15 meters.

**Tips:** Stand tall as you hug. Don't bend forward at the waist. Balance on the standing foot and keep your pace slow.

### In daily life
Pulling your knee up to tie a shoe is the same position.

### Why it matters
Runners and field players use it before running to open the glutes and the hips, and to practice balance on one leg.
