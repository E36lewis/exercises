The first step of a wrestling takedown. From a low stance you drop your back knee and drive your lead leg forward and deep in a long, low step. It teaches how to change level and close distance fast.

### How to do it
1. Start in a staggered stance with your knees bent, hands up and your weight evenly spread.
2. Lower your hips by bending your knees.
3. Take a long, low step with your lead foot between your opponent's feet.
4. Drop your back knee toward the floor as your lead knee bends.
5. Keep your head up and your back straight.
6. Push off the lead foot to return to the stance.

**Tips:** Step low, not up and over. Keep your back flat and your head up. Practise slowly and on a mat. Don't let the back knee slam into the floor.

### In daily life
It's a deep forward step like the one you take to catch yourself when you stumble.

### Why it matters
In wrestling and other combat sports, the shot is the way to get to the legs before the opponent reacts. Drilling it teaches speed, depth and safe knees.
