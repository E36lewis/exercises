A backpedal is running backward while keeping your eyes forward. It is a defensive footwork drill that trains the quads and the control to retreat quickly.

### How to do it
1. Mark 10 to 20 meters (about 10 to 20 yards) with a cone at each end.
2. Stand in an athletic stance with knees bent and chest slightly over your toes.
3. Push off the front foot and reach back with the opposite foot, staying low.
4. Take short, fast steps and keep your weight on the balls of your feet.
5. Pump your arms as in sprinting.
6. At the far cone, plant and sprint forward. Do 4 to 6 rounds.

**Tips:** Keep your hips low and your chest over your knees. Do not reach with long steps or lean back. Check behind you before you start.

### In daily life
Walking backward out of a tight space or stepping back from a crowd uses the same controlled retreat.

### Why it matters
Defensive backs, goalkeepers, basketball and tennis players backpedal to stay in front of the play. It builds the quads and the balance to change direction at once.
