The twisting spine is a seated stretch that turns your upper body over a bent knee. It gently stretches the muscles along the spine, the glutes and the outer hip, and it opens the chest as you turn.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with both legs straight in front of you and your back tall.
2. Bend your right knee and place your right foot flat on the floor outside your left thigh.
3. Hook your left elbow outside your right knee, or place your left hand on it.
4. Place your right hand on the floor behind you and sit up taller.
5. Turn your chest and head to the right, using your elbow against the knee to deepen the twist a little.
6. Hold for several slow breaths, then unwind and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Lengthen your spine before each turn and rotate from your ribs rather than your lower back. Keep both sit bones on the floor. Ease off if you feel pinching or sharp pain.

### In daily life
It helps you turn to look behind you when reversing a car or reaching for a seat belt, and eases stiffness after long periods of sitting.

### Why it matters
Twisting the spine through a gentle range of motion can help keep your back and hips mobile, which makes everyday turning easier.
