Fartlek, Swedish for speed play, is a run in which you mix fast and easy stretches without a fixed plan. You pick landmarks and surge to them, then ease off.

### How to do it
1. Warm up with 10 minutes of easy running.
2. Run for 20 to 40 minutes on a road, trail or park.
3. Surge hard to a landmark, such as the next lamp post or the next tree.
4. Return to an easy jog until you feel recovered.
5. Repeat with surges of different lengths, from 20 seconds to 3 minutes.
6. Cool down with an easy jog.

**Tips:** Let the effort follow how you feel. Keep the easy parts truly easy. Don't surge too early on the first ones.

### In daily life
The varied pace mimics real life, such as hurrying across a road and then walking.

### Why it matters
Runners use fartlek to build speed and stamina without the strict pacing of intervals, and field players use it to mix surges with recovery.
