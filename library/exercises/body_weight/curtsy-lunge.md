A lunge in which you step one leg behind and across the other, as in a curtsy. It works the glutes and thighs and challenges the outer hip muscles and hip rotators.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your hands at your chest.
2. Step your right foot back and across behind your left leg.
3. Bend both knees and lower until your front thigh is near parallel to the floor.
4. Keep your hips and chest facing forward.
5. Push through your left foot to return to standing.
6. Repeat on the same side, or alternate legs.

**Tips:** Keep your front knee over your toes and don't let it cave in. Take a wide enough step to keep balance. Use a shallow depth or hold a rail if you feel unsteady.

### In daily life
It resembles stepping around an obstacle or crossing one foot behind to reach into a car's back seat.

### Why it matters
Court, field and skating sports include sidesteps and crossovers. This lunge trains the outer hip and hip rotators that guide the knee in those moves.
