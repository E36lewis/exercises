Strides are short, relaxed fast runs of 80 to 100 meters. You build up to about 90 percent of top speed and then ease off, which works on running form without much fatigue.

### How to do it
1. Choose a flat, straight 80 to 100 meters (about 90 to 110 yards) of grass or track.
2. Start by jogging, then build speed over the first third.
3. Run smoothly and fast in the middle third, with quick, light steps.
4. Ease off in the final third and walk back.
5. Do 4 to 8 strides, usually after an easy run.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders and face relaxed. Stay tall, with a quick turnover and a short ground contact. Don't sprint all out.

### In daily life
It resembles running to catch a train, but more relaxed.

### Why it matters
Distance runners add strides after easy runs to keep their legs quick and to practice form without the stress of a hard workout.
