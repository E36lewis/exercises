The bird dog is a body-weight exercise on hands and knees. You extend one arm and the opposite leg while holding your trunk still, which trains the back extensors, glutes, shoulders and core to work together.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Keep your back flat and your neck in line with your spine.
3. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back until both are level with your torso.
4. Hold for a moment without twisting your hips.
5. Bring them back to the start.
6. Repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Do not let your lower back sag or your hips twist. Move slowly. Use a mat if your knees are sensitive.

### In daily life
It trains the steady spine you need when you reach across a table or crawl to get something from under a bed.

### Why it matters
It can help build the endurance of the muscles that support the spine, and many people use it as a gentle starting point for core and back work.
