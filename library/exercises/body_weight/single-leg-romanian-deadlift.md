This is a one-leg hip hinge using only your body weight. It works the glutes and hamstrings, and the hips and core keep you balanced.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg with a slight bend in the knee.
2. Place your hands on your hips or hold them out in front.
3. Hinge at your hip and extend the other leg straight behind you.
4. Lower your torso until it is close to parallel to the floor.
5. Squeeze your glutes to stand tall again.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your hips square and your back flat. Rest a hand on a wall if you wobble. Move slowly to feel your hamstring stretch.

### In daily life
You balance like this when you pull on a sock while standing or reach down to unplug a charger.

### Why it matters
It teaches the hip hinge with no load, which can help you learn balance and build the base for heavier single-leg lifts.
