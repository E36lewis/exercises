The imitation skating stride is a dryland drill that copies the side push of skating. You push off one leg to the side and glide the other, then switch.

### How to do it
1. Mark a space about 2 meters (about 2 yards) wide on a smooth floor or a mat.
2. Stand with knees bent and your chest forward.
3. Push off one leg sideways with your whole foot.
4. Land on the other leg and hold the single-leg position for a second, with your arms swinging across.
5. Push back the other way.
6. Work for 30 to 45 seconds and rest 30 seconds. Do 4 to 6 rounds.

**Tips:** Sink into a deep knee bend and stay low. Push through the whole foot. Keep the knee in line with the toes.

### In daily life
The side push is like pushing off a wall when you jump across a puddle.

### Why it matters
Speed skaters, ice hockey and roller players use it to train the skating push in the off-season, and to groove balance on one leg.
