The tip toe extension is a body-weight stretch for the top of the foot and toes. Pointing the foot and curling the toes under stretches the toe extensor muscles, which run along the top of the foot from the shin.

### How to do it
1. Stand with one foot a little behind you, or sit with one leg crossed over the other.
2. Point your toes down and press the tops of your toes toward the floor.
3. Gently increase the pressure until you feel a stretch across the top of the foot.
4. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds while you breathe slowly.
5. Release and repeat on the other foot.

**Tips:** Use gentle pressure only and do not force your toes. A cushioned mat can help if the floor feels harsh. Stop if you feel pain in the arch or ankle.

### In daily life
The tops of your toes get stretched in stairs, kneeling and wearing tight shoes, so freedom here makes those more comfortable.

### Why it matters
Stretching the toe extensors can help keep your feet flexible, which supports walking and balance.
