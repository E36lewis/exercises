The world's greatest stretch strings together a deep lunge, a trunk rotation and a hamstring stretch in one flowing move. It opens the hips, back and chest in a single drill.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position.
2. Step one foot outside your hand into a deep lunge.
3. Place the inside forearm toward the floor, or keep the hand down, and feel the hip stretch.
4. Rotate your chest toward the front leg and reach that arm to the ceiling. Look at your hand.
5. Lower the arm, straighten the front leg and rock back, toes up, to stretch the hamstring.
6. Return to the lunge and step back. Do 5 per side.

**Tips:** Move slowly and breathe. Keep the back leg straight and the heel of the front foot down. Use a block or a cushion under your hand if the floor is too far.

### In daily life
Bending to reach under a car seat or twisting to pick up a bag from the back seat uses these same ranges.

### Why it matters
It is a staple warm-up for athletes in all sports because it covers the hips, thoracic spine and hamstrings in one pass.
