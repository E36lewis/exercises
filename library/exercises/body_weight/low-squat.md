A deep bodyweight squat held low. It works the glutes, thighs, calves and inner thighs, and it is a stretch for the hips and ankles because you hold the bottom position.

### How to do it
1. Stand with feet slightly wider than shoulder-width and toes turned out a little.
2. Brace your abdomen and keep your chest up.
3. Bend your hips and knees to sit down between your heels.
4. Go as low as you can while keeping your heels on the floor and your back long.
5. Pause at the bottom, pressing your knees gently outward with your elbows.
6. Press through your feet to stand back up.

**Tips:** Keep your knees pointing the same way as your toes. If your heels lift, place a small plate under them or squat to a shallower depth. Stop if you feel sharp knee pain.

### In daily life
This is the resting posture for picking up toys from the floor, playing at a child's level, planting seedlings or tying a shoe without bending your back.

### Why it matters
Spending time at the bottom of a squat can support hip and ankle mobility, which makes getting down to the floor and back up easier.
