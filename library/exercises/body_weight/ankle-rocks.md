Ankle rocks are a floor mobility drill for dorsiflexion, the movement of drawing your shin forward over your foot. They stretch the calf and Achilles and open up the ankle.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on one knee with the other foot flat on the floor in front of you.
2. Keep that front heel down and your toes pointing forward.
3. Rock your front knee forward over your toes as far as you can without the heel lifting.
4. Pause for a second, then rock back.
5. Do 10 to 15 slow rocks, then change sides.

**Tips:** Keep the heel glued to the floor and the knee tracking over the second toe, not caving in. Stop at a pull in the ankle, not a pinch at the front.

### In daily life
Squatting down to pick something up, climbing stairs and walking downhill all need free ankle bend.

### Why it matters
Good dorsiflexion helps with squatting depth, landing from jumps and running mechanics. Runners, court and field players and anyone who squats gain from it.
