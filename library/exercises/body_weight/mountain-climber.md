Mountain climbers are done in a plank. You drive your knees alternately toward your chest as if running on the spot, which works the trunk and hip flexors and raises your heart rate.

### How to do it
1. Start in a straight-arm plank, with hands under the shoulders.
2. Brace your trunk so your body forms a line from head to heels.
3. Drive one knee toward your chest.
4. Switch legs quickly, as if running.
5. Keep your hips level.
6. Work 20 to 40 seconds on and 20 seconds off for 4 to 6 rounds.

**Tips:** Don't let your hips rise or sag. Keep the shoulders over the hands. Slow down if your form slips.

### In daily life
The plank position works the same stabilizers used to push yourself up from the floor.

### Why it matters
Fighters, runners and court players build trunk strength and conditioning with it.
