You lie face down and lift your arms and legs off the floor together. It strengthens the muscles along the spine, the glutes and the upper back.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your arms stretched overhead and your legs straight.
2. Look at the floor to keep your neck long.
3. Lift your arms, chest and legs a few inches off the floor at the same time.
4. Squeeze your glutes and upper back.
5. Hold for a moment.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Lift a little, not as high as you can. Don't crank your neck up. Keep the movement smooth. Stop if you feel pinching in your lower back.

### In daily life
These muscles keep you upright when you stand, sit, or bend and lift something light.

### Why it matters
Swimmers, skydivers, gymnasts and climbers need endurance in the muscles along the spine to hold a long body line, whether stretched out in water, in free fall or on a hang.
