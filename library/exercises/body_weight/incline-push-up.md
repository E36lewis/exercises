A push-up with your hands on a raised surface such as a bench or counter. The higher the surface, the lighter the load. It is an easy way to learn the push-up.

### How to do it
1. Place your hands on a stable bench, step or counter, slightly wider than your shoulders.
2. Step your feet back to make a straight line from head to heels.
3. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
4. Lower your chest toward the surface.
5. Stop when your chest is close to it.
6. Push back to straight arms.

**Tips:** Make sure the surface can't slide. Keep your body straight, not sagging. Lower the surface over time to make it harder.

### In daily life
It is like pushing off a counter or a table to stand up.

### Why it matters
Tai chi and precision sports need steady upper-body strength without strain. This version is gentle and good for beginners and for anyone building up to a full push-up.
