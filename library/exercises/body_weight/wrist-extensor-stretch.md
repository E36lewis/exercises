The wrist extensor stretch lengthens the muscles on the top of the forearm that lift the wrist. Tight extensors are linked with tennis elbow.

### How to do it
1. Straighten one arm in front of you at shoulder height, palm down.
2. Bend the wrist so the fingers point toward the floor.
3. With the other hand, gently pull the back of the hand toward you.
4. Keep the elbow straight.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then change sides.

**Tips:** Pull gently, never to pain. Keep the shoulder relaxed. Stretch after use and not on a cold, sore tendon.

### In daily life
Typing, using a mouse and carrying bags all load these muscles.

### Why it matters
Racket players, climbers, gymnasts, archers and wheelchair users use their forearms heavily. Keeping the extensors supple may help prevent tennis elbow.
