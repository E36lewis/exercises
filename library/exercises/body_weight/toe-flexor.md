This toe flexor stretch is a second library version of the kneeling foot stretch. You tuck your toes under and sit back, which lengthens the muscles on the sole of the foot and the toes. Some of the lower leg muscles are involved as well.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on a mat with your toes tucked under.
2. Put your hands on the floor beside your knees.
3. Sit back slowly toward your heels.
4. Stop when you feel a stretch in your toes and arch, and hold while you breathe slowly.
5. Release by shifting your weight forward and lifting your hips.

**Tips:** Sit back only as far as is comfortable, as the stretch can be strong. Come out of it slowly. Avoid it if your toes feel numb or painful, or you have a foot injury.

### In daily life
It helps your feet recover after a long shift on your feet or a day spent in stiff shoes.

### Why it matters
Mobile feet can help you walk, balance and push off comfortably, and may reduce stiffness in the arch.
