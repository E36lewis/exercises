The body-weight tricep dip, often called a bench dip, uses a bench or sturdy chair behind you. You lower and raise your body with your arms, which mainly works the triceps. The chest and shoulders assist.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the edge of a bench with your hands gripping it beside your hips and your fingers pointing forward.
2. Walk your feet out until your knees are bent at about a right angle, then slide your hips off the bench.
3. Bend your elbows straight back to lower your hips toward the floor.
4. Stop when your upper arms are about parallel to the floor.
5. Push through your palms to straighten your arms.

**Tips:** Keep your hips close to the bench so the load stays off the front of your shoulders. Keep your elbows pointing back rather than flaring out. Straighten your legs for a harder version, or keep your feet closer for an easier one.

### In daily life
It matches the push you use to get up from a low sofa or to rise from the floor with your hands behind you.

### Why it matters
It is a simple way to train the triceps anywhere with only a chair, and it builds the pressing strength that helps the shoulder joint stay steady under load.
