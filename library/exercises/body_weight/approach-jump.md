A run-up jump: you take a few quick steps, plant both feet, and swing your arms to jump as high as you can. It is the jump used to spike in volleyball or rise for a basketball layup or dunk.

### How to do it
1. Stand a few steps back from your jump spot with your arms by your sides.
2. Take two or three steps, quickening as you go. The last two steps are long and fast.
3. Plant your feet in a heel-to-toe step, bending your hips and knees as your arms swing back.
4. Drive your arms up and jump straight up as high as you can.
5. Reach up at the top with both arms or one.
6. Land softly on both feet, with your knees bent and your hips back.

**Tips:** Land on both feet with soft knees over your toes. Keep your steps under control. Practise on a non-slip surface, and stop when you are tired and your landing becomes noisy.

### In daily life
It is the same leap you make to reach something high while moving.

### Why it matters
Volleyball and basketball players use a run-up to turn forward speed into height. Training it builds the timing of the last steps, the arm swing and the landing.
