You stand with your back against a wall and slide your arms up and down in a W-to-Y path while they stay in contact with the wall. It trains the shoulder blades to move smoothly and helps your reach overhead.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your back, head and hips against a wall and your feet a little way out.
2. Bend your elbows to 90 degrees with your arms and hands touching the wall.
3. Slide your arms up the wall until they are overhead.
4. Keep your forearms and wrists on the wall.
5. Slide back down to the start.
6. Repeat slowly.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs down so your lower back doesn't arch. Don't shrug. Go only as high as you can keep contact with the wall. Stop if you feel a pinch at the front of the shoulder.

### In daily life
It is the motion of reaching up to a shelf or into the back seat without hunching.

### Why it matters
Overhead reach and shoulder blade control matter in any sport that uses the arms. It is useful for everyone, especially after long periods of sitting.
