A high plank where you lift one hand at a time to touch the opposite shoulder. Your trunk has to resist twisting while your weight shifts, which trains the abdominals and the muscles around the shoulder.

### How to do it
1. Start in a high plank with your hands under your shoulders and your feet a little wider than hip-width.
2. Make a straight line from head to heels and brace your abdomen.
3. Lift your right hand and tap your left shoulder.
4. Put it back down without letting your hips swing.
5. Tap with the other hand.
6. Keep alternating at a steady pace.

**Tips:** Your hips should barely move. Widen your feet to make it easier. Keep your neck in line with your spine and don't let your hips rise.

### In daily life
It matches what your trunk does when you carry a bag on one side or reach with one arm while leaning on the other.

### Why it matters
Racket, combat and court sports ask you to reach and strike with one arm while the trunk stays steady. This drill trains that anti-rotation control, which helps transfer force from the legs to the arm.
