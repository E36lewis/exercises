A bodyweight lunge where you step backward. It works the thighs (quadriceps) and glutes, with the hamstrings, adductors and calves helping, and it asks you to balance on one leg as you go.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your hands on your hips.
2. Step back with one foot, landing on the ball of that foot.
3. Lower your back knee toward the floor.
4. Keep your front knee over your ankle and your torso upright.
5. Push through your front foot to return to standing.
6. Alternate legs.

**Tips:** Step back far enough that your front shin stays almost vertical. Hold a wall or chair for balance if needed. Stop if you feel pain in your knee.

### In daily life
Getting up from kneeling to pick something off the floor, or stepping back out of a narrow space, uses this pattern.

### Why it matters
It is a gentle way to learn single-leg control before you add weight. It can help build leg strength and balance with no equipment.
