The lat stretch is an overhead reach that lengthens the large muscle of the back (the latissimus dorsi) and the side of the trunk.

### How to do it
1. Stand or kneel and reach one arm overhead, holding a doorframe, a post or the other wrist.
2. Lean away from the arm, bending the trunk to the side.
3. Tuck the ribs in and keep the hips still.
4. Feel the stretch along the side of the back.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds and breathe.
6. Change sides.

**Tips:** Keep your ribs from flaring and don't arch your lower back. Stay in the plane of your body.

### In daily life
Reaching for a high shelf uses the same arm position.

### Why it matters
Swimmers, gymnasts, climbers and skiers need full overhead range. Open lats let the arms rise without arching the lower back.
