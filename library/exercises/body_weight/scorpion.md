The scorpion is a dynamic floor stretch. Lying face down, you swing one leg up and across your body to twist the spine and open the front of the hip.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your arms out to the sides in a T.
2. Bend one knee and lift that foot toward the ceiling.
3. Rotate your hips and swing the foot across your body toward the opposite hand.
4. Keep both shoulders on the floor as far as you can.
5. Pause, then return and switch legs. Do 6 to 10 slow reps per side.

**Tips:** Move slowly and do not force the foot to the floor. Keep the shoulders down and press your arms into the floor. Stop if you feel any pinch in the lower back.

### In daily life
It resembles twisting in bed to reach something behind you.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, climbers, swimmers and fighters need a mobile spine and hips. The scorpion warms both and prepares the trunk for twisting.
