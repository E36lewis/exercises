The glute stretch is a floor stretch for the buttock muscles. Crossing one ankle over the opposite knee and pulling the leg toward you lengthens the muscles at the back and side of the hip.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
2. Cross your right ankle over your left knee, so your right knee points out to the side.
3. Reach through the gap and clasp your hands behind your left thigh, or around the shin.
4. Gently pull your left knee toward your chest until you feel a stretch in your right buttock.
5. Keep your head and shoulders relaxed on the floor.
6. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds while you breathe slowly.
7. Release and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Stop at a mild, steady stretch and not a sharp one. If you feel it in your knee, ease off. Keep the crossed foot flexed, so your ankle stays supported.

### In daily life
Long spells in a chair or a car leave the hips stiff. This stretch is an easy floor reset after a day at a desk or a long drive.

### Why it matters
Loosening the muscles around the back of the hip can help your hip range of motion, and may make squats, hinges and walking feel freer.
