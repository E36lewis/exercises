This is a general lower-body stretch that uses a small, controlled bounce at the end of the range. In its side-lunge form it targets the inner thighs, hamstrings and thigh muscles.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wide apart.
2. Shift your weight to one side, bending that knee into a side lunge, with the other leg straight.
3. Place your hands on your bent thigh for support.
4. Gently sink a little deeper and rise slightly, in a small bouncing motion.
5. Shift across to the other side and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep the bounce small and controlled; do not force it. Keep your heels down and your knee over your toes. Warm up first, and stop if you feel sharp pain.

### In daily life
A wide stance with a bent knee is how you step sideways onto a boat or crouch to look under a sofa.

### Why it matters
Gentle, repeated movement through range can help prepare the legs for sport that needs wide, quick steps, and supports hip and groin mobility. Many coaches prefer steady stretches, so treat this as a warm-up option.
