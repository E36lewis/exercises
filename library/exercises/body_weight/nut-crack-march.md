A moving warm-up drill where you march forward, lifting each knee and opening it outward before you step. It works the thighs, inner thighs, calves and hips, and gets your lower body moving.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your arms relaxed.
2. Lift one knee toward your chest.
3. Open the knee outward as if opening a gate.
4. Place that foot down in front.
5. Repeat on the other leg.
6. Continue forward at a steady pace.

**Tips:** Stand tall and balance on the standing leg. Keep the steps controlled and smooth. Use a clear, flat path to avoid tripping. Try 3 to 4 passes of 15 to 20 meters as part of a warm-up, or 30 to 60 seconds of marching.

### In daily life
Lifting the knee and turning the hip out is what you do when you step over a low fence or climb out of a bath.

### Why it matters
It can help hip mobility and balance, and it works as a gentle warm-up before running or training.
