Wrist circles are a gentle warm-up. You draw slow circles with your hands to loosen the wrist joints and tendons.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit with your arms forward and elbows bent.
2. Make loose fists or clasp your hands together.
3. Rotate both wrists in large circles, 10 times one way.
4. Reverse the direction for 10 more.
5. Shake out your hands.

**Tips:** Make the circles full and slow. Keep your forearms still. Skip a painful point.

### In daily life
Typing or driving makes the wrists stiff, and circles bring them to life.

### Why it matters
Any sport that grips or pushes loads the wrist. It is a quick prep for climbing, racket sports, gymnastics and handlebars.
