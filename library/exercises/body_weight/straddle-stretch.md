The straddle stretch is a seated stretch with the legs wide apart. It lengthens the inner thighs and the hamstrings.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your legs in a wide V and your toes pointing up.
2. Sit tall, with your back straight.
3. Walk your hands forward between your legs, hinging at the hips.
4. Stop when you feel a pull along the inner thighs.
5. Hold for 30 to 60 seconds, breathing slowly.
6. Come up slowly.

**Tips:** Hinge from the hips, don't round the back. Keep the knees pointing to the ceiling. Sit on a cushion if the pelvis tips back.

### In daily life
Flexible inner thighs make it easier to step wide or sit on the floor.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, dancers, climbers and fighters rely on wide leg positions. The stretch supports high kicks and splits.
