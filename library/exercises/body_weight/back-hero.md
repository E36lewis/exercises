The back hero is a stretch for the front of the thighs and hips, based on the yoga hero pose. It targets the quadriceps (including rectus femoris) and the hip flexors, and asks for good knee comfort.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on a mat with your knees together and sit back onto your heels. Place a cushion between your calves and thighs if it is uncomfortable.
2. Place your hands on the floor behind you.
3. Slowly lean back, keeping your hips low.
4. Lift your hips slightly if you want a deeper stretch.
5. Hold and breathe steadily.
6. Come back up slowly with your hands.

**Tips:** Go only as far as feels like a gentle stretch. Keep your knees comfortable. Skip this stretch or ease off if you feel knee pain or sharp pinching, and put a folded towel under your ankles if they feel tight.

### In daily life
Flexible thighs let you kneel to play with a child, sit on the floor at a low table and rise again.

### Why it matters
Stretching the front of the thighs after running, cycling or long sitting can help maintain mobility in the hips and knees and support comfortable movement.
