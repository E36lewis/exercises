The stance squat is a body-weight squat done in a wide stance, at a steady cardio pace. A wider stance brings in the inner thighs more than a narrow squat does. It also works the thighs, calves and glutes.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wider than your shoulders and your toes turned slightly out.
2. Bend your hips and knees to lower into a squat, pushing your knees out over your toes.
3. Keep your chest up and let your arms come forward for balance.
4. Pause briefly at the bottom.
5. Press through your whole foot to stand back up.
6. Repeat in a steady rhythm.

**Tips:** Keep your weight over your mid-foot and avoid letting your knees fall in. Go only as deep as you can without your lower back rounding. Slow down if your heels lift. Try 3 sets of 12 to 20 reps at a steady pace, or 30 to 45 seconds of continuous squats with 30 seconds of rest.

### In daily life
A wide stance is the one you take to lower yourself to the ground to play with a child, garden or work at a low shelf.

### Why it matters
It builds endurance in the legs and hips, and the wide stance can help you gain comfort in the hips for deeper positions.
