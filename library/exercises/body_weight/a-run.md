The A run is a faster form of the A skip. You run with a high knee, quick feet and no hop between steps, which trains the rhythm of sprinting.

### How to do it
1. Mark 20 to 30 meters (about 20 to 30 yards) of flat ground.
2. Start with a few A skips to find the rhythm.
3. Switch to quick running steps, driving each knee to hip height.
4. Keep the lifted foot dorsiflexed and snap it down under your hips.
5. Swing the arms in opposition to the legs.
6. Jog back to recover. Do 3 to 5 passes.

**Tips:** Stay tall and keep your steps short and fast. Land on the ball of the foot, under your body. Do not lean back.

### In daily life
The quick knee lift is the same as running up a steep flight of stairs.

### Why it matters
Sprinters, field players and runners use it to work on stride frequency and knee drive, as part of a warm-up before speed work.
