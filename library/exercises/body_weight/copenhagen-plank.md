A side plank with your top leg on a bench and your bottom leg lifted. The inner thigh muscles of the top leg work hard to hold you up. It is one of the best-supported ways to prevent groin strains.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with a bench or box beside you.
2. Put your top leg on the bench, with your knee or ankle on top, and prop yourself on your bottom forearm.
3. Lift your hips so your body makes a straight line.
4. Raise your bottom leg off the floor, or let it rest under the bench for an easier version.
5. Hold, breathing steadily.
6. Lower and switch sides.

**Tips:** Start with the knee on the bench and a short hold. Don't let your hips sag or roll forward. Stop if you feel a pinch in the groin. Build up hold time over several weeks.

### In daily life
The inner thighs steady your legs when you step sideways onto a curb or slip on ice.

### Why it matters
Field, collision, skating, combat and court sports involve kicking, sprinting and sudden sideways pushes that strain the groin. Strong inner thigh muscles protect it, and trained players have fewer groin injuries.
