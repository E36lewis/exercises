A crunch where you bring opposite elbow and knee together while the other leg extends, as if pedalling a bicycle. It works the abdominals and the obliques.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your hands lightly behind your head.
2. Lift your shoulders off the floor and bring your knees up to a tabletop.
3. Twist and bring your right elbow toward your left knee while you extend the right leg.
4. Switch sides in a smooth pedalling motion.
5. Keep your lower back pressed to the floor.
6. Continue for the set, then lower.

**Tips:** Move slowly. Rotate from your ribs, not by pulling on your neck. Keep your elbows wide. Keep the extended leg higher if your back arches.

### In daily life
It is like twisting to reach something beside you while lying or sitting back.

### Why it matters
Combat sports use rotation and trunk flexion for striking and grappling. The oblique work helps those movements.
