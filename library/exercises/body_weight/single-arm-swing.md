This is a loose arm swing with no equipment, used as a shoulder mobility drill. It warms up the deltoid and the muscles around the shoulder.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Let one arm hang loosely at your side.
3. Swing it forward and back in a relaxed motion.
4. Widen the swing a little with each pass, staying comfortable.
5. Slow down and let the arm settle.
6. Switch arms.

**Tips:** Keep your body still and your neck relaxed. Let the arm move freely instead of forcing it. Keep the range comfortable and don't swing hard.

### In daily life
Your arms swing the same way when you walk briskly, and this drill can help them feel freer after a long time at a desk.

### Why it matters
Moving the shoulder gently through its range can help warm it up before you train and keep it loose.
