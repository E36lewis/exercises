A simple bodyweight stretch for the muscles at the back and sides of your neck and the top of your shoulders. You move your head slowly in two directions, forward and to each side.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall with your arms relaxed.
2. Lower your chin gently toward your chest.
3. Hold until you feel a mild stretch at the back of your neck.
4. Return to the centre.
5. Tilt your ear toward your shoulder and hold, then repeat to the other side.
6. Breathe slowly throughout.

**Tips:** Move slowly and don't bounce. Keep your shoulders low and relaxed. Never force the stretch; stop if you feel pain or tingling.

### In daily life
It helps loosen the neck after long hours at a desk, reading in bed or looking down at a phone.

### Why it matters
Regular gentle stretching can help keep your neck moving freely and supports good posture.
