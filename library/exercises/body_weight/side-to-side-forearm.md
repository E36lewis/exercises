This is a gentle forearm stretch that moves your hand sideways at the wrist. It eases the muscles along both edges of your forearm.

### How to do it
1. Hold one arm out in front of you with your elbow straight and your palm facing down.
2. Rest your other hand on the back of that hand.
3. Gently bend your wrist towards your thumb side and hold.
4. Return to the middle, then bend towards your little-finger side and hold.
5. Turn your palm up and repeat in both directions.
6. Switch arms.

**Tips:** Move slowly and stop at a mild pull, never a sharp pain. Keep your shoulder relaxed and your elbow straight but not locked. Breathe out as you ease into each side.

### In daily life
Your wrists drift sideways when you use a mouse, hold a phone or hammer a nail. This stretch can help you loosen up afterwards.

### Why it matters
Your forearm muscles guide the wrist and fingers all day. Stretching them can help your wrists feel looser and your grip feel easier.
