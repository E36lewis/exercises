Ankle circles are a gentle warm-up. You draw slow circles with the foot to take the ankle through its full range and loosen the joint.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand holding a support, with one foot off the floor.
2. Point your toes and begin to draw a large circle with your big toe.
3. Make the circle as big as you can, moving from the ankle and not the knee.
4. Do 10 circles in one direction, then 10 in the other.
5. Change feet.

**Tips:** Keep the leg still and the movement smooth. Make the circles full, not tiny. Skip any painful point.

### In daily life
It is useful after sitting a long time or after a flight.

### Why it matters
Most sports load the ankle. A few minutes of circles warms the joint and its tendons before running, jumping and cutting.
