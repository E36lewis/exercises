The sprawl is a defensive move against a takedown. You throw your legs back and your hips down to the floor, then return to your feet. It is also a fast conditioning drill.

### How to do it
1. Stand in a wrestling stance with your knees bent and hands in front.
2. Drop your hands to the floor and shoot your feet back, hips low.
3. Land with your hips close to the ground and your legs wide.
4. Pop your feet back under you as fast as possible.
5. Return to the stance.
6. Work 20 to 30 seconds on and 20 seconds rest for 4 to 6 rounds, or 8 to 12 reps.

**Tips:** Throw the legs back hard and keep the hips down. Stay on your toes and don't hit the knees. Move quickly through the return.

### In daily life
Quickly dropping down and standing back up is a common daily need.

### Why it matters
Wrestlers and mixed martial artists use it to stop takedowns. It also gives a high heart rate burst.
