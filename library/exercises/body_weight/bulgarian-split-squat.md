The body-weight Bulgarian split squat is a single-leg squat with your rear foot raised on a bench and no added load. It works the thighs, glutes and inner thighs, and is a good way to learn the movement.

### How to do it
1. Stand about two steps in front of a bench with your hands on your hips or in front of you.
2. Place the top of one foot on the bench behind you.
3. Lower your hips straight down until your front thigh is about parallel to the floor.
4. Keep your torso tall.
5. Press through your front foot to stand.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your front knee tracking over your toes. Stay tall. Use a stable bench, and hold on to a wall or rail if you are wobbly.

### In daily life
It resembles the way you step up onto a curb or climb a steep flight of stairs.

### Why it matters
It can help build leg strength and balance without equipment, and it is a good way to practise the pattern before you add weight.
