You walk in a straight line placing one foot directly in front of the other, heel to toe. It trains balance with a narrow base of support.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall next to a wall or a line on the floor.
2. Place your right heel directly in front of your left toes.
3. Shift your weight forward and step your left heel in front of your right toes.
4. Keep your eyes forward and your arms out slightly for balance.
5. Walk ten steps in a straight line.
6. Turn around carefully and walk back.

**Tips:** Walk close to a wall or counter at first. Look at a point ahead, not your feet. Go slowly and keep your steps controlled. Progress to walking backward or with eyes closed only when it is safe.

### In daily life
It is the balance you use to cross a narrow plank, walk along a curb or move through a crowded aisle.

### Why it matters
Tai chi, curling and precision sports ask for slow, steady balance on a narrow base. The drill is low impact, and a good test of balance for people of any age.
