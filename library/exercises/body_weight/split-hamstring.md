The split hamstring stretch is a body-weight stretch for the back of the thigh. Your legs are split, one straight in front and the other bent or tucked, so one hamstring is lengthened at a time. You may feel it in the buttock as well.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor, or kneel on one knee with the other leg straight in front of you.
2. Keep the front heel on the floor with your toes pointing up.
3. Sit tall, then hinge forward from your hips with a long back.
4. Slide your hands down your shin toward your foot until you feel a mild pull behind the thigh.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds while you breathe slowly.
6. Switch legs.

**Tips:** Lead with your chest rather than your head, so your spine stays long and does not round. If you cannot reach your foot, rest your hands on your thigh or use a towel around the foot. Ease off if you feel a sharp pain, tingling or a pull behind the knee.

### In daily life
This pose lengthens the muscles you use to bend forward and pick up a dropped item without bending your knees much, or to straighten a leg while putting on socks.

### Why it matters
Regularly stretching the hamstrings can help keep your hips moving freely, and it eases the hamstring pull on the pelvis that can limit forward bending.
