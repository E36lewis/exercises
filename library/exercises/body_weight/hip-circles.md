Hip circles are a dynamic mobility drill. You lift one knee and draw large circles with it, taking the hip joint through its full range.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg next to a wall, holding it for balance.
2. Lift the other knee to about hip height.
3. Draw a big circle with the knee, out to the side, back, and down.
4. Do 8 to 10 circles in one direction and then 8 to 10 in the other.
5. Change legs.

**Tips:** Keep your standing leg slightly bent and your trunk tall. Make the circles large and smooth. Don't rock the pelvis.

### In daily life
The motion is the one you use to step over a low fence.

### Why it matters
Good for any sport. It warms the hip for running, kicking, skating and cutting.
