A hold on your hands and toes with your knees bent and just off the floor. It trains the trunk, shoulders and thighs together and is the starting position of many crawling drills.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Tuck your toes under.
3. Brace your abdomen and lift your knees about an inch off the floor.
4. Keep your back flat and your head in line with your spine.
5. Hold, breathing steadily.
6. Lower your knees to finish.

**Tips:** Keep your hips level, not high. Don't let your back arch or sag. Keep the knees low. Start with short holds.

### In daily life
It's the steady crouch you use when you look under a bed or work at a low spot.

### Why it matters
Gymnastics, climbing and combat sports require holding a tight, low position with the arms and legs both bearing weight. It builds the stability to do it.
