The handstand push-up is done upside down, usually with your feet against a wall. It is a bodyweight press overhead, so it works the shoulders and triceps hard, plus your upper back and trunk to hold you steady.

### How to do it
1. Set your hands on the floor, about shoulder-width apart, a short distance from a wall.
2. Kick up carefully until your heels rest against the wall and your arms are straight.
3. Brace your abdomen and keep your body in a long line.
4. Bend your elbows and lower your head toward the floor, in control.
5. Press the floor away to straighten your arms again.
6. Come down by lowering your legs back one at a time.

**Tips:** Place a folded mat or cushion under your head. Learn the easier versions first, such as a pike push-up. Have a spotter nearby until you are confident.

### In daily life
Pushing yourself up off the floor, or pushing a heavy door or a box overhead, uses the same shoulder and arm strength in a smaller form.

### Why it matters
It is a demanding test of pressing strength and balance, and it can help build the shoulder strength that carries over to overhead lifts.
