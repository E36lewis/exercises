You lie face down and lift your arms and legs, then kick and move opposite arms and legs up and down like swimming. It trains the back, glutes and shoulders to hold posture.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your arms stretched overhead and your legs straight.
2. Look at the floor to keep your neck long.
3. Lift your arms and legs a few inches off the floor.
4. Lift your right arm and left leg higher while lowering the other pair.
5. Switch in a small, quick flutter.
6. Keep going for the set, then lower to the floor.

**Tips:** Keep the movements small and controlled. Don't lift so high that your lower back pinches. Keep your neck long. Breathe steadily.

### In daily life
These muscles hold you upright and straight when you carry a load or stand for long periods.

### Why it matters
Swimmers keep a long, flat body line while the arms and legs alternate. The drill builds the back endurance needed for good swimming posture.
