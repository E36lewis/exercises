This is a seated stretch with both legs straight in front of you. It stretches the hamstrings at the back of both thighs at once.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with both legs straight in front of you.
2. Sit tall, with your toes pointing up.
3. Hinge forward from your hips and reach towards your toes.
4. Hold when you feel a stretch behind your thighs.
5. Return slowly to sitting tall.

**Tips:** Keep your back long and lead with your chest. Don't bounce or pull hard on your feet. Bend your knees slightly or sit on a folded towel if it feels tight.

### In daily life
You use this range when you bend forward to touch your toes or pick up something from the floor with straight legs.

### Why it matters
Longer hamstrings can help your pelvis tilt more freely, which can make forward bends and sitting on the floor feel more comfortable.
