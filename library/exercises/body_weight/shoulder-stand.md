This is an inverted body-weight pose where you balance on your shoulders with your legs pointing up. You hold it with your hands supporting your lower back. It works the core, glutes and the muscles around the shoulder blades to keep the body in a line, and it gives a calm stretch.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back on a mat, ideally with a folded blanket under your shoulders.
2. Bend your knees and lift your legs and hips off the floor.
3. Place your hands on your lower back, elbows shoulder-width apart.
4. Slowly extend your legs up towards the ceiling.
5. Aim for a straight line from shoulders to feet.
6. Hold, breathing steadily.
7. To come down, bend your knees and roll slowly back to the floor.

**Tips:** Keep your weight on your shoulders and upper arms, not your neck. Don't turn your head. Skip this if you have neck problems.

### In daily life
Getting up from the floor after playing with children, or lifting your legs onto a sofa, goes more smoothly with the body awareness and core control this pose builds.

### Why it matters
It can build core and shoulder control and offers a calm stretch. It supports flexibility in the back and hips.
