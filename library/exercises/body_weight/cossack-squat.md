A wide side-to-side squat where you sink onto one bent leg while the other leg stays straight. It builds strength in the hips and thighs and stretches the inner thigh.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet about twice shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out.
2. Shift your weight onto your right leg and sit your hips back and down.
3. Keep the left leg straight with the toes pointing up, if that is comfortable.
4. Lower as far as you can with your heel down and chest up.
5. Push through your right foot to return to the middle.
6. Repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep the bent knee over your toes and your heel on the floor. Hold a doorframe or light weight in front if you need balance. Don't bounce at the bottom.

### In daily life
It is like crouching sideways to pick something up from under a low shelf.

### Why it matters
Combat sports, skating, gymnastics, climbing, dance and snow sports demand wide stances and deep side-to-side movements. It builds the strength and flexibility to do them safely.
