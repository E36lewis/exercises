Downward dog is a yoga pose in which your body forms an upside-down V, hands and feet on the floor. It stretches the calves and the back of the legs, and opens the shoulders as you press the floor away.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders.
2. Spread your fingers and tuck your toes under.
3. Lift your knees and push your hips up and back.
4. Straighten your legs as far as is comfortable; keep your knees bent if your back rounds.
5. Press your hands into the floor and let your head hang between your arms.
6. Breathe slowly, then lower your knees to release.

**Tips:** Bend your knees if your hamstrings are tight. Lengthen your back rather than forcing your heels flat. Ease out if you feel pain in your wrists.

### In daily life
It eases the stiffness in your calves and hamstrings after long sitting, or after a day on your feet.

### Why it matters
It can help maintain ankle, calf and shoulder flexibility, which supports comfortable walking and squatting.
