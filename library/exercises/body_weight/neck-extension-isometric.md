A hold where you push the back of your head into your hands and the neck doesn't move. It strengthens the muscles at the back of the neck without putting stress on the joints.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall with your chin slightly tucked.
2. Clasp your hands behind your head.
3. Push your head back into your hands while your hands resist.
4. Build the effort to about half of your strength over two seconds.
5. Hold for about ten seconds, breathing normally.
6. Relax slowly.

**Tips:** Don't move the head and don't jerk. Use a gentle push, not your full effort. Stop at once if you feel pain, numbness or tingling in the arms.

### In daily life
These muscles hold your head up when you look at a screen or read in bed.

### Why it matters
Collision, combat, wheeled, mounted and water sports expose the head to jolts and impacts. Strong neck muscles help brace the head and may reduce the strain of those forces.
