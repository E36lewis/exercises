An acceleration sprint is a short, flat-out run of 10 to 30 meters from a standing or crouched start. It trains the push off the ground that gets you up to speed quickly.

### How to do it
1. Mark a start line and a finish line 10 to 30 meters (about 10 to 30 yards) away.
2. Take a split stance or a low crouch with your weight forward.
3. Go on your own signal and drive hard, with a strong forward lean in the first steps.
4. Push the ground back behind you and let your body rise gradually over the first 5 to 6 steps.
5. Run through the finish line, then walk back. Rest 60 to 120 seconds between runs.
6. Program 4 to 8 reps at full effort.

**Tips:** Keep the shin angles low and the steps short at first. Drive the arms hard. Warm up well, since sprints load the hamstrings.

### In daily life
Sprinting to catch a bus or a child heading for the road is an acceleration run.

### Why it matters
Run, field and court sports are decided over the first few steps. Practicing short starts builds the force and technique to get away from a defender or reach a ball first.
