The couch stretch is a deep kneeling stretch for the front of the hip and the thigh. The back foot is propped on a wall or couch, which stretches the quadriceps and the hip flexors together.

### How to do it
1. Kneel with the back of one shin against a wall or couch and your knee on the floor, close to the wall.
2. Place the other foot flat in front in a lunge position.
3. Squeeze the glute of the back leg and tuck your pelvis under.
4. Raise your torso until you feel the stretch along the front of the back thigh and hip.
5. Hold for 30 to 60 seconds, breathing slowly.
6. Change sides.

**Tips:** Do not arch your lower back. Tuck your tailbone and keep your ribs down. Pad the back knee. Move slightly away from the wall if the stretch is too strong.

### In daily life
Sitting for hours shortens the hip flexors. This stretch counters it and makes standing up straight easier.

### Why it matters
Cyclists, wheelchair users and runners tend to have tight hip flexors and quads. Lengthening them helps hip extension, a fuller stride and a more comfortable lower back.
