A bodyweight forward lunge. It works the quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings and calves, with the core steadying your trunk while you balance on a split stance.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and your hands on your hips.
2. Take a long step forward with one foot.
3. Lower your hips straight down until both knees are bent at about a right angle.
4. Keep your front knee over your ankle and your torso upright.
5. Push through your front foot to return to standing.
6. Repeat on the other leg, or finish all reps on one side first.

**Tips:** Take a big enough step that your front knee doesn't travel far past your toes. Keep your trunk upright and your front heel down. Hold a wall for balance if you wobble.

### In daily life
Each rep copies a long stride, so it carries over to taking stairs two at a time, stepping over a puddle or bending down to a low shelf.

### Why it matters
Working one leg at a time can show and help even out strength differences between your sides, and it trains a stable hip and knee in a walking-like position.
