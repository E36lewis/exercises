A small curl of the shoulders off the floor with your knees bent. It is the basic exercise for the rectus abdominis.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat.
2. Place your hands across your chest or lightly by your head.
3. Brace your abdomen and curl your ribs toward your hips.
4. Lift your head and shoulders just off the floor.
5. Pause for a moment.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Small movement is enough; your lower back stays on the floor. Don't pull on your neck. Keep your chin off your chest. Breathe out as you curl up.

### In daily life
It is the motion of sitting up in bed with your knees bent.

### Why it matters
Combat, gymnastics and climbing involve curling the trunk, as when pulling the knees up on a hang or striking. The crunch trains the abdominal muscles for that.
