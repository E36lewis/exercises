A standing stretch for the front of your thigh (the quadriceps) and the hip flexors, the muscles that lift your thigh. You hold one foot behind you, and you can raise the opposite arm to lengthen the stretch.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall near a wall or support and hold it with one hand.
2. Bend one knee and bring your heel toward your buttock.
3. Hold your foot or ankle with your hand on the same side.
4. Keep your knees close together and your standing leg slightly bent.
5. Tuck your pelvis under and gently press your hips forward, until you feel a stretch in the front of your thigh.
6. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Don't arch your lower back or pull your knee out to the side. Keep your knee pointing down. Ease off if you feel pain in your knee.

### In daily life
Sitting for long periods tends to shorten the front of the hips and thighs. This stretch can make it easier to stand tall, walk with a longer stride and climb stairs.

### Why it matters
Flexible hip flexors and quadriceps can help you move your hips freely and support comfortable posture.
