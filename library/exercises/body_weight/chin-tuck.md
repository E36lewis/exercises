You draw your head straight back, as if making a double chin, without tilting it down. It wakes up the deep muscles at the front of the neck that hold the head over the spine.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall and look straight ahead.
2. Place a finger on your chin if it helps.
3. Draw your head straight back, keeping your eyes level.
4. Feel a stretch at the base of your skull and the deep neck muscles work.
5. Hold for about five seconds.
6. Relax and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your eyes level, not looking down. It should be a gentle movement. Don't force it. Stop if you feel pain, tingling or dizziness.

### In daily life
These muscles keep your head over your shoulders when you look at a screen, read or drive.

### Why it matters
Good neck posture and deep neck strength help any sport, and they help people who spend hours at a desk or on a phone.
