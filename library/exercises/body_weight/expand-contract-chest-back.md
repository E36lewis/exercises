This is a bodyweight stretch that moves between opening your chest and rounding your upper back. In the open position it stretches the chest and front of the shoulders; in the crossed position it stretches the muscles between and over the shoulder blades.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall with your arms out in front of you.
2. Open your arms wide and squeeze your shoulder blades together, opening your chest.
3. Breathe in as you open.
4. Then cross your arms, round your upper back and reach forward.
5. Breathe out as you round.
6. Move between the two positions slowly.

**Tips:** Move within a comfortable range. Keep your neck relaxed. Stop if you feel pinching in your shoulders.

### In daily life
It eases the tightness that builds across the shoulders after typing, driving or looking at a phone.

### Why it matters
It can help maintain shoulder and upper back mobility, which supports good posture over a long day.
