The bicep stretch lengthens the biceps at the front of the upper arm. Because the biceps crosses the shoulder, the stretch also reaches the front of the shoulder and a little of the chest.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall.
2. Reach one arm out to your side at about shoulder height, or place your palm on a wall behind you.
3. Straighten your elbow and turn your palm to face away from your body, with your thumb pointing down.
4. Gently turn your chest away from the arm until you feel a pull in the front of the arm.
5. Hold for about 20 to 30 seconds while you breathe slowly.
6. Release and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulder down and your elbow straight but not locked. Stretch to a gentle pull, not pain. Ease off if you feel pinching at the front of the shoulder.

### In daily life
It can help ease the tight, pulled feeling in your arms after carrying bags or holding a steering wheel for a long time.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the arms and front of the shoulders flexible, which supports reaching behind you or overhead comfortably.
