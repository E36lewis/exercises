You lie face down and raise your arms in a Y shape above your head. It strengthens the lower part of the trapezius, which helps your shoulder blade move correctly when you reach overhead.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down on the floor with your arms stretched overhead in a Y, thumbs up.
2. Pull your shoulder blades down and back.
3. Lift your arms off the floor, leading with your thumbs.
4. Pause for a moment with your arms in line with your ears.
5. Lower slowly.
6. Repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your forehead near the floor and your neck long. Don't arch your lower back. Lift only as high as you can with good form. Use no weight at first.

### In daily life
These muscles help you reach to the top shelf or lift a bag into the overhead bin.

### Why it matters
Good shoulder blade control helps keep the shoulder healthy under any arm work. Every sport that involves overhead reaching benefits, so it is useful for everyone.
