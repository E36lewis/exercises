A push-up on your fingertips instead of your palms. It works the chest, front of the shoulders and triceps like a normal push-up, and it adds a load for the muscles of your fingers and forearms that hold your hand in a claw.

### How to do it
1. Start in a push-up position with your fingers spread and your weight on your fingertips, wrists neutral.
2. Make a straight line from head to heels.
3. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
4. Lower your chest toward the floor.
5. Pause just above the floor.
6. Push back up without letting your fingers collapse.

**Tips:** Build up slowly, starting with a few reps or with your knees down. Do a regular push-up warm-up first. Stop if you feel pain in your fingers, wrists or elbows.

### In daily life
Strong fingers can help with gripping a jar lid, climbing, carrying a bag by its handles or holding a racquet.

### Why it matters
It adds finger and forearm strength to the usual push-up. Keep it to a few careful reps, as fingertip loading is demanding on your joints.
