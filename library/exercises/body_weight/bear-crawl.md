You crawl forward on your hands and toes with your knees just off the floor. It builds shoulder and trunk stability while your body moves.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and knees with your hands under your shoulders.
2. Tuck your toes and lift your knees about an inch off the floor.
3. Keep your back flat and your hips low.
4. Move your right hand and left foot forward together.
5. Then move your left hand and right foot.
6. Crawl 5 to 10 metres, then crawl backward or rest.

**Tips:** Move slowly and keep your hips level. Don't let your hips rise or your back sag. Keep your knees close to the floor and your head in line with your spine.

### In daily life
It's the way a toddler moves, and you use similar control crawling under a table.

### Why it matters
Combat, collision, gymnastics and climbing involve loading the arms and trunk while moving. The crawl trains the shoulders and trunk to stay steady and the arms and legs to work together.
