The calf wall is a stretch for the calf muscles, done by leaning toward a wall. With your back knee straight it reaches the gastrocnemius, and bending the knee shifts the stretch to the soleus.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a wall and place your hands on it at chest height.
2. Step one foot back, keeping both feet pointing forward.
3. Keep your back heel on the floor and your back leg straight.
4. Lean your hips toward the wall until you feel a stretch in your back calf.
5. Hold for about 20 to 30 seconds, then bend the back knee slightly to feel the lower calf.
6. Switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your toes pointing forward and your heel down. Do not bounce. Stretch to a gentle pull, not pain.

### In daily life
Flexible calves make it easier to walk down steep ramps and squat to pick something up.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the ankle mobile, which supports comfortable walking and running.
