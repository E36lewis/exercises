A vertical jump in which you pull your knees up toward your chest at the top. It builds explosive drive from the hips and knees.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Dip into a quarter squat and swing your arms back.
3. Jump up as high as you can.
4. Pull both knees up toward your chest at the top.
5. Extend your legs before you land.
6. Land softly on both feet with bent knees, then reset.

**Tips:** Warm up first. Land softly on the balls of your feet, then heels. Use a non-slip surface. Do a few reps with rest, and stop when height falls.

### In daily life
It's the jump you make to clear a low obstacle.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, climbers and swimmers use explosive hip and knee drive to jump, tuck and start. The jump trains power and body control in the air.
