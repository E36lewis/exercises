A safe way to fall sideways. You tuck your chin and slap the mat with your arm so that the impact spreads over the arm, back and side, not your head or hand. It is a basic skill in judo and other martial arts.

### How to do it
1. Stand or squat with your feet shoulder-width apart and your arms crossed in front of your chest.
2. Tuck your chin to your chest.
3. Roll onto one side, lowering yourself to the mat.
4. As you land, slap the mat with your whole arm at about 45 degrees from your body.
5. Keep your legs lifted so they don't hit the mat.
6. Return to standing and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Start from the floor, then squat, then stand. Always practise on a mat. Keep your chin tucked so your head never touches. Slap with a flat arm and an open hand, never land on an outstretched hand or elbow.

### In daily life
It teaches how to fall without hurting your head, wrists or hips, which helps on ice or stairs.

### Why it matters
Combat and skating sports include falls. Knowing how to fall reduces head and wrist injuries, so you can train and play with confidence.
