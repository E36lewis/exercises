A flying sprint is a short run at top speed after a build-up. You accelerate over 20 to 30 meters, then hit full speed through a timed 10 to 30 meter zone.

### How to do it
1. Mark a build-up zone of 20 to 30 meters, then a fast zone of 10 to 30 meters (about 10 to 30 yards) with cones.
2. Warm up well, including strides.
3. Accelerate smoothly through the build-up.
4. Hit maximal speed at the first cone and hold it with relaxed, tall running.
5. Decelerate gradually after the last cone.
6. Walk back and rest 3 to 5 minutes between reps. Do 3 to 6 reps.

**Tips:** Run tall and relaxed, with the foot landing under the hips. Do not try to speed up in the last meters. Warm up carefully.

### In daily life
It is a sprint at full speed, like racing to a finish line.

### Why it matters
Runners and field players train top speed here. It teaches relaxation at maximum velocity, which sprinters need.
