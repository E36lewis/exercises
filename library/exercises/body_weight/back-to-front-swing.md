The back to front swing is a body-weight arm movement in which you swing both arms from behind your body to the front. It warms up the shoulders and chest and takes the shoulder joint through a wide range.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart.
2. Extend your arms behind your body with your palms facing in, within a comfortable range.
3. Swing both arms forward in an arc to shoulder height or just above.
4. Return the arms along the same path.
5. Repeat in a smooth, controlled rhythm.

**Tips:** Keep your trunk steady and do not lean back. Keep your shoulders relaxed. Use a small range if your shoulders feel tight and never force the arms behind you.

### In daily life
The swinging motion matches the natural arm swing of a brisk walk, as well as reaching back for a seatbelt and forward again.

### Why it matters
Moving the shoulders both behind and in front of the body can help warm up the chest and the back of the shoulders before exercise and supports a comfortable range of motion.
