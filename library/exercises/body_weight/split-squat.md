A single-leg squat from a staggered stance, with both feet staying put. You bend both knees to lower and stand back up. It builds thigh and glute strength and is the base for lunges and Bulgarian split squats.

### How to do it
1. Step one foot well forward and keep the other foot back with the heel lifted.
2. Stand tall with your hands on your hips.
3. Bend both knees and lower your hips straight down.
4. Stop when your back knee is just above the floor.
5. Push through the front foot to stand up.
6. Finish the reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your front heel down and your knee over your toes. Stay upright and don't let your front knee cave in. Use a wall or rail for balance if you wobble.

### In daily life
It is the pattern behind going up stairs or getting out of a low car seat one leg at a time.

### Why it matters
Because most sports happen on one leg at a time, this builds balanced leg strength for all sports. It also leads you into lunges and the Bulgarian split squat.
