This is a no-equipment hold on one forearm and the side of your foot. It works the obliques and the deep side of your trunk, and your glutes and hip muscles keep your hips lifted.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your side with your legs straight and your feet stacked.
2. Place your forearm on the floor with your elbow directly under your shoulder.
3. Lift your hips until your head, hips and feet make one straight line.
4. Brace your abs and breathe steadily.
5. Hold for the time you chose without letting your hips sink or drift back.
6. Lower with control and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Push the floor away with your forearm so your shoulder stays tall. Keep your hips and shoulders stacked instead of rolling forward. To make it easier, bend your knees and keep them on the floor.

### In daily life
Your trunk has to stay firm on one side when you carry a heavy shopping bag in one hand or hold a toddler on your hip.

### Why it matters
It trains your sides to resist bending, which can help keep your spine steady and your hips level when you walk, lift and play sport.
