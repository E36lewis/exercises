A hold where you push your forehead into your hand and the neck doesn't move. It strengthens the muscles at the front of the neck.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall with your chin slightly tucked.
2. Place your palm on your forehead.
3. Push your head forward into your hand while your hand resists.
4. Build the effort to about half of your strength over two seconds.
5. Hold for about ten seconds, breathing normally.
6. Relax slowly.

**Tips:** Keep it still and smooth. Don't push hard or jam the chin down. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or tingling.

### In daily life
These muscles lift your head off the pillow when you sit up and hold it steady when you brake in a car.

### Why it matters
Collision, combat, wheeled, mounted and water sports can snap the head forward. A strong front of the neck helps hold the head steady and balanced against the back of the neck.
