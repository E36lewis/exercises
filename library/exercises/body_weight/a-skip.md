The A skip is a rhythmic skip with a high knee drive and a fast, active foot strike. It is a basic sprint mechanics drill that teaches the knee lift and foot placement of fast running.

### How to do it
1. Mark out 20 to 30 meters (about 20 to 30 yards) of flat ground.
2. Stand tall and skip forward with a step-hop rhythm.
3. On each step, drive the knee up to hip height with the toes pulled up, ankle under the knee.
4. Snap the foot down under your hips and land on the ball of the foot.
5. Swing the opposite arm forward with each knee.
6. Walk back to recover. Do 3 to 5 passes.

**Tips:** Stay tall and do not lean back. Keep the lifted foot dorsiflexed. Land under the body, not out in front. Start slowly and speed up only when the rhythm is clean.

### In daily life
The rhythm is the same as skipping down the street, with the knee lift of running up a flight of stairs.

### Why it matters
Sprinters, field players and distance runners use it in warm-ups to ground good knee lift and foot strike, which supports speed and efficient running.
