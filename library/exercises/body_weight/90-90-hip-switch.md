The 90 90 hip switch is a seated mobility drill. Both knees are bent to right angles, one leg in front and one to the side, and you rotate your hips to swap sides. It works the hip's inward and outward rotation.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent. Let both knees fall to the right so the front shin and the back shin each make about a right angle.
2. Sit tall with your chest up.
3. Lift both knees, rotate your hips and lower the knees to the left, so the legs swap roles.
4. Use your hands for balance if you need to.
5. Do 8 to 10 switches each way, or hold each side for 30 seconds.

**Tips:** Sit as tall as you can and avoid dropping onto one hand. Move slowly and do not force a knee onto the floor. Sit on a cushion if your hips are tight.

### In daily life
Getting into and out of a car, sitting cross-legged and turning on a chair all use hip rotation.

### Why it matters
Free hip rotation helps every sport that turns, cuts, kicks or swings, and it keeps the hip joint comfortable under load.
