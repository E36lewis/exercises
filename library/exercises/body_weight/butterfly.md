The butterfly is a seated stretch for the inner thighs and groin (the adductors and gracilis). You sit with the soles of your feet together and let your knees fall open.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your back tall.
2. Bend your knees and bring the soles of your feet together.
3. Hold your feet or ankles and draw them gently toward you.
4. Let your knees drop toward the floor, without pushing them.
5. Hinge forward a little from your hips, keeping your back long.
6. Hold for about 20 to 30 seconds while you breathe.

**Tips:** Keep your back long and avoid rounding. Do not push on your knees. Stretch to a gentle pull, not pain, and sit on a cushion if your hips are tight.

### In daily life
Supple inner thighs make it easier to sit cross-legged on the floor or step out of a low car.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the hips open, which supports a comfortable stride and wide stances in sport.
