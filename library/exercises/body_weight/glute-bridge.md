A floor exercise where you lift your hips from the floor with your feet planted. It targets the glutes and is the basic bridge from which harder variations are built.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat, hip-width apart.
2. Put your arms by your sides.
3. Brace your abdomen and push your heels into the floor.
4. Lift your hips until your shoulders, hips and knees make a straight line.
5. Squeeze your glutes at the top for a moment.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Don't arch your lower back at the top; stop when your body is straight. Keep your ribs down and your knees in line with your toes. Drive through your whole foot.

### In daily life
It is the hip push that helps you get up from the floor or sit up in bed.

### Why it matters
Strong glutes help you run, jump, change direction and stay balanced in every sport, and they support the lower back. This version needs no kit, so anyone can build hip strength and learn to feel the glutes working.
