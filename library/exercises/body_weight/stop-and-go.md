Stop and go is a run that trains slowing down to a halt and then speeding up again. It builds the braking strength and the push that field and court sports require.

### How to do it
1. Mark a 20-meter (about 20-yard) lane with cones every 5 meters.
2. Sprint to the first cone and stop in 2 steps, with knees bent and weight low.
3. Hold the stop for a second, balanced.
4. Sprint at once to the next cone and stop again.
5. Continue to the end, then jog back.
6. Do 4 to 6 runs, resting 60 seconds.

**Tips:** Sink your hips to stop, with the chest over the knees. Don't stop with straight legs. Take shorter steps to brake.

### In daily life
Stopping at a kerb as a car goes by is the same action.

### Why it matters
Football, soccer, basketball and tennis are full of stops and starts. Practicing deceleration builds the eccentric strength that guards the knees.
