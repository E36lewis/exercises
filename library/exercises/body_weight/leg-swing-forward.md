The forward leg swing is a dynamic warm-up. You stand on one leg and swing the other forward and back like a pendulum, taking the hip through its range and warming the hamstrings and hip flexors.

### How to do it
1. Stand next to a wall or post and rest one hand on it.
2. Shift your weight onto the inside leg with a soft knee.
3. Swing the outside leg forward and back in a straight line, keeping your torso tall.
4. Let each swing travel a little higher, but stay relaxed and in control.
5. Do 10 to 15 swings, then turn around and swing the other leg.

**Tips:** Keep your trunk still and do not arch your back at the end of the swing. Let the leg swing, do not kick it. Keep the range pain free.

### In daily life
It looks like the leg swing you use in a long stride or when stepping over a fence.

### Why it matters
A good warm-up for runners, field players, martial artists and dancers. It readies the hips for sprinting and kicking, and may help reduce the chance of a pulled hamstring.
