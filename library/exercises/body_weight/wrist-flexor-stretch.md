The wrist flexor stretch lengthens the muscles of the underside of the forearm that curl the wrist and fingers. It eases forearm tightness.

### How to do it
1. Straighten one arm in front of you at shoulder height, palm up.
2. Point the fingers toward the floor.
3. With the other hand, gently draw the fingers back toward you.
4. Keep the elbow straight.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then change sides.

**Tips:** Keep the pull gentle and the shoulder relaxed. Spread the fingers if you want to extend the stretch to the hand.

### In daily life
Gripping tools, bags and handlebars shortens these muscles.

### Why it matters
Climbers, gymnasts, racket players and wheelchair users grip all day. A looser forearm supports grip comfort and may reduce the strain on the elbow.
