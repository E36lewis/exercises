You lie face down and raise your arms out to the sides to form a T, squeezing your shoulder blades together. It strengthens the muscles between the shoulder blades and the back of the shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down on the floor or a bench with your arms out to the sides.
2. Turn your thumbs up toward the ceiling.
3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
4. Lift your arms to shoulder height or a little higher.
5. Pause for a moment.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your neck long and your forehead near the floor. Don't shrug your shoulders up to your ears. Don't swing; lift with the back muscles. Use no weight until you can do the full set cleanly.

### In daily life
These muscles keep your shoulders back and your posture upright when you sit at a desk or carry a backpack.

### Why it matters
Because the move strengthens the mid-back and back shoulders, it helps counter the rounded posture that comes with sitting and pressing. All sports that use the arms benefit.
