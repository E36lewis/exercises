Frog pose is a yoga stretch for the inner thighs. With your knees wide and your hips back, it stretches the adductors and the groin.

### How to do it
1. Start on hands and knees on a soft surface.
2. Slide your knees wide apart.
3. Keep your ankles in line with your knees, feet turned out.
4. Lower onto your forearms.
5. Sit your hips back toward your heels until you feel a gentle stretch.
6. Hold, breathing slowly, then come out slowly.

**Tips:** Pad your knees with a folded towel. Stop at a mild stretch. Don't force your knees wider.

### In daily life
It eases the tightness in your inner thighs after sitting cross-legged or driving for a long time.

### Why it matters
It can help hip flexibility, which supports squatting and walking with a wider stride.
