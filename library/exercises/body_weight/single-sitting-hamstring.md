This is a seated stretch for one leg at a time. It stretches the hamstrings at the back of the thigh.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with one leg straight in front of you and the other bent.
2. Rest the sole of the bent foot against your straight leg if that's comfortable.
3. Sit tall.
4. Hinge forward from your hips and reach towards your toes.
5. Hold when you feel a stretch behind your thigh.
6. Switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your back long instead of rounding. Don't bounce. Soften the knee of the straight leg if it feels tight.

### In daily life
You can bend forward to tie a lace or reach a low shelf on one side with less pulling behind your leg.

### Why it matters
Stretching one leg at a time shows you any difference between sides, and flexible hamstrings can help your hips move freely.
