A hollow hold in which you rock back and forth like a rocking chair. Your body stays in one tight curve. It trains the trunk to keep its shape while moving.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back in a hollow hold, with your arms overhead and your legs straight, lifted.
2. Press your lower back into the floor.
3. Rock back so your shoulders move toward the floor.
4. Rock forward so your hips and legs move toward the floor.
5. Keep your body shape the same throughout.
6. Continue, then lower to finish.

**Tips:** Learn the hollow hold first. Keep your lower back down; if it arches, bend your knees. Rock from a tight trunk, not by throwing your arms.

### In daily life
It trains tension through the whole trunk, like keeping a stiff body when you roll over.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and climbers use the hollow shape in swings, kips and tight body lines. Rocking builds the control to keep it.
