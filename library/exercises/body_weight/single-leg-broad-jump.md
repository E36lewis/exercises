A jump forward for distance, taking off and landing on the same leg. It builds single-leg power and is a common hop test.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg with a soft knee and your arms ready.
2. Swing your arms back and bend your hip and knee to dip down.
3. Swing your arms forward and jump out as far as you can.
4. Land on the same foot, bending your knee and hip.
5. Hold the landing steady for two seconds.
6. Reset and repeat, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Hold each landing without putting the other foot down. Keep the knee over your toes. Build up slowly with short jumps. Stop if your landings wobble or the knee hurts.

### In daily life
It's like hopping across stepping stones.

### Why it matters
Field, court and running sports include one-footed push-offs and landings. The hop test is also used to compare both legs and decide if you are ready to return to sport.
