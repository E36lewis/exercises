Toe object pick up is a body-weight foot exercise where you grip small objects with your toes. The muscles of the arch and the toe flexors do the gripping.

### How to do it
1. Sit in a chair with your foot flat on the floor. Put some small objects, such as marbles or a washcloth, in front of you, with a bowl beside them.
2. Curl your toes around one object to grip it.
3. Lift it and move it over the bowl.
4. Release it into the bowl.
5. Repeat with each object, then switch feet.

**Tips:** Start with soft, light objects such as a towel and move to smaller items later. Keep your ankle still so only the toes move. Stop if you feel cramping.

### In daily life
Gripping with your toes is a skill for balancing on one leg, walking on loose sand or standing on a moving bus.

### Why it matters
It can help build the small muscles that hold up the arch and steady your balance, which supports foot strength and stability.
