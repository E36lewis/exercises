The frog leap is a jumping exercise that starts from a deep, wide squat. It works the thighs, glutes, calves and inner thighs, and raises your heart rate.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet wider than your shoulders, toes turned out.
2. Lower into a deep squat with your hands between your knees.
3. Drive through your feet and jump forward and up.
4. Swing your arms forward for momentum.
5. Land softly back in the squat position.
6. Reset and jump again.

**Tips:** Land quietly with bent knees. Keep your knees over your toes. Jump on a flat, non-slip surface. Try 4 to 6 sets of 5 to 8 leaps, resting 60 to 90 seconds, or leap down 15 to 20 meters of open ground.

### In daily life
It copies springing off the ground and landing low, like jumping over a ditch.

### Why it matters
It can help build leg power and the control to land safely, which supports jumping sports.
