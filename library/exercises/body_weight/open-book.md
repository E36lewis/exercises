The open book is a lying rotation drill for the upper back. You lie on your side with the knees stacked and open your top arm like a book cover, turning your chest to the ceiling.

### How to do it
1. Lie on one side with your hips and knees bent to about 90 degrees and your arms straight out in front, palms together.
2. Keep your knees stacked and pressed together.
3. Lift the top arm and open it up and back, following your hand with your eyes.
4. Rotate your chest toward the ceiling as far as you can without the knees moving.
5. Pause, then return and repeat 8 to 10 times. Turn over for the other side.

**Tips:** Keep the knees together. Move from the upper back, not the lower back. Breathe out as you open. Do not force the arm to the floor.

### In daily life
It helps when you twist to look over your shoulder when reversing the car.

### Why it matters
Hitting, throwing, paddling and aiming sports all rely on a supple upper back. Better thoracic rotation lets the shoulders and lower back do less work.
