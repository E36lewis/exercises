You balance on one leg, hinge forward, then rotate your hips open and closed like an airplane tilting its wings. It trains control of hip rotation on one leg.

### How to do it
1. Stand on your right leg with a soft knee and your left leg lifted behind you.
2. Hinge forward at the hips until your trunk is close to parallel to the floor.
3. Hold your arms out like wings.
4. Rotate your hips and chest open toward the left, then close back toward the floor.
5. Keep your standing knee steady over your toes.
6. Do all the reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Use a wall or a chair for balance until you feel steady. Move slowly. Don't let the standing knee twist.

### In daily life
It trains the control you need to turn while standing on one leg, as when you reach to pick up something while stepping.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, climbers, dancers, tai chi practitioners and field players move and turn on one leg with control. This drill builds hip rotation strength and balance to do that safely.
