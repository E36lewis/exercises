The cross body shoulder stretch pulls one arm across your chest to stretch the back of the shoulder, including the posterior capsule and rear deltoid.

### How to do it
1. Stand or sit tall.
2. Bring one arm straight across your chest at shoulder height.
3. Hold it just above the elbow with the other hand.
4. Gently pull the arm toward your chest until you feel a stretch in the back of the shoulder.
5. Keep the shoulder down, away from your ear.
6. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, then change arms.

**Tips:** Do not twist your trunk or lift the shoulder. Pull on the upper arm, not on the elbow joint. Keep the stretch gentle and pain free.

### In daily life
Reaching across your body for a seat belt or to scratch your opposite shoulder uses this range.

### Why it matters
Overhead and throwing sports, racket sports, swimming, climbing and gymnastics can tighten the back of the shoulder. Keeping it supple supports a full reach and a healthy joint.
