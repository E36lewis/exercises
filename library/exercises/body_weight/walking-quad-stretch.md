The walking quad stretch is a dynamic warm-up in which you step forward and pull one heel to your glute with each step, stretching the front of the thigh.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall.
2. Lift one heel toward your glute and catch the ankle with the same-side hand.
3. Squeeze the glute and keep your knees close.
4. Hold for a second and feel the front of the thigh stretch.
5. Release and step forward, then catch the other foot.
6. Do 8 to 10 reps per leg across 10 to 15 meters.

**Tips:** Keep your knees together and your trunk upright. Tuck the pelvis, don't arch your back. Hold a wall at first if you wobble.

### In daily life
Pulling a foot back when you stand on one leg to take a sock off uses this position.

### Why it matters
It prepares runners, field players and cyclists by warming the quads and loosening the hip flexors.
