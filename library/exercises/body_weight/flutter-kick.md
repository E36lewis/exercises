You lie on your back and make small, quick up-and-down kicks with straight legs held just above the floor. It builds endurance in the hip flexors and lower abdominals.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your hands under your hips or by your sides.
2. Press your lower back into the floor and brace your abdomen.
3. Lift both legs a few inches off the floor, keeping them straight.
4. Kick one leg up a little as the other goes down.
5. Keep alternating in small, quick kicks.
6. Lower your legs to the floor to finish.

**Tips:** If your lower back arches, raise your legs higher or bend your knees. Keep the kicks small and steady. Breathe throughout and keep your neck relaxed.

### In daily life
Your lower abdomen and hip flexors do this work when you lift your legs into bed or hold your feet off the floor.

### Why it matters
Swimmers kick for minutes at a time. This dry-land version builds the hip and trunk endurance needed to keep a steady kick with good body position in the water.
