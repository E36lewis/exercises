Butt kicks are a running drill where you flick each heel toward your glutes with each step. They warm the hamstrings and teach a quick leg recovery.

### How to do it
1. Mark 20 to 30 meters (about 20 to 30 yards) of flat ground.
2. Stand tall and jog forward with small steps.
3. Flick each heel up toward your glute, keeping the thigh under the hips.
4. Land on the ball of the foot.
5. Swing your arms as in running.
6. Walk back and rest. Do 3 to 5 passes, or work in place for 20 to 30 seconds.

**Tips:** Keep your thighs pointing down, not forward. Stay tall, and don't lean. Use quick steps.

### In daily life
The flick resembles the heel motion in a casual jog.

### Why it matters
A hamstring warm-up for run and field sports, it teaches the quick heel recovery of sprinting.
