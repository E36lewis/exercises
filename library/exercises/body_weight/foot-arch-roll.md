The foot arch roll is a gentle bodyweight exercise for the arch of your foot. It moves the foot between its outer and inner edges, which works the small muscles of the sole.

### How to do it
1. Stand barefoot, holding a support if needed.
2. Place one foot flat on the floor.
3. Roll your weight onto the outer edge of the foot.
4. Roll back across the sole to the inner edge, lifting the arch slightly.
5. Move slowly.
6. Repeat, then switch feet.

**Tips:** Keep the movements small. Hold a support for balance. Stop if you feel cramping.

### In daily life
It trains the small foot muscles that adjust when you walk on cobbles, sand or a trail.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the feet mobile and supports balance, especially if you spend most of the day in shoes.
