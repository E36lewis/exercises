A bodyweight press from the floor. It works the chest (pectoralis major), the front of the shoulders and the triceps, while your abdominals, glutes and serratus anterior keep your body in one line.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and toes, hands slightly wider than your shoulders.
2. Make a straight line from head to heels.
3. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
4. Lower your chest toward the floor, with your elbows at about 45 degrees to your body.
5. Stop when your chest is just above the floor.
6. Push the floor away until your arms are straight.

**Tips:** Don't let your hips sag or rise. Keep your neck in line with your spine. If you can't do a full rep, put your hands on a bench or drop to your knees.

### In daily life
Getting up from the floor, pushing a stuck door, or catching yourself in a fall all rely on pushing against something with your whole body.

### Why it matters
It trains the upper-body push and the trunk together, needs no equipment, and is easy to make harder or easier.
