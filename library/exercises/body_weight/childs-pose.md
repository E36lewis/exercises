Child's pose is a resting yoga stretch. You sit back onto your heels and fold forward with your arms out in front, which gently stretches the back, hips and shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on a mat with your big toes together and your knees apart.
2. Sit your hips back toward your heels.
3. Fold forward and lay your chest between or on your thighs.
4. Reach your arms forward along the floor.
5. Rest your forehead on the floor or a cushion.
6. Stay for several slow breaths.

**Tips:** Keep your breathing relaxed. Place a cushion under your hips if they do not reach your heels. Ease off if you feel pain in your knees.

### In daily life
It is a way to rest and release tension in the back after a long day on your feet or a hard session.

### Why it matters
It can help you relax and gently lengthen the back, hips and shoulders, which supports recovery between sets or sessions.
