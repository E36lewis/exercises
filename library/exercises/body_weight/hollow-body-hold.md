A hold on your back with your arms, shoulders and legs lifted off the floor and your lower back pressed down. It trains the abdominals to keep the whole body in one tight, shallow curve.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your arms by your ears and your legs straight.
2. Press your lower back into the floor and brace your abdomen.
3. Lift your shoulders and your straight legs a few inches off the floor.
4. Keep your arms beside your ears and your ribs pulled down.
5. Hold, breathing in short breaths.
6. Lower with control to finish.

**Tips:** If your lower back peels off the floor, raise your legs higher or bend your knees. Don't pull on your neck. Build hold time slowly.

### In daily life
It is the same full-body tension you use to brace when lifting a heavy bag over your head or holding yourself steady on a bumpy ride.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts, climbers, swimmers, fighters and skydivers all need a tight body line. A hollow shape keeps the trunk stiff so force passes through it, whether on a bar, a wall, in the water or in free fall.
