Carioca is a sideways run in which the trailing leg crosses in front and then behind the lead leg. The twist at the hips and shoulders trains hip rotation and quick feet.

### How to do it
1. Mark 10 to 15 meters (about 10 to 15 yards) of space.
2. Stand sideways with your knees slightly bent and arms out.
3. Cross the back foot in front of the lead foot.
4. Step the lead foot out to the side.
5. Cross the back foot behind the lead foot, and step the lead foot out again.
6. Keep the shoulders facing forward while the hips twist.
7. Work the other direction on the way back. Do 4 to 6 passes.

**Tips:** Let the hips rotate and keep the shoulders square. Stay light on the balls of your feet. Start slowly before speeding up.

### In daily life
Sidestepping along a crowded wall with crossing feet is the same pattern.

### Why it matters
Field, court and combat sports need rotating hips and quick crossover steps. The drill supports coordination and a fluid change of direction.
