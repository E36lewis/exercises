You draw the ball of your foot toward your heel without curling your toes, which lifts the arch. It builds the small muscles inside the foot that support the arch.

### How to do it
1. Sit with your foot flat on the floor and your knee over your ankle.
2. Keep your toes long and relaxed on the floor.
3. Draw the ball of your foot toward your heel to make the arch rise.
4. Hold for about five seconds without curling your toes.
5. Relax.
6. Repeat, then do the other foot. Progress to standing.

**Tips:** The foot should shorten, not the toes curl. Keep your heel and the ball of the big toe on the floor. Don't force it; it takes practice to find the muscles.

### In daily life
These muscles help the foot to grip the ground when you stand barefoot or walk on uneven ground.

### Why it matters
Runners, dancers, gymnasts, climbers and tai chi practitioners rely on a steady foot for balance and push-off. A strong arch also helps ease plantar foot pain.
