The cat stretch is a gentle spine mobility exercise on hands and knees. You move your back from rounded to arched, which moves each part of the spine through its range.

### How to do it
1. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
2. Breathe in and let your belly drop as you lift your head and tailbone.
3. Breathe out, press your hands into the floor and round your back toward the ceiling.
4. Tuck your chin and tailbone slightly.
5. Move smoothly between the two shapes.
6. Repeat for several slow breaths.

**Tips:** Move slowly and within a comfortable range. Keep your arms steady. Use a mat if your knees are sensitive, and ease off if you feel pain.

### In daily life
It loosens a stiff back when you get out of bed or stand up from a long drive.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the spine moving and is a gentle warm-up for the back before heavier exercise.
