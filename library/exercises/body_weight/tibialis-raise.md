A raise of the toes toward the shins, with your heels on the floor and your back against a wall. It strengthens the muscle on the front of the shin.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your back against a wall and your feet about a foot away from it.
2. Keep your legs straight and your heels on the floor.
3. Pull your toes up toward your shins as high as you can.
4. Pause for a moment at the top.
5. Lower your toes slowly.
6. Repeat for high reps, with a smooth rhythm.

**Tips:** Move your ankle only; don't bend your knees or hips. Move your feet farther from the wall to make it harder. Stop if the shin hurts sharply.

### In daily life
The shin muscle lifts your toes at every step so your foot clears the ground when you walk.

### Why it matters
Runners, hikers, field and court players have shin splints from tired shin muscles, and weak ones cause foot slap. Training the muscle helps the shin cope with the pounding of many steps.
