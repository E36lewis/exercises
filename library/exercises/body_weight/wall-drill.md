The wall drill teaches the posture of a sprint start. You lean against a wall at about 45 degrees and practice lifting and driving each knee.

### How to do it
1. Stand facing a wall, with your hands flat on it at shoulder height and your feet back so your body forms a straight line leaning at about 45 degrees.
2. Lift one knee to hip height with the toes pulled up.
3. Drive that foot down hard and lift the other knee.
4. Alternate, switching with a quick rhythm.
5. Do 3 or 4 sets of 5 to 10 contacts per leg, or 10 to 15 seconds.

**Tips:** Hold the body in a straight line from head to ankle. Don't let the hips sag. Keep your toes pulled up and hit the floor under your hips.

### In daily life
The lean is like pushing a stuck car or a heavy cart.

### Why it matters
Sprinters and field players use it to learn posture and knee drive in the start without the speed.
