A bodyweight bound from side to side, landing on one foot each time. It is a cardio and power drill that works the thighs, calves and the muscles along the sides of your hips as they push off and then catch you.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg with a slight bend in the knee, on a flat, non-slip surface with room on both sides.
2. Sink a little into your hip and knee.
3. Push off sideways through the whole foot and leap to the other side.
4. Land softly on the opposite foot, with the knee bent and your hip loaded.
5. Pause briefly to find your balance, then leap back.
6. Continue side to side at a controlled pace.

**Tips:** Land quietly and keep your knee tracking over your toes, not caving inward. Look ahead, not at your feet. Start with short leaps and clear the space of obstacles to protect your ankles.

### In daily life
The side-to-side push and catch is what you do when you jump over a puddle, a gap in the pavement or a small stream and land on one foot.

### Why it matters
Pushing off and landing sideways can help build leg power, balance and ankle control in a direction that straight-ahead training misses, and it supports agility in many sports.
