This is a standing stretch using your body weight. You raise one arm overhead and bend your trunk to the opposite side. It opens the side of your body, especially the lat (latissimus dorsi) and the muscles around your shoulder blade and arm.

### How to do it
1. Stand tall with your feet about hip-width apart.
2. Raise one arm overhead.
3. Gently lean your upper body to the opposite side.
4. Reach long through your fingertips and feel the stretch along your side.
5. Hold, breathing slowly.
6. Return to upright and switch sides.

**Tips:** Lean sideways, not forward or back. Keep both hips level. Stretch gently and stop before any pain.

### In daily life
After a long time sitting, your sides can feel short and tight. This stretch can ease that and make it more comfortable to reach to a high shelf or twist for something on the back seat.

### Why it matters
It supports flexibility along your side and shoulder, which can help you move more freely.
