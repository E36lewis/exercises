Upward dog is a yoga stretch in which you lie face down and press your chest up on straight arms, with only your hands and the tops of your feet on the floor. It opens the chest, shoulders, abdomen and the fronts of the hips, and it also stretches the front of the shins and the tops of the feet.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down with your legs extended and the tops of your feet on the floor.
2. Place your palms flat beside your lower ribs, with your elbows bent and pointing back.
3. Press into your hands, straighten your arms and lift your chest and thighs off the floor.
4. Keep the tops of your feet pressing down and your legs active.
5. Roll your shoulders back and away from your ears, and look forward or slightly up.
6. Hold for a few breaths, then lower yourself slowly.

**Tips:** Keep your neck long and avoid dropping your head back. Spread the arch through your upper back instead of squeezing your lower back. Lift your chest only as high as feels comfortable, and skip it if you have back pain.

### In daily life
It counteracts the forward lean of sitting at a desk or driving and helps you stand tall with your chest open.

### Why it matters
Opening the chest and the fronts of the hips can help your posture and make it easier to extend your spine.
