A gentle stretch for the neck and upper shoulder muscles, using only the weight of your hand. You tilt your head toward one shoulder to lengthen the muscles on the opposite side of your neck.

### How to do it
1. Sit or stand tall with your shoulders relaxed.
2. Place one hand on the top of your head.
3. Let your head tilt gently toward that shoulder.
4. Let the weight of your hand add a little stretch.
5. Hold for a comfortable time, breathing slowly.
6. Release and repeat on the other side.

**Tips:** Use only the weight of your hand and never pull. Keep the opposite shoulder down. Stop if you feel pain, tingling or dizziness.

### In daily life
It eases the stiff neck that builds up after hours at a desk, on a long drive or looking down at a phone.

### Why it matters
Gentle stretching can help you feel looser and supports comfortable neck movement, such as checking over your shoulder.
