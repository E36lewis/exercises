This is a seated rotating core exercise that needs no equipment. You hold your hands together, lean back and turn from side to side. It works the abs and the obliques, the muscles at the sides of your waist, with the hip flexors helping you stay leaned back.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent and your heels on the ground.
2. Lean back slightly until you feel your abs working, keeping your back straight.
3. Clasp your hands together in front of your chest.
4. Rotate your torso to one side.
5. Return through the centre and rotate to the other side.
6. For more challenge, lift your feet off the floor.

**Tips:** Rotate from your ribs, not just your arms. Keep your chest lifted and your back long. Stay smooth and controlled, and keep your feet down if your back feels strained.

### In daily life
Turning to reach behind you as you get out of a car, or swinging a bag onto your shoulder, uses the same trunk rotation. It also makes carrying a bag on one side easier to manage.

### Why it matters
It builds core strength and rotational control with no equipment, so you can do it at home. That supports good posture and can help protect your lower back.
