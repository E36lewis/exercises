The camel is a kneeling backbend from yoga. You arch back with your hands on your heels, which stretches the front of the thighs, hip flexors, abdomen and chest.

### How to do it
1. Kneel on a mat with your knees hip-width apart and your thighs upright.
2. Place your hands on your lower back, with your fingers pointing down.
3. Lift your chest and press your hips forward.
4. Lean back and reach one hand, then the other, to your heels if you can.
5. Keep your hips over your knees and let your head rest in line with your spine.
6. Hold for a few breaths, then come up slowly with your hands on your hips.

**Tips:** Keep your hips over your knees and your neck relaxed. Go only as far as feels comfortable; keep your hands on your lower back if you cannot reach your heels. Skip or ease off if you feel pain in your lower back, neck or knees.

### In daily life
It opens the front of the body after a long time slouched over a desk or a phone.

### Why it matters
It can help keep the spine and hips mobile, which supports an upright, open posture.
