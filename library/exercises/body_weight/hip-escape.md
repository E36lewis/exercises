Also called a shrimp, this ground movement has you on your back, pushing off with your feet to slide your hips away. Grapplers use it to create space and escape from under an opponent.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat.
2. Turn onto your right side and place your right elbow and forearm on the floor.
3. Push off your feet to slide your hips back and away from your head.
4. Move your top knee up as you slide to make space.
5. Return to your back, then repeat toward the other side.
6. Move across the mat for a set distance.

**Tips:** Use a smooth mat. Move your hips, not your arms. Keep your neck relaxed and your chin tucked. Start slowly, as a partner can resist later.

### In daily life
It is the pattern for scooting along the floor on your back to reach something under the furniture.

### Why it matters
In combat sports, escaping from under an opponent starts with moving your hips. It builds the strength and coordination of the hips and trunk on the ground.
