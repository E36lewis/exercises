This is a body-weight push-up where you shift your weight over one hand as you lower. The triceps lead, and your chest and shoulders help.

### How to do it
1. Start in a high plank with your hands a little wider than your shoulders.
2. Squeeze your glutes and abs so your body is one straight line.
3. Shift your chest towards one hand as you bend your elbows.
4. Keep the elbow on the loaded side close to your ribs and let the other arm stay straighter.
5. Lower until your chest is just above the floor.
6. Press back to the middle, then lower towards the other side.

**Tips:** Keep your hips level and don't let them sag or twist. Keep your wrists under your hands. If it's too hard, drop to your knees or lean on a raised surface.

### In daily life
You lean more on one arm than the other when you push yourself up from the grass or lift yourself out of a low chair.

### Why it matters
Shifting your weight makes each arm take turns working harder. This can help build pushing strength and keep your shoulders and wrists under control.
