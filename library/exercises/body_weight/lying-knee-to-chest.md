Lying knee to chest is a gentle floor stretch. You pull one knee toward your chest to ease the lower back and the glutes.

### How to do it
1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat.
2. Lift one knee and clasp it with both hands, behind the thigh or on the shin.
3. Draw the knee toward your chest, keeping the other foot on the floor or the leg extended.
4. Keep your head and shoulders relaxed.
5. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds, breathing slowly.
6. Change sides, or hug both knees.

**Tips:** Keep the lower back flat on the floor. Pull gently. Don't clasp over the knee cap if it hurts.

### In daily life
A good way to unwind after a long day of sitting or standing, or on waking.

### Why it matters
It gives easy relief from lower back tightness for everyone, including runners, cyclists and lifters.
