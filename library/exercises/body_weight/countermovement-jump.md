A vertical jump that starts with a quick dip and an arm swing. The dip stores energy in the muscles and tendons so that you can jump higher. It is also a common test of leg power.

### How to do it
1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
2. Quickly bend your hips and knees to a quarter squat while swinging your arms back.
3. Immediately drive your arms up and jump as high as you can.
4. Extend your hips, knees and ankles fully in the air.
5. Land on both feet at once, bending your knees and sitting your hips back.
6. Reset and repeat.

**Tips:** Land softly and quietly, with your knees over your toes. Don't pause at the bottom of the dip. Jump on a non-slip floor, and stop when your jumps get lower.

### In daily life
It's the jump you do to reach something on a high shelf or to hop over an obstacle.

### Why it matters
Court and field sports are won by jumping, sprinting and changing direction. This jump trains the leg power for rebounds and headers. It is also a way to measure progress.
