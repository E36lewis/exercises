You lie face down with your arms in a W shape and squeeze your elbows and shoulder blades back. It strengthens the rotator cuff and the lower trapezius.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down on the floor with your elbows bent and your hands by your head, a W shape.
2. Rest your forehead on the floor.
3. Pull your shoulder blades down and together.
4. Lift your elbows and hands off the floor, rotating your forearms up.
5. Pause for a moment.
6. Lower slowly and repeat.

**Tips:** Keep your neck long. Lift with your upper back, not your lower back. Go only as high as you can control. Use no weight until it is smooth.

### In daily life
It supports the shoulder when you throw a ball, brush your hair or reach behind you.

### Why it matters
Throwing, swinging and swimming sports rely on a rotator cuff that keeps the shoulder joint centered. This drill builds that strength and shoulder blade control.
