The dip is a body weight push on parallel bars. You lower your body between two bars by bending your elbows and press back up. It builds the chest, shoulders and triceps.

### How to do it
1. Set parallel dip bars to a width that suits your shoulders. Grip them and lift yourself with straight arms.
2. Lean your trunk forward slightly to work the chest, or stay upright to work the triceps.
3. Bend your elbows and lower your body slowly until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor.
4. Keep your shoulders down and your elbows close to your body.
5. Push the bars away and straighten your arms to return.

**Tips:** Stay within a range where your shoulders feel fine. Going too deep stretches the front of the shoulder, and it is where most dip injuries occur. Do 3 sets of 5 to 12 reps, adding weight only when 12 are easy. Use assistance if you can't do 5. Don't swing or bounce at the bottom. Check that the bars are stable.

### In daily life
Pushing yourself up from a pool edge, out of a chair and onto a ledge all use the same pressing action.

### Why it matters
Collision, gymnastics, rock-climbing and combat athletes need to push their own body weight and to hold strong shoulders. Dips build upper-body pushing strength for lifts, ring work, blocks and strikes.
