The L sit is a hold in which your body is supported by straight arms on two bars and your legs are held out in front of you. Your torso and legs make an L shape. It builds compression strength in the trunk and hips, and strength in the shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Set the parallel dip bars to about shoulder width and stand between them.
2. Hold the bars, lock your elbows and lift your body off the floor.
3. Push the bars down and your shoulders away from your ears.
4. Lift your legs, knees bent at first, until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
5. Straighten your legs if you can, and keep your toes pointed.
6. Hold for the planned time, then lower yourself with control.

**Tips:** Start with knees bent and hold for 10 to 15 seconds. Build to straight legs and 20 to 30 seconds. Do 4 to 6 holds with 1 minute's rest. Keep the bars stable and stop if your wrists or shoulders hurt. Do not drop from the top.

### In daily life
Lifting yourself out of a pool, pushing up from a low chair and holding yourself on the arms of a seat all use this support.

### Why it matters
Gymnasts and rock climbers hold their body weight with straight arms while compressing the hips. The L sit is a staple of both because it builds the hip flexors, abdominals and shoulders in the shape that advanced skills need.
