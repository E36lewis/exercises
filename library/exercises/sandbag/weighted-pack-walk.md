A weighted pack walk is a steady walk while carrying a loaded sandbag or a rucksack. It builds the legs, back and heart for hiking and carrying work.

### How to do it
1. Fill a sandbag or a pack. Start with about 10 percent of your body weight.
2. Carry the bag high on your back, snug against your spine, or hug it to your chest.
3. Stand tall with your shoulders back and your eyes forward.
4. Walk at a brisk, steady pace with short, even steps.
5. Keep your breathing steady and swing your arms.
6. Keep going for the planned time, then set the bag down carefully.

**Tips:** A usual session is 20 to 60 minutes at a pace you can talk through, in heart-rate zone 2. Build the load and distance slowly, by no more than about 10 percent a week. Wear comfortable shoes, and avoid the load pulling you backward. Stop if your back hurts.

### In daily life
Carrying shopping, a backpack on a trip and bags of groceries from the car use the same steady loaded walking.

### Why it matters
Runners can use it for a low-impact aerobic and strength session. Hikers, hunters and anyone who carries a pack gain stamina and leg strength, which helps them cope with long days on foot.
