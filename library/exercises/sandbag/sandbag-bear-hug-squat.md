The sandbag bear hug squat is a squat holding a sandbag against your chest in both arms. The bag shifts and pulls you forward, so your trunk and upper back must work to keep you upright.

### How to do it
1. Lift a sandbag to your chest and wrap both arms around it, as if giving it a hug.
2. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly.
3. Hold the bag tight and brace your trunk.
4. Sit down by bending your hips and knees until your thighs are parallel to the floor, or lower if comfortable.
5. Keep your chest up and your elbows under the bag.
6. Push through your whole foot to stand.

**Tips:** Use a bag you can hold tightly, for example 20 to 40 kg, and do 3 to 4 sets of 6 to 10 reps. Keep your back flat and don't let the bag pull your shoulders forward. Set it down by bending your knees. Lock your fingers to avoid dropping it.

### In daily life
Picking up a big bag of soil, a child or a heavy box from the floor uses the same hug and stand.

### Why it matters
Combat and collision sports involve moving and holding a bulky, shifting weight, an opponent. This squat builds the legs and the trunk to do that.
