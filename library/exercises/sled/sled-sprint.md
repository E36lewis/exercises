The sled sprint is a short, hard sprint while pulling a light or moderate sled. The resistance makes you lean forward and push strongly, like the first steps of a sprint.

### How to do it
1. Load the sled lightly, about 10 to 20 percent of your body weight on turf, to start.
2. Attach the harness and lean forward, with the strap taut.
3. Start in a split stance, one foot ahead of the other.
4. Sprint 10 to 30 m, driving your knees and pushing the ground behind you.
5. Slow down gradually and unclip once the sled has stopped.
6. Walk back and rest fully.

**Tips:** Do 4 to 8 sprints with 1 to 3 minutes' rest between, so that each one is fast. Keep the load light enough that your form still looks like a sprint. Warm up first. Keep the area clear and don't stop abruptly, because the sled can slide into your heels.

### In daily life
Accelerating up a hill with a heavy pack or pushing off hard from a standstill uses the same driving action.

### Why it matters
Field, collision, court and run sports are decided in the first few steps. Resisted sprints load the drive phase and build the strength needed to accelerate.
