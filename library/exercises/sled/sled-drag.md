The sled drag is walking forward while pulling a loaded sled behind you by a harness or straps. The legs push the ground away with every step. It is loaded walking that is easy on the joints and has almost no lowering phase.

### How to do it
1. Attach a harness or a long strap to a sled and load it with plates.
2. Face away from the sled and put the harness on, or hold the straps with your hands.
3. Lean slightly forward, with your chest up and your back flat.
4. Walk forward with steady, strong steps, pushing through your whole foot.
5. Walk 20 to 40 m, then turn the sled around and walk back.

**Tips:** Start with a load you can drag at a brisk walk without your form changing. A usual plan is 4 to 6 drags of 20 to 40 m with 1 to 2 minutes' rest. Keep the strap low and away from your feet, and make sure nobody is in the path.

### In daily life
Hauling a heavy load of firewood or pulling a loaded cart over soft ground uses the same pattern.

### Why it matters
Field, collision, run and skate sports need strong legs that cope with many steps. Sled drags add that strength and conditioning without pounding, and they can be done year round.
