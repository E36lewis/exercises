The trap bar deadlift is a heavy lift from the floor. You stand inside a hexagonal bar and lift it by the side handles. The load hangs beside your body instead of in front, so it is easier on the lower back and works the thighs more than a standard deadlift.

### How to do it
1. Stand in the middle of the trap bar with your feet about hip-width apart.
2. Push your hips back, bend your knees and grip the handles, with your chest up and your back flat.
3. Take a breath and brace your core. Push the floor away with your feet.
4. Stand fully upright, with your hips and knees finishing together.
5. Lower the bar by pushing your hips back and bending your knees, and set it down under control.

**Tips:** Do not round your lower back or jerk the bar off the floor. Keep the bar close and your shoulders slightly in front of the handles. Load plates evenly, use collars, and keep your feet clear of the plates.

### In daily life
It is the pattern for lifting a heavy box, a bag of soil or a piece of furniture from the floor.

### Why it matters
It builds strength and power in the hips and legs with less back strain than a straight bar, so it serves almost every sport, from sprinting and jumping to tackling and climbing.
