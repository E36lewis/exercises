This is a one-leg hip hinge with your foot on a Bosu ball. It works the glutes and hamstrings, and the core and ankle muscles work to keep you steady on the wobbling dome.

### How to do it
1. Stand with one foot in the centre of the Bosu ball and the other foot off the floor.
2. Keep a slight bend in your standing knee.
3. Hinge forward and extend the free leg straight behind you.
4. Lower your torso while keeping your back flat.
5. Squeeze your glutes to stand tall again.
6. Finish your reps, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Move slowly and keep your hips square. Keep a wall or rail within reach. Start with no weights.

### In daily life
You make small corrections like this when you step onto a wobbly surface, such as a boat deck or a loose gravel path.

### Why it matters
The soft dome asks the small muscles of your foot and ankle to react, and this can help your balance and control at your hips.
