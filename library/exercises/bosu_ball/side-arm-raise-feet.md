This is a lateral raise done while standing on a Bosu ball. You lift your arms out to the sides to shoulder height. It works the shoulders (deltoids) and upper trapezius, while your core and legs work to keep you balanced on the unsteady surface.

### How to do it
1. Stand on the Bosu ball (dome up or flat side up, as you prefer) with your feet hip-width apart and your knees slightly bent.
2. Let your arms hang by your sides.
3. Brace your core.
4. Lift your arms out to the sides up to shoulder height.
5. Pause briefly.
6. Lower slowly.

**Tips:** Keep your shoulders away from your ears. Don't swing your arms. Get steady on the ball first, and stand near a wall in case you wobble.

### In daily life
Raising your arms to catch your balance on a bus or a slippery floor uses the same shoulders and the same quick adjustments in your feet.

### Why it matters
It builds shoulder strength and trains balance and core control together, which supports stability.
