A push-up with your hands on a Bosu ball (a half-dome balance trainer). It works the chest, shoulders and triceps, while the forearms, shoulder blade muscles and trunk work to steady the wobble.

### How to do it
1. Place your hands on the Bosu, on the dome or on the flat side turned up, shoulder-width apart.
2. Step back into a straight line from head to heels.
3. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
4. Lower your chest toward the ball with your elbows at about 45 degrees.
5. Stop with your chest just above your hands.
6. Press back up, keeping the Bosu steady.

**Tips:** Keep your body rigid and control the wobble. Move slowly. The flat side up is the more demanding version, so begin with the dome up.

### In daily life
Leaning on soft or tilting surfaces, like pushing up from a mattress or a cushion, calls for the same quick adjustments in the wrist and shoulder.

### Why it matters
Because the surface moves, your wrists and shoulders have to react constantly, which can build joint control as well as pushing strength.
