This is a one-leg sideways jump using a Bosu ball. It works the thighs, calves and glutes, and your hips and core help keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg beside the Bosu ball.
2. Bend your knee slightly and sit back a little.
3. Hop sideways and land softly on the same leg.
4. Hold your balance briefly before you move.
5. Hop back and repeat, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Land softly with a bent knee, tracking over your toes. Start with small hops. Keep your chest up and your hips level.

### In daily life
You make this move when you dodge sideways to avoid a cyclist or step quickly out of the way of a closing door.

### Why it matters
Sideways power and control are used in many sports, and training them can help your hips and ankles handle quick changes of direction.
