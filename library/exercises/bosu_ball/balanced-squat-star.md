The balanced squat star combines a single-leg squat on a BOSU ball with reaching your free foot out to different points, like a star. It works the thighs, hips, calves and the small muscles that keep your ankle and hip steady.

### How to do it
1. Place a BOSU ball dome-side up near a wall or support.
2. Stand on the dome on your left leg with your right leg lifted.
3. Bend your left knee and hips to lower into a partial squat.
4. Reach your right foot forward and tap lightly, then return.
5. Repeat to the side and then behind you, as if tracing a star.
6. Stand back up through your left foot, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Keep your standing knee in line with your toes and your hips level. Tap lightly without putting weight on the reaching foot. This is hard, so stay near a support and begin with a shallow squat.

### In daily life
Reaching with one foot while standing on the other is what you do when you step over a curb, a toy or a puddle without losing your balance.

### Why it matters
It can help build single-leg strength and control, including the muscles at the outer hip and ankle that guard against rolled ankles and wobbly knees.
