This is a one-leg forward jump using a Bosu ball for the hop or the landing. It works your calves, hamstrings and glutes, and your core and ankles keep you steady.

### How to do it
1. Stand on one leg beside the Bosu ball.
2. Bend your knee slightly and swing your arms back.
3. Hop forward and land softly on the same leg.
4. Stabilise for a moment, holding your balance.
5. Reset and repeat, then switch legs.

**Tips:** Land softly with a bent knee and keep your knee in line with your toes. Start with small hops. Make sure the ball is steady before you land.

### In daily life
You hop like this when you jump over a puddle or land on one foot after hopping off a kerb.

### Why it matters
Learning to land on one leg can help build power and control in your ankles and knees, which can support steadier movement in running and sport.
