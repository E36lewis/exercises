This is a static leg exercise. You lean your back on a Bosu ball against a wall and hold a squat position. It works the thighs, hamstrings and glutes, while your calves and core help you stay steady on the ball.

### How to do it
1. Place the Bosu ball against a wall and lean your lower back on it, with your feet about shoulder-width apart and a step forward from the wall.
2. Slide down until your knees are bent to about 90 degrees.
3. Keep your knees over your ankles and your feet flat.
4. Hold the position, breathing steadily.
5. Push through your feet to stand back up.

**Tips:** Keep your heels down and your knees in line with your toes. Don't let your knees fall inward. Stop if you feel knee pain, and slide up carefully.

### In daily life
Waiting in line, crouching to check a low cupboard and staying low to garden all ask your legs to hold a bent position for a while.

### Why it matters
Holding a squat builds leg endurance. The ball behind you adds a small balance challenge, which supports knee and hip stability.
