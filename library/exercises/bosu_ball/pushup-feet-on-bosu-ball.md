A push-up with your feet on a Bosu ball (a half-dome balance trainer) and your hands on the floor. It works the chest, shoulders and triceps, while your core and legs balance on the wobbly dome. With your feet raised, more of your weight rests on your shoulders.

### How to do it
1. Place your feet on the dome side of the Bosu, with the flat side down.
2. Put your hands on the floor, a little wider than shoulder-width.
3. Make a straight line from head to heels.
4. Brace your abdomen and glutes.
5. Lower your chest toward the floor.
6. Press back up while keeping your hips level.

**Tips:** Master regular push-ups and push-ups with feet on a bench first. Use a clear space and a non-slip floor. Stop if your shoulders or wrists feel pinched.

### In daily life
Staying stable when your feet are on uneven ground, such as stepping onto a boat or loose gravel, uses the balance and control trained here.

### Why it matters
Raising the feet shifts the load toward the shoulders, and the unstable base can help train your trunk to stay still.
