The balanced squat is a squat done standing on a BOSU ball, an unstable half-dome. It works the thighs, glutes and calves, and the wobble adds a balance challenge for your ankles, hips and trunk.

### How to do it
1. Place the BOSU dome-side up on a flat floor, near a wall or support.
2. Step onto the center with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
3. Find your balance and hold your arms out in front.
4. Push your hips back and bend your knees, lowering into a squat.
5. Keep your chest lifted and knees over your toes.
6. Drive through your feet to stand back up.

**Tips:** Move slowly and keep your weight spread across your feet. Do not let your knees cave in. Start with a shallow squat and keep something to hold close by in case you lose balance.

### In daily life
The squat is how you sit on a low couch, while the wobble is like standing up on a moving train or bus.

### Why it matters
Squatting on an unstable surface can help train the small muscles around the ankle and hip that keep you steady, as well as the big leg muscles.
