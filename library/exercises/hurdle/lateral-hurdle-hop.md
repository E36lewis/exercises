The lateral hurdle hop is a side-to-side hop over a low hurdle. You push off from the sides of your feet and land on the other side with control. It builds power and control at the ankle and knee.

### How to do it
1. Place a low hurdle, 15 to 30 cm, on the floor and stand beside it.
2. Stand on both feet, knees soft and weight on the balls of your feet.
3. Swing your arms and jump sideways over the hurdle.
4. Land on both feet, with your knees pointing in the direction of your toes.
5. Pause for a second to balance, then jump back.
6. Repeat for the planned reps.

**Tips:** Start with a low hurdle and two-footed landings. Progress to a faster rhythm without pausing, and later to one-legged hops. Do 3 sets of 6 to 10 hops. Land softly with knees bent, never letting them fall inward. Use a firm, non-slip surface.

### In daily life
Jumping sideways over a puddle, hopping over a low obstacle on a path and a quick sidestep all call for this skill.

### Why it matters
Court, skate and snow sports require sideways power and a stable knee on landing. This hop trains both and works as a prehab tool for ankles and knees.
