The stair climber is a cardio machine with steps that keep moving downward, so you climb continuously. It raises your heart rate and works your glutes, thighs and calves with low impact on your joints.

### How to do it
1. Step onto the machine, hold the rails lightly for balance and start at a slow speed.
2. Stand tall with your chest up and place your whole foot on each step.
3. Climb at a steady pace that lets you talk but not sing.
4. Increase the speed or time gradually as you improve.
5. Slow down for a minute at the end to cool down.

**Tips:** Do not lean on the handrails or lean far forward, because that lowers the work your legs do. Keep your whole foot on the step and your back straight. Hold on lightly and keep your steps even.

### In daily life
It is the same as climbing many flights of stairs, a hill or a long set of steps at a stadium.

### Why it matters
Running sports benefit from steady uphill work that builds the legs and the heart with less pounding than running.
