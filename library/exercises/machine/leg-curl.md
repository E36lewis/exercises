The leg curl is a machine exercise that isolates the hamstrings. You bend your knees against a pad to pull your heels toward your buttocks. It is a simple way to build hamstring strength without loading the back.

### How to do it
1. Adjust the machine so the pad rests on the back of your lower legs, just above your heels, and the knee joint lines up with the machine's pivot.
2. Sit or lie down and hold the handles.
3. Curl your heels toward your buttocks by bending your knees.
4. Pause at the top, squeezing your hamstrings.
5. Lower the pad slowly until your legs are almost straight.

**Tips:** Move smoothly and do not swing the weight or lift your hips. Lower slowly, because that part builds strength too. Choose a weight you can control for the full range.

### In daily life
It trains the muscles you use to bend your knees when you climb stairs or kick your leg back.

### Why it matters
Field, collision and snow sports often involve hamstring strains. A strong hamstring helps balance the quadriceps and protect the knee.
