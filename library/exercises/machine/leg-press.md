A machine exercise where you push a weighted platform away with your feet while seated or reclined. It mainly works the quadriceps (the front of your thighs), with the glutes, inner thighs and calves helping.

### How to do it
1. Sit in the machine with your back and hips flat against the pad.
2. Place your feet on the platform, about shoulder-width apart.
3. Release the safety handles and hold the grips.
4. Lower the platform slowly by bending your knees, keeping your lower back on the pad.
5. Stop when your knees are bent comfortably, before your hips tuck under.
6. Push through your whole foot to straighten your legs, without locking your knees.

**Tips:** Keep your knees tracking over your toes and your hips down. Avoid locking out your knees. Set the safety stops and don't load more than you can control.

### In daily life
It trains the same straight-legged push you use to stand up from a low sofa or to shove a heavy box across the floor.

### Why it matters
Because the machine supports your back and guides the path, you can load your legs heavily without having to balance a bar, which makes it useful for building leg strength.
