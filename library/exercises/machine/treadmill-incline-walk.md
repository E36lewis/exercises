The treadmill incline walk is hill walking in the gym. You set the treadmill to a slope and walk at a steady pace. It raises your heart rate and works the glutes and calves with low impact on the joints.

### How to do it
1. Set the treadmill to a gentle slope and a comfortable walking speed.
2. Step on and begin walking while holding the rails only to find your balance.
3. Raise the incline slowly to a level that challenges you but lets you keep good form.
4. Walk upright with your arms swinging naturally.
5. Slow the belt and lower the incline for a short cool-down.

**Tips:** Do not hold the rails or lean forward heavily, because this takes work away from your legs. Keep your steps natural and your head up. Increase the slope before the speed.

### In daily life
It copies the effort of walking up a hill or a long ramp.

### Why it matters
Running and hiking sports benefit from hill work that builds the calves, glutes and heart. It gives hill training with less pounding than running.
