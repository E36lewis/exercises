The glute ham raise is a hamstring and back exercise done on a special machine. With your feet locked in and your knees on a pad, you lower your body forward and curl back up. It strengthens the hamstrings at the knee and hip together and is a progression from the Nordic curl.

### How to do it
1. Adjust the machine so your feet are held firmly against the plate and your hips sit just beyond the pad.
2. Start with your body upright and your arms crossed on your chest.
3. Slowly lower your torso toward the floor, keeping your body straight from knees to head.
4. Use your hamstrings and glutes to curl and lift your body back to the start.
5. Keep your hips from folding and avoid arching your lower back.

**Tips:** Keep a straight line from knees to head. If it is too hard, hold your hands out front or push off the floor. Build up the number of reps slowly, since sore hamstrings are likely at first.

### In daily life
It builds the strength for sprinting up a hill or catching yourself when you slip.

### Why it matters
Field, collision, running and court sports all have a high rate of hamstring injuries. Heavy, slow hamstring work like this helps protect them.
