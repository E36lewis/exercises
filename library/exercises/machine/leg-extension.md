The leg extension is a machine exercise that isolates the quadriceps. You sit and straighten your knees against a padded bar. It is used to build thigh strength and in knee rehabilitation.

### How to do it
1. Sit on the machine and adjust the back so your knees line up with the pivot.
2. Place the pad on the front of your lower shins, just above your ankles.
3. Hold the handles and brace your core.
4. Straighten your knees until your legs are nearly straight, and pause.
5. Lower slowly back to the start.

**Tips:** Do not swing the weight or lock your knees hard at the top. Lower slowly. If you have knee pain or have had surgery, ask a clinician about a safe range and load.

### In daily life
It trains the muscles that straighten your knee when you stand from a chair or kick.

### Why it matters
Snow and court sports put heavy demands on the quadriceps for absorbing landings and turns. The leg extension builds them, and is a common knee rehab exercise.
