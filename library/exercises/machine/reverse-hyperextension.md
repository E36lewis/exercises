A machine exercise where you lie face down with your hips at the edge and lift your legs behind you. It works the glutes and the muscles along your lower back, with the hamstrings helping.

### How to do it
1. Lie face down on the machine, with your upper body supported and your hips at the edge.
2. Hold the handles.
3. Let your legs hang down.
4. Brace your abdomen.
5. Lift your legs until they're in line with your body, squeezing your glutes.
6. Lower slowly, without letting the weight swing.

**Tips:** Don't swing your legs or lift higher than your body line, as arching your lower back strains it. Use a light weight at first. Stop if you feel lower back pain.

### In daily life
The motion of straightening your hips behind you is the same as standing up from a lift, or from a bend over a sink.

### Why it matters
It can build the glutes and the muscles that hold your spine upright, and it moves your hips and spine without loading your back with a heavy weight on top.
