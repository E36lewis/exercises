A small, pyramid-shaped muscle over the bridge of the nose, between the eyebrows. It runs from the nasal bone and the cartilage beside it up into the skin of the lower forehead.

### What it does
- Pulls the inner ends of the eyebrows downward
- Wrinkles the skin across the bridge of the nose into horizontal lines

### In daily life
It works when you frown, squint into bright light, concentrate hard on small print or show disapproval.

### Why it matters
It is a facial expression muscle with no part in posture or strength training. It supports how we show focus and effort, and relaxing it can be a cue for easing tension in the face.
