A thick muscle lying under the biceps on the front of the upper arm. It runs from the lower front of the humerus to the ulna, just below the elbow.

### What it does
- Bends the elbow with the forearm in any position: palm up, palm down or thumbs up.
- Is the main elbow flexor, with the biceps and brachioradialis helping.

### In daily life
It does much of the work when you lift a full saucepan, carry a toddler on your hip, or lower a heavy box to the floor.

### Why it matters
Because it attaches to the ulna, it is not affected by how the forearm is turned, so it takes much of the load in every grip and adds to arm size and pulling strength. It is supplied mainly by the musculocutaneous nerve, with a small share from the radial nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Hammer curl, Bicep curl, Incline bicep curl, Chin up, Pull up, Inverted row
