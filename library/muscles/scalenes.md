Three muscles (anterior, middle and posterior) at the side of the neck. They run from the neck vertebrae to the first two ribs. The nerves and artery that supply the arm pass between the anterior and middle scalenes.

### What it does
- Side-bend the neck
- Flex the neck forward and rotate it slightly
- Lift the first two ribs to help breathing in

### In daily life
They work when you tilt your head toward your shoulder, take a deep breath or lift your chest during a hard effort such as a sprint.

### Why it matters
They help move the neck and help you breathe, especially when breathing hard. If they feel tight, easing them gently can help neck comfort.
