A short muscle in the sole of the foot, on the inner side below the big toe. It divides into two heads, and each tendon contains a small sesamoid bone, which acts as a pulley under the ball of the foot.

### What it does
- Bends the big toe at its base joint.
- Helps the big toe press into the ground as you push off.
- Shares the load under the ball of the foot with the sesamoids.

### In daily life
It lets you press through the big toe when you climb stairs, spring into a jump or stand on tiptoe.

### Why it matters
It protects the sesamoid bones and helps the big toe carry body weight. Pain under the big toe joint in runners and dancers often involves this area. It is supplied by the medial plantar nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flexor, Lateral leap, Twisting jump
