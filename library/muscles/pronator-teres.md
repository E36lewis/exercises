A rounded muscle on the upper front of the forearm. It runs diagonally from the inner side of the elbow (medial epicondyle, with a second head from the ulna) to the middle of the outer forearm bone, the radius.

### What it does
- Pronates the forearm (turns the palm down)
- Helps bend the elbow, weakly

### In daily life
It helps when you turn a screwdriver, pour a drink from a bottle or turn your palm down to push up from a chair arm.

### Why it matters
It supports forearm rotation and gives the inner elbow some control during throwing and gripping. It is also a muscle that can get tired with a lot of repeated wrist turning, such as with hand tools.

### Exercises that train it
Bicep curl, reverse bicep curl, pushup hands on bosu ball
