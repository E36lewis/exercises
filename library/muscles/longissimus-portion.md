The middle column of the erector spinae, the long muscle strips beside the spine. It is the longest of the three. It starts on the sacrum and lumbar spine and runs up to the ribs and neck vertebrae. Its top part, the longissimus capitis, reaches the mastoid, the bony lump behind the ear.

### What it does
- Extends the spine and the head.
- Bends the trunk and neck to the same side.
- Helps turn the head toward the same side.

### In daily life
Keeping your head up while you read on a train, bending down to tie a shoe and standing back up, and staying upright while you carry shopping.

### Why it matters
It carries a lot of the day's postural load. Training it can help the back share the stress of lifting and running.

### Exercises that train it
Back extension, Hyperextension, Bird dog, Romanian deadlift, Cat stretch, Running
