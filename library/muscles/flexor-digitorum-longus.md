A long muscle deep in the back of the calf. It starts on the back of the tibia, runs behind the inner ankle bone and across the sole, then splits into four tendons that go to the tips of the four lesser toes.

### What it does
- Curls the four lesser toes down (flexion).
- Helps point the foot down (plantar flexion) and turn the sole inward (inversion).
- Supports the arch of the foot as you push off.

### In daily life
It helps you stand steady on tiptoe, keep your toes on the ground while walking uphill and balance on a narrow beam or curb.

### Why it matters
Because the tendon runs behind the inner ankle, it helps hold up the arch, and strain there is a common site of inner-ankle pain. It is supplied by the tibial nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flex, Gap jump, Double heisman, Toe object pick up, High knees, Running
