A long muscle on the back of the forearm. Its tendon turns around a small bony bump on the radius at the wrist (Lister's tubercle) and runs to the tip of the thumb. It forms the back edge of the anatomical snuffbox.

### What it does
- Straightens (extends) the end joint of the thumb.
- Helps extend the thumb at its base.
- Helps pull the thumb back toward the index finger, in line with the palm.

### In daily life
It gives you the thumbs-up, lifts the thumb off a keyboard and lets you flatten your hand on a table.

### Why it matters
Because the tendon changes direction at the wrist, it can wear or rupture after a wrist fracture or with repeated stress. Its strength helps you control thumb extension and grip release. It is supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Lean back forearm, Side to side forearm
