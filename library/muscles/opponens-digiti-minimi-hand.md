A small triangular muscle at the base of the little finger, in the muscle pad on the outer edge of the palm. It runs from the hook of the hamate (a wrist bone) to the fifth metacarpal, the bone behind the little finger.

### What it does
- Draws the fifth metacarpal forward and across the palm.
- Deepens the hollow of the palm.
- Brings the little finger towards the thumb.
- Helps the little finger work in a cupped grip.

### In daily life
Gripping a mug or a jar lid, holding a ball and cupping water in your hand.

### Why it matters
Cupping the palm lets the hand wrap around objects. The little finger side of the grip is also important for holding tools and bars firmly.
