The large fan-shaped chest muscle. It has a collarbone part (clavicular head) and a breastbone-and-ribs part (sternocostal head), and both run to a flat tendon on the upper arm bone, just below the shoulder.

### What it does
- Brings the arm across the body (horizontal adduction).
- Lifts the arm forward, mostly with the collarbone fibres.
- Turns the arm inward.
- Pulls the raised arm down, with the sternocostal fibres.

### In daily life
Pushing a door or a heavy cart, hugging someone and getting up from the floor with your hands.

### Why it matters
It is the main muscle behind pressing and pushing strength. Different bench angles shift work between the upper (collarbone) and lower (sternum) parts.

### Exercises that train it
Bench press, Incline bench press, Chest press, Push up, Incline flye, Wide chest
