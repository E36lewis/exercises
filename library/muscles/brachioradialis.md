A long muscle on the thumb side of the forearm, making the rounded bulge just below the elbow. It runs from the outer lower end of the humerus down to the lower end of the radius near the wrist.

### What it does
- Bends the elbow, most strongly with the thumb up (a neutral grip).
- Brings the forearm back toward the thumbs-up position from palm-up or palm-down.

### In daily life
It works when you raise a cup of tea, hammer a nail, or carry a briefcase with your thumb on top.

### Why it matters
Its long lever arm makes it best suited for fast or heavy lifts in a neutral grip, and it adds to forearm size. It is supplied by the radial nerve, one of the few elbow flexors that is not supplied by the musculocutaneous nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Hammer curl, Reverse bicep curl, Chin up, Pull up, Inverted row, Row
