A long, slender forearm muscle with a short belly and a long tendon that runs to the palm. You can see the tendon at the wrist when you touch your thumb to your little finger and bend the wrist. It is missing in some people, with no known loss of strength.

### What it does
- Weakly bends the wrist.
- Tightens the palm's fascia, the tough sheet under the skin.
- Helps cup the hand.

### In daily life
Gripping a bar and cupping your hand to carry water.

### Why it matters
Its absence causes no noticeable loss of grip strength, which is why surgeons often use its tendon as a graft. Other wrist flexors do its job.

### Exercises that train it
Reverse bicep curl
