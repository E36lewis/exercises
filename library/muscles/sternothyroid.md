A thin strap muscle at the front of the neck, lying deeper than the sternohyoid. It runs from the back of the top of the breastbone to the thyroid cartilage, the firm part of the larynx you feel as the Adam's apple.

### What it does
- Pulls the larynx (voice box) down
- Helps return the larynx to rest after swallowing
- May help lower the pitch of the voice by lengthening the vocal folds

### In daily life
It works when you swallow food, hum a low note or clear your throat, bringing the voice box back down after each lift.

### Why it matters
It supports the position of the larynx during swallowing and voice use. It is a small helper, not a muscle you train directly, but it shows how neck movement and voice share the same structures.
