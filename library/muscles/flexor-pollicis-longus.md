A long, thin muscle deep in the front of the forearm. It starts on the radius (the forearm bone on the thumb side) and the tough membrane between the two forearm bones. Its tendon runs through the wrist and along the thumb to the tip. It is the thumb's own long flexor, set apart from the finger flexors beside it.

### What it does
- Bends the tip joint of the thumb. No other muscle does this.
- Helps bend the thumb's lower joints and, weakly, the wrist.
- Gives the thumb pad its force when it presses against the fingers.

### In daily life
Pressing a button on a phone, pinching a coin off a table, holding a pen, squeezing a tube of toothpaste, or turning a key in a lock all load this tendon.

### Why it matters
Pinch strength depends on it. A strong, well-conditioned thumb flexor can help you keep hold of a bar, a rope or a heavy bag without the thumb giving way.

### Exercises that train it
Farmer carry, Wrist curl palm up, Lean back forearm, Side to side forearm, Alternating hang clean, Single arm clean
