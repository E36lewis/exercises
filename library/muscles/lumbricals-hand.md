Four small muscles in the palm. They start on the tendons of flexor digitorum profundus, the deep finger flexor, and run along the thumb side of each finger to join the tendons on the back of the fingers. They are unusual because they attach to tendons, not to bone.

### What it does
- Bend the finger knuckles (the joints at the base of the fingers).
- Straighten the middle and end finger joints at the same time.
- Allow the "table top" or "hook" hand shapes, where the knuckles bend while the fingers stay straight.
- Help fine finger movement.

### In daily life
Typing, writing, buttoning a shirt, picking up a coin and playing a keyboard or guitar.

### Why it matters
Their pairing of bending and straightening is what lets the fingers move precisely. They are used in tasks that need control more than force.
