Four small muscles in the sole of the foot. They start on the tendons of flexor digitorum longus, deep in the sole, and run along the inner side of the four smaller toes to the tendon on top of each toe.

### What it does
- Bend the toes at the base joints (where the toes meet the foot).
- Straighten the toes at the joints nearer the tips.
- Keep the toes from curling under as the heel lifts and you push off.
- Fine-tune pressure under the ball of the foot.

### In daily life
Gripping the ground when you balance on one leg, pushing off during a walk or run, and keeping your toes flat inside a shoe.

### Why it matters
Healthy lumbricals can help you stay stable on the front of the foot and push off cleanly. Toe exercises such as spreading and gently curling the toes can help train them.
