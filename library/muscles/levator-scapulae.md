A strap-like muscle at the back and side of the neck. It starts on the side projections of the top four neck vertebrae and runs down and back to the upper inner corner of the shoulder blade. Its name means lifter of the scapula (shoulder blade).

### What it does
- Lifts the shoulder blade (elevation).
- Rotates the blade so its lower tip moves toward the spine.
- Bends and turns the neck to the same side when the shoulder blade is fixed.

### In daily life
Shrugging, carrying a bag on one shoulder, turning to check a blind spot, and holding a phone against your shoulder.

### Why it matters
Long hours at a desk can leave this muscle tight. Mobility work and balanced shoulder strength can help neck comfort and a steady shoulder position.

### Exercises that train it
Shrug, Upright row, Row, Incline row, Halo, Windmill
