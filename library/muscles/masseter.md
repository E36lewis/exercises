A thick, rectangular chewing muscle in the cheek. It runs from the cheekbone (zygomatic arch) down to the outer surface of the angle of the lower jaw. You can feel it tighten just in front of your ear when you clench your teeth.

### What it does
- Closes the jaw by lifting the lower jaw.
- Clenches the teeth.
- Helps push the jaw forward and move it from side to side.
- Provides much of the force of biting.

### In daily life
Chewing tough food, biting into an apple, talking and clenching your teeth when you are focused or stressed.

### Why it matters
It is very strong for its size and does most of the work of chewing. If your jaw feels tight, relaxing it and avoiding constant clenching can help.
