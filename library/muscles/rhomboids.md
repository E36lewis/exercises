Rhomboid major and minor lie between the spine and the shoulder blade, under the trapezius. They run from the lower neck and upper thoracic spine to the inner edge of the scapula.

### What it does
- Retract the scapula (pull the shoulder blades together)
- Elevate the scapula slightly
- Rotate the scapula downward and hold it against the ribs

### In daily life
They work when you pull a door open, row a boat, or sit up tall and draw your shoulders back.

### Why it matters
They balance the chest muscles that pull the shoulders forward, which supports good posture. They also steady the shoulder blade during any pulling work, such as rows and pull-ups.

### Exercises that train it
Pendlay row, incline row, inverted row, banded pull apart, pull up, renegade row
