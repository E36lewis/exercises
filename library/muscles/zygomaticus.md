A pair of slim facial muscles on each side, the major and minor zygomaticus. They run from the cheekbone (zygomatic bone) diagonally down to the corner of the mouth and upper lip.

### What it does
- Lifts the corner of the mouth up and back (zygomaticus major)
- Lifts the upper lip (zygomaticus minor)
- Makes a smile or laugh

### In daily life
It works when you greet a friend with a smile, laugh at a joke or show you are pleased in a photo.

### Why it matters
It is an expression muscle, so it supports communication rather than strength. It is not trained with exercises, but it is a good reminder that small facial muscles carry much of our social signalling.
