A small suboccipital muscle at the base of the skull. It runs from the transverse process of the first neck vertebra (the atlas) up to the skull, behind the ear.

### What it does
- Tilts the head back (extension).
- Bends the head to the same side.
- Fine-tunes head position.
- Helps steady the head on the neck.

### In daily life
Looking up at a shelf, levelling your gaze after you tilt your head, and balancing your head when you walk.

### Why it matters
It supports small adjustments at the skull-neck joint and the sense of where your head is in space. Tension in the suboccipitals is often felt as an ache at the base of the skull.

### Exercises that train it
Neck banded hold, Neck pull
