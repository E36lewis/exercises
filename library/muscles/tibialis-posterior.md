The deepest muscle at the back of the lower leg, lying between the calf muscles and the shin bones. Its tendon passes behind the inner ankle bone and fans out under the arch of the foot.

### What it does
- Inverts the foot (turns the sole inward)
- Plantar flexes the ankle (points the foot) weakly
- Supports the inner arch and helps hold the foot steady during push-off

### In daily life
It works when you walk on a sloping path, push off the ground during a run and balance on one foot with your ankle wobbling.

### Why it matters
It is a key arch support, so it helps the foot stay firm when it takes your weight. If it weakens, the arch can drop, and strengthening it may help foot and ankle stability.

### Exercises that train it
Calf hop, high knees, lunge, goblet squat, swing kick, cross hop hold
