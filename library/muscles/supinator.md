A short, flat muscle on the outer side of the forearm just below the elbow. It wraps around the upper part of the radius from the outer elbow and the top of the ulna.

### What it does
- Supinates the forearm (turns the palm up)
- Does this slowly and without much load, whatever the elbow angle
- Hands over to the biceps when the elbow is bent and the turn is hard

### In daily life
It works when you turn a door handle, rotate a key in a lock, tip a spoon up toward your mouth or turn your palm up to receive change.

### Why it matters
Its nerve (the posterior interosseous nerve) passes through the muscle, so this area is a common place for nerve irritation. A strong supinator can support forearm control in racket sports, climbing and tool work.
