A band of muscle along the inner edge of the sole. It runs from the heel bone to the inside of the base of the big toe and makes the visible bulge on the inner side of the foot.

### What it does
- Moves the big toe away from the second toe (abduction).
- Helps bend the big toe at its base.
- Supports the inner (medial longitudinal) arch of the foot.

### In daily life
It keeps the big toe pointing straight ahead as you walk, and it helps the foot stay steady when you rise onto your toes to reach a high shelf or balance on one leg.

### Why it matters
It holds the big toe in line and takes some load off the inner arch, which can help the foot absorb the shock of each step. It is supplied by the medial plantar nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flexor, Foot arch roll, Running
