The deepest layer of the thin muscles between the ribs. They sit on the inside of the chest wall, with the intercostal nerves and vessels running between them and the layer above. Their fibres run in the same direction as the internal intercostals, and they are often treated as part of that layer.

### What it does
- Draw neighbouring ribs together.
- Help lower the ribs during forceful breathing out.
- Keep the spaces between the ribs firm as pressure changes in the chest.

### In daily life
Blowing up a balloon, laughing hard, coughing, and getting your breath back after climbing a flight of stairs.

### Why it matters
Their exact role in quiet breathing is still debated, but they help the rib cage act as a stable frame. That can support trunk stability when you brace.

### Exercises that train it
Press sit up, Banded core twist
