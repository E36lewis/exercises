A muscle on the front of the forearm. It starts at the inner elbow on the common flexor tendon and runs down to the base of the second metacarpal, the bone behind the index finger. Its tendon is easy to feel at the wrist, just to the thumb side of the middle.

### What it does
- Bends the wrist (flexion).
- Tilts the wrist toward the thumb side (radial deviation).
- Helps turn the palm down (pronation).

### In daily life
It flicks the wrist when you pour from a jug, hit a nail, throw a ball or pull on a rope.

### Why it matters
Together with the flexor carpi ulnaris it makes the wrist a stable base for gripping. Overuse of this group at the inner elbow is a common cause of golfer's elbow. It is supplied by the median nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Wrist curl palm up, Farmer carry, Side to side forearm, Bicep curl, Hammer curl
