A circular muscle around the eye, in the eyelids and the bony rim of the eye socket. It has an outer orbital part and an inner palpebral part in the lids, and is served by the facial nerve.

### What it does
- Closes the eyelids.
- Squeezes the eyes shut (the orbital part).
- Blinks the eyes gently (the palpebral part).
- Helps move tears towards the tear duct.

### In daily life
Blinking, squinting in bright sunlight, winking and screwing up your eyes at a sneeze.

### Why it matters
It protects the eye from dust, light and drying out. The "smile lines" at the outer corners of the eyes come from it.
