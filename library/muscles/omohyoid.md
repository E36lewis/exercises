A thin strap muscle at the front of the neck with two bellies joined by a middle tendon. It runs from the upper edge of the shoulder blade, across the side of the neck, to the hyoid bone, and its angled path looks like a "V".

### What it does
- Lowers (depresses) the hyoid bone.
- Steadies the hyoid during swallowing.
- Tenses the deep layer of neck fascia, which can help keep the big neck veins open.

### In daily life
Swallowing, speaking and breathing in deeply.

### Why it matters
It helps position the hyoid bone so the other muscles of swallowing and speech can pull against a stable base. It works as part of the infrahyoid ("strap") muscle group.
