Three small muscles between the hand bones (metacarpals) on the palm side. Each starts on the side of one metacarpal and attaches to the finger it faces, so each pulls its finger towards the middle finger. The thumb has its own muscles.

### What it does
- Brings the fingers together (adduction), towards the middle finger.
- Helps bend the knuckles when they work with the lumbricals.
- Helps straighten the end finger joints through the finger tendons.

### In daily life
Holding a sheet of paper between your fingers, closing your fingers around a pencil and gripping a bar.

### Why it matters
The interossei give the fingers sideways control. Squeezing a small ball or a sheet of paper between fingers is a simple way to feel them work. A handy memory aid is "PAD": Palmar ADduct, with the dorsal interossei abducting ("DAB").
