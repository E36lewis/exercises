A small muscle between the nose and the upper lip. It starts on the upper jaw above the first incisor tooth and attaches to the nasal septum, the wall between the nostrils, and the base of the nose.

### What it does
- Pulls the tip of the nose down.
- Helps widen the nostrils slightly.

### In daily life
It tugs the nose down a little when you speak, smile broadly or breathe in deeply through your nose.

### Why it matters
This muscle contributes to the fine movements of the nose, and is part of the face's set of small expression muscles. It is supplied by the buccal branch of the facial nerve (VII).
