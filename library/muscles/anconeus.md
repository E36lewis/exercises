A small triangular muscle on the back of the elbow, beside the triceps. It runs from the outer lower end of the humerus (upper arm bone) to the upper back of the ulna.

### What it does
- Helps the triceps straighten the elbow (extension).
- Stabilises the elbow joint and tightens its capsule.
- Moves the ulna slightly to the side when the forearm turns palm down.

### In daily life
It is active when you push a heavy door, press up from a chair, or hold your elbow steady as you write or use a screwdriver.

### Why it matters
It works with the triceps to keep the elbow steady while the arm is loaded, so it can support pressing and leaning on your hands. It is supplied by the radial nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Push up, Push up fingers, Skull crusher
