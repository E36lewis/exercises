A small muscle in the sole of the foot, along the outer edge below the little toe. It is the foot counterpart of the muscle of the same name in the hand.

### What it does
- Bends the little toe at its base joint.
- Helps the outer edge of the forefoot press into the ground.

### In daily life
It helps keep the little toe in contact with the ground as you stand, step on rough ground or push off in sandals.

### Why it matters
It is small, but the outer forefoot helps share load and balance on the outside of the foot. It is supplied by the lateral plantar nerve, a branch of the tibial nerve.
