A thin strap muscle at the front of the neck, one of the infrahyoid muscles. It runs straight up from the back of the top of the breastbone and the inner collarbone to the hyoid bone, a small U-shaped bone under the chin.

### What it does
- Pulls the hyoid bone down after it has been lifted
- Holds the hyoid steady so the muscles above it can work
- Helps lower the larynx with the other strap muscles

### In daily life
It works after every swallow, bringing the hyoid back to rest, and it helps keep the hyoid steady while you open your mouth wide to yawn or speak.

### Why it matters
It does not move the neck much, but it supports swallowing and speech. It is a good example of a small muscle that works in a team, and it is not something you train on its own.
