A small rotator cuff muscle that sits in the hollow above the spine of the shoulder blade. Its tendon passes under the acromion and attaches to the top of the upper arm bone.

### What it does
- Starts lifting the arm out to the side (abduction) in the first part of the movement
- Pulls the head of the humerus into the socket
- Works with the deltoid to carry the arm higher

### In daily life
It helps when you lift your arm to the side to take a coat off a hook, reach for a cup on a shelf or start to raise your arm to wave.

### Why it matters
Its tendon passes through a tight space and is often overloaded, so it is a common site of shoulder pain. Strengthening it, within comfortable limits, can support healthy shoulder movement.

### Exercises that train it
Single arm lateral raise, halo, windmill, kettlebell swing, seated shoulder press, clean
