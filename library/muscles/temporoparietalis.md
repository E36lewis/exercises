A thin, flat muscle in the scalp, above and slightly behind the ear. It is part of the muscle layer that includes the occipitofrontalis and attaches to the galea aponeurotica, the tough sheet that covers the top of the skull.

### What it does
- Tightens the scalp sheet
- May lift the ear a little
- May help raise the eyebrow when it works with the muscles of the forehead

### In daily life
Few people can use it deliberately. Some people can wiggle their ears or raise their eyebrows, and this muscle may take part in that tiny movement.

### Why it matters
It has very little effect on strength or posture, and it is not a training target. It matters mainly as part of the anatomy of the head and face, where small muscles help with expression.
