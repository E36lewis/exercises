A thin muscle, also called sternocostalis, on the inside of the front chest wall. It runs from the back of the lower breastbone out to the cartilages of the second to sixth ribs.

### What it does
- Pulls the rib cartilages down and in
- May assist breathing out, though its role is minor
- Helps stiffen the front chest wall

### In daily life
It is active in the background during forced breathing out, as when you blow up a balloon, cough hard or sing a long phrase.

### Why it matters
It adds a small amount of support to the front chest wall and to expiration. You can't train it on its own, but good breathing habits support trunk and chest comfort.
