Three small muscles deep in the sole of the foot, each lying along the inner side of the third, fourth and fifth long foot bones (metatarsals). Their fibres run in parallel, and they work as a small group with the other short muscles of the foot.

### What it does
- Adduct the third to fifth toes, drawing them toward the second toe
- Help flex the toes at the base joints (metatarsophalangeal joints)
- Help keep the toes straight and spread evenly under load

### In daily life
They help you grip the floor with your toes, hold a one-legged stance on a wobbly surface and keep the forefoot tidy as you push off to take a step.

### Why it matters
Healthy foot muscles help hold the arch and the toes steady, so the bigger calf and shin muscles have a firm base to work from. Training the toes is a simple way to build that base.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flexor, lateral walk
