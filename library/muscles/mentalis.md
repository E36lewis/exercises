A small, cone-shaped facial muscle in the chin. It runs from the lower jaw, near the lower front teeth, down to the skin of the chin. It is one of the few facial muscles that attaches skin to bone.

### What it does
- Lifts and wrinkles the skin of the chin.
- Pushes the lower lip up and out.
- Helps make a pout or a doubtful expression.
- Helps hold liquid and food in the mouth.

### In daily life
Pouting, looking doubtful or sad, and pushing your lower lip against the rim of a cup as you drink.

### Why it matters
It helps shape the lower lip for speech and eating, and it is part of how we show feelings through the face.
