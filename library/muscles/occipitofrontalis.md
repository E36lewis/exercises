A broad scalp muscle with two bellies joined by a tendon sheet (the galea aponeurotica) that covers the skull. The frontalis belly lies on the forehead and the occipitalis belly on the back of the head. The facial nerve serves both.

### What it does
- Raises the eyebrows (frontalis).
- Wrinkles the forehead (frontalis).
- Moves the scalp.
- Pulls the scalp back (occipitalis).

### In daily life
Showing surprise, widening your eyes and lifting your brows when you speak, and furrowing your forehead when you concentrate.

### Why it matters
It helps us show feelings and lets the eyes open wider. A headache that comes with a tight scalp or forehead may be linked to tension in it.
