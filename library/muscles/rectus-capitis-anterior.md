A short, deep muscle at the top of the neck, in front of the joint between the skull and the first neck vertebra (atlas). It runs from the side of the atlas to the base of the skull.

### What it does
- Flexes the head at the top of the neck (a small nod)
- Helps stabilise the skull on the atlas

### In daily life
It helps with the small nod of agreement and with tucking your chin as you look down at a phone or a book.

### Why it matters
It is one of the small deep neck stabilisers that give fine control of head position. It works with the larger neck muscles that carry the heavier loads.
