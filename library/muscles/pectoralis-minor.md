A thin triangular muscle lying under the pectoralis major. It runs from ribs 3 to 5 to the coracoid process, a small bony hook on the front of the shoulder blade.

### What it does
- Pulls the shoulder blade forward (protraction) and down.
- Tips the shoulder blade forward.
- Lifts the ribs on a forced breath when the shoulder blade is fixed.

### In daily life
Reaching forward, pushing a cart and slumping at a desk.

### Why it matters
When it is tight, it can pull the shoulders forward and round the upper back. Mobility work for the chest and strengthening the upper back can help balance it.

### Exercises that train it
Push up, Bench press, Wide chest, Side push up, Pull up, Pull over
