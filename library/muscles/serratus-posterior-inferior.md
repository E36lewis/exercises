A thin, flat muscle in the lower back. It runs from the spinous processes of the lowest thoracic and upper lumbar vertebrae up and out to the lower four ribs.

### What it does
- Pulls the lower ribs down and back
- May steady the lower chest wall during breathing out and twisting

### In daily life
It may help during deep breathing and when you twist your trunk, for example to reach behind you.

### Why it matters
Its exact role is small and is still discussed, but it is thought to support the lower rib cage and breathing mechanics. It also lies over the larger back muscles.
