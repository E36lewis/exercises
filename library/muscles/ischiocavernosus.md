A small paired muscle of the perineum, the area between the legs. It starts on the lower edge of the sit bone (ischium) and wraps over the root of the penis or clitoris. Each side has one. It sits just outside the deep pelvic floor, in the layer below it.

### What it does
- Squeezes the root of the penis or clitoris, which helps maintain an erection.
- Adds a little firm support to the base of the pelvis.
- Is supplied by the pudendal nerve.

### In daily life
Its work is mostly in sexual function. It also takes part in the body's bracing reflex when you cough or lift.

### Why it matters
Healthy pelvic muscles can support sexual function and control of the bladder and bowel, and pelvic floor exercises can help train the group.

### Exercises that train it
Squat, Step up, Pistol squat
