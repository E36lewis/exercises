A small rotator cuff muscle on the back of the shoulder blade, just above the teres major. It runs from the upper outer edge of the scapula to the greater tubercle of the upper arm bone.

### What it does
- Rotates the arm outward (external rotation)
- Holds the head of the humerus against the back of the socket
- Helps bring the arm toward the body and back

### In daily life
It helps when you reach back to comb your hair, wind up to throw a ball or pull your arm out of a sleeve.

### Why it matters
Working with the infraspinatus, it turns the arm outward and keeps the joint centred. Training it can support shoulder balance, especially for people who press or throw often.

### Exercises that train it
Rotatory cuff external rotation, banded pull apart, wide chest, single arm lateral raise
