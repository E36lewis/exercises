A small, flat muscle on the top of the foot. It is the innermost part of the extensor digitorum brevis and is often treated as a separate muscle. It starts on the heel bone and ends on the first bone of the big toe.

### What it does
- Extends (lifts) the big toe at its base joint.

### In daily life
It helps raise the big toe just before the foot lands, and it adds to the lift of the longer big-toe extensor when you stand on a slope or step onto a curb.

### Why it matters
A stiff or weak big toe can change how you push off when you walk. This small muscle supports the toe's ability to lift and spread load across the foot. It is supplied by the deep fibular nerve.
