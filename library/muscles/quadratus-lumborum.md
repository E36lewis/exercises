A deep muscle of the lower back, on each side of the lumbar spine, roughly square in shape. It runs from the top of the pelvis (iliac crest) to the twelfth rib and the sides of the upper four lumbar vertebrae.

### What it does
- Side-bends the trunk when one side works
- Helps extend the lumbar spine when both sides work together
- Hikes the hip on the same side and anchors the twelfth rib when you breathe

### In daily life
It works when you lean sideways to pick something up, carry a bag on one side or walk, keeping the pelvis level with every step.

### Why it matters
It is a key stabiliser between the pelvis and the ribs, so it supports upright posture and can help during one-sided loads such as suitcase carries and single-leg work.

### Exercises that train it
Reverse hyperextension, hyperextension, back extension, side plank, stability ball front plank, turkish get up
