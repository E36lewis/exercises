A flat muscle at the back of the neck, lower than the splenius capitis and partly beneath it. It runs from the spinous processes of the upper chest vertebrae up to the transverse processes of the top few neck vertebrae.

### What it does
- Extends the neck when both sides work
- Rotates the neck to the same side
- Side-bends the neck to the same side

### In daily life
It helps when you look over your shoulder while reversing a car, lean back to see a high shelf or hold your neck steady while a friend talks to you from the side.

### Why it matters
It anchors the upper neck vertebrae to the upper back, so it can help keep the head from drifting forward. Even, balanced neck training can support posture over long periods of sitting.

### Exercises that train it
Neck pull, neck stretch, neck banded hold
