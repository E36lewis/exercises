A small facial muscle, deep to the other muscles that lift the lip, on each side of the face. It starts on the upper jaw, in the small hollow below the cheekbone, and runs down to the corner of the mouth. The facial nerve supplies it, as it does all the muscles of expression.

### What it does
- Lifts the corner of the mouth.
- Takes part in smiling.
- Deepens the fold that runs from the nose to the mouth corner.

### In daily life
Smiling at a friend, laughing at a joke, and showing warmth in a greeting.

### Why it matters
Facial expression is a large part of how people read each other. This muscle helps give a smile its shape and can aid clear, relaxed speech.
