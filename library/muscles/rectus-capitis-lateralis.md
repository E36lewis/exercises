A short, deep muscle at the top of the neck. It runs from the side projection of the first neck vertebra (atlas) to the base of the skull, just behind the ear.

### What it does
- Bends the head to the same side (lateral flexion)
- Helps stabilise the skull on the atlas

### In daily life
It helps you tilt your head toward a shoulder, for example when you cradle a phone or look into a side mirror.

### Why it matters
It supports fine head balance and the sense of head position, which can help keep the upper neck steady.
