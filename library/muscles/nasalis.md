A thin facial muscle over the nose, in two parts. The transverse part runs across the bridge of the nose and the alar part sits at the side of each nostril. It is served by the facial nerve.

### What it does
- Flares the nostrils (the alar part).
- Compresses the bridge of the nose (the transverse part).
- Narrows the nostrils when you sniff or press air through the nose.
- Adds to expressions such as anger or distaste.

### In daily life
Breathing hard during a run, taking a deep sniff, and wrinkling your nose at a bad smell.

### Why it matters
It keeps the nostrils open when you need more air, which can help nasal breathing during hard effort.
