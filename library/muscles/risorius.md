A thin facial muscle that runs sideways from the corner of the mouth toward the cheek. It is small and varies from person to person.

### What it does
- Pulls the corner of the mouth sideways
- Helps make a tight-lipped smile or grimace

### In daily life
It works when you smile broadly, grimace or pull your mouth wide when you strain hard.

### Why it matters
It is an expression muscle with no role in training, but it supports natural expression.
