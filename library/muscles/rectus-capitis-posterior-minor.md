A small, flat muscle at the back of the upper neck, beside the posterior major. It runs from the first neck vertebra (atlas) to the base of the skull, close to the midline.

### What it does
- Extends the head at the top of the neck
- Helps stabilise the skull on the atlas

### In daily life
It helps when you tip your head back to look up, or hold your head steady while you read or work at a screen.

### Why it matters
It supports fine head control and works with its neighbours to keep the upper neck steady. Its small size means gentle, steady neck work suits it best.

### Exercises that train it
Neck banded hold, neck pull, neck stretch
