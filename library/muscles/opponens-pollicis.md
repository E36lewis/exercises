A small triangular muscle at the base of the thumb, deep to abductor pollicis brevis. It runs from the trapezium (a wrist bone) and the flexor retinaculum to the whole length of the first metacarpal, the thumb's long hand bone.

### What it does
- Brings the thumb across the palm to meet the fingertips (opposition).
- Rotates the thumb bone inward.
- Helps make the thumb pad face the finger pads.

### In daily life
Pinching, writing with a pen, using a phone or touching your thumb to each fingertip.

### Why it matters
Opposition is the movement that makes the human hand so useful for grasping and using tools. Losing it makes tasks like pinching and gripping hard.
