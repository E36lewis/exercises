A thick chewing muscle on the inside of the lower jaw. It runs from the sphenoid bone and palate (the roof of the mouth) down to the inner surface of the jaw angle. Its fibres run in the same direction as the masseter's, so the two together form a sling around the angle of the jaw.

### What it does
- Closes the jaw.
- Moves the jaw forward when both sides work.
- Moves the jaw towards the opposite side when one side works, which is the grinding motion.
- Works with the masseter to lift the jaw with force.

### In daily life
Chewing and grinding food, such as a steak or raw carrot, and clenching.

### Why it matters
It is a main power muscle for chewing and for the side-to-side grinding that breaks food down. It is hidden inside the jaw, so jaw massage usually reaches it only from inside the mouth.
