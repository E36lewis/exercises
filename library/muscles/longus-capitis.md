A deep muscle at the front of the neck, lying on the front of the upper neck vertebrae. It starts on the front of the side projections of the third to sixth neck vertebrae and runs up to the underside of the skull at the base. It belongs to the prevertebral muscles, deep to the throat. The front branches of the upper neck nerves supply it.

### What it does
- Nods the head forward (flexes the head).
- Helps bend the neck forward.
- Helps tilt the head to the same side when one side works alone.

### In daily life
Nodding yes, looking down at a phone, tucking your chin to sit tall, and gently lifting your head from a pillow.

### Why it matters
It is one of the deep neck flexors, which steady the head on the spine. Training them can help with neck posture and comfort.
