A long muscle on the front of the shin, just outside the shin bone. It runs from the upper outer shin down to the inner side of the midfoot and the base of the big toe's metatarsal.

### What it does
- Dorsiflexes the ankle (lifts the foot up toward the shin)
- Inverts the foot (turns the sole inward)
- Supports the inner arch of the foot
- Lowers the foot in a controlled way after the heel strikes the ground

### In daily life
It lifts your toes so your foot clears the ground in each step, and it lets you rock back onto your heels when you stop on a slope or stand in a queue.

### Why it matters
When it is weak, the foot can slap the ground and trip you. Training it can help with ankle control and may ease shin soreness from running or walking downhill.

### Exercises that train it
Downward dog, upward dog, lunge, goblet squat, toe flexor, foot arch roll
