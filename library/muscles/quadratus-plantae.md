A small, flat muscle deep in the sole of the foot, also called the flexor accessorius. It runs from the heel bone (calcaneus) to the tendon of the long toe flexor.

### What it does
- Helps flex the four smaller toes
- Straightens the pull of the long toe flexor tendon, which would otherwise tug the toes toward the inner side of the foot

### In daily life
It helps you curl your toes to grip the ground, pick something up with your foot and hold your balance on tiptoe.

### Why it matters
It adds steady pressure from the toes during push-off. Training it can support toe strength and foot control, which helps when you walk and run.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flexor, toe flex, toe object pick up, calf raise, calf hop
