A large, flat, triangular muscle across the upper back and back of the neck. It runs from the skull and the spine, down to the chest spine, out to the collarbone, the acromion and the spine of the shoulder blade, in upper, middle and lower parts.

### What it does
- Elevates the shoulder blade with the upper fibres (shrug)
- Pulls the shoulder blades together with the middle fibres
- Rotates the blade upward and pulls it down with the lower fibres
- Extends and side-bends the neck when the upper fibres work

### In daily life
It works when you shrug in doubt, carry a bag on one shoulder, reach to a high shelf and keep your head up while you sit at a desk.

### Why it matters
Its three parts guide the shoulder blade in nearly every arm movement. Training all three evenly can support shoulder posture and may ease neck and shoulder tension.

### Exercises that train it
Overhead press, neck pull, neck stretch, sumo high pull, renegade row, side arm raise feet
