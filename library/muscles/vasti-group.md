The three vastus muscles of the quadriceps: lateralis on the outer thigh, medialis on the inner thigh and intermedius deep between them. All run from the thigh bone to the kneecap, then through the patellar tendon to the shin bone. The fourth quadriceps muscle, the rectus femoris, is separate.

### What it does
- Extends the knee (straightens the leg)
- Controls the knee as it bends under load, working while it lengthens
- Helps the kneecap track in its groove (the vastus medialis especially)

### In daily life
They work when you stand up from a chair, climb stairs, lower into a squat to pick something up and walk down a hill.

### Why it matters
They are the main source of leg-straightening power, so they support walking, running and jumping. Strong vasti can help protect the knee by sharing the load across the joint.

### Exercises that train it
Lunge, side lunge, bulgarian split squat, step up, deadlift, running
