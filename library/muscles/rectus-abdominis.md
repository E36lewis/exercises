The long, flat "six-pack" muscle on the front of the abdomen. It runs in two vertical bands from the pubic bone up to the cartilage of the lower ribs and the lower end of the breastbone. Bands of tendon across it make the blocks.

### What it does
- Flexes the trunk (curls the ribs toward the pelvis)
- Tilts the pelvis backward
- Helps keep the abdomen firm when you lift and breathe out hard

### In daily life
It works when you sit up in bed, cough, laugh or brace your trunk as you pick up a box.

### Why it matters
It adds trunk strength and works with the obliques and deeper core to help protect the spine under load. How visible it is depends on body fat, not only strength.

### Exercises that train it
Reverse crunches, v up, hanging leg raise, situp, plank, deadbug
