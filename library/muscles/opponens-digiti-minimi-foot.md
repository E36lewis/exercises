A small muscle in the sole near the little toe. In most people it is not a separate muscle: it is either absent or a deep slip of flexor digiti minimi brevis, running from the long plantar ligament to the fifth metatarsal.

### What it does
- Helps bend the fifth (little) toe.
- Pulls the fifth metatarsal slightly towards the sole.
- Supports the outer edge of the foot.

### In daily life
Keeping your balance on uneven ground and gripping with the outside of the foot as you push off.

### Why it matters
Because it is small and not always present, it adds little to everyday strength. Its job in a typical foot is shared with the flexor of the little toe.
