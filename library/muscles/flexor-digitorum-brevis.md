A muscle in the middle of the sole, just above the plantar fascia (the thick band along the sole). It starts on the heel bone and splits into four tendons that go to the middle bones of the four lesser toes.

### What it does
- Bends the four lesser toes at their middle joints.
- Helps hold up the long arch of the foot.
- Helps the toes grip the ground.

### In daily life
It lets you grip the floor with your toes when you balance, curl them around a stair edge or pick up a sock.

### Why it matters
Strength in the small muscles of the sole can help support the arch and spread load across the forefoot, which matters for standing, walking and running. It is supplied by the medial plantar nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Toe flexor, Toe flex, Toe object pick up, Foot arch roll
