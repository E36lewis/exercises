The two-headed muscle on the front of the upper arm. The long head starts above the shoulder socket and the short head on the coracoid process of the shoulder blade. They join and attach to the radius (a forearm bone) near the elbow.

### What it does
- Bends the elbow (flexion).
- Turns the forearm so the palm faces up (supination).
- Helps raise the arm forward at the shoulder.

### In daily life
It bends your arm to carry a shopping bag, and it turns your hand palm up when you tighten a screw or take a bowl from a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It is a strong flexor and supinator, so it matters in carrying, climbing and pulling. The long head also helps steady the front of the shoulder. It is supplied by the musculocutaneous nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Bicep curl, Drag curl, Incline bicep curl, Pull up, Alternating pushup row, Renegade row
