A thin band of muscle in the deep perineum of women. It arches over the front of the urethra and attaches to the pubic and sit bones on each side. It is part of the external urethral sphincter complex.

### What it does
- Squeezes the urethra shut to hold urine in.
- Works with the urethral sphincter to control the flow.

### In daily life
It tightens when you laugh, sneeze or hurry for a bus with a full bladder.

### Why it matters
It adds closing force to the urethra, which supports bladder control. It is supplied by the perineal branch of the pudendal nerve.
