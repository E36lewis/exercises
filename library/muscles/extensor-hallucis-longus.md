A slim muscle in the front compartment of the shin, between the tibialis anterior and the extensor digitorum longus. Its tendon crosses the front of the ankle and runs along the top of the foot to the tip of the big toe. You can see the tendon stand out when you pull the big toe up.

### What it does
- Straightens (extends) the big toe.
- Helps pull the foot up toward the shin (dorsiflexion).
- Helps turn the sole slightly inward (inversion).

### In daily life
It lifts the big toe when you step over a threshold, keeps the toe clear on stairs and helps you rock back onto your heels.

### Why it matters
The big toe carries a large share of your balance. Control of the toe's lift can help with a smoother stride and steadier standing on one leg. It is supplied by the deep fibular nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Tip toe extension, Upward dog
