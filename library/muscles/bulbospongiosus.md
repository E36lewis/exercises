A small muscle of the pelvic floor, in the perineum (the area between the legs). In men it wraps the base of the penis. In women it lies on each side of the vaginal opening.

### What it does
- Squeezes the urethra to help empty its last drops of urine.
- Helps with ejaculation in men.
- Narrows the vaginal opening in women.

### In daily life
It contracts without your thinking about it at the end of urination, and during sexual activity.

### Why it matters
Along with the other pelvic floor muscles, it plays a part in urinary control and sexual function. It is supplied by the perineal branch of the pudendal nerve.
