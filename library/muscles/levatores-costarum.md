Twelve small, triangular muscles on each side of the upper back. Each starts on the side projection (transverse process) of a vertebra and runs down and outward to the rib just below. The lower ones may reach the second rib below. They are supplied by the back branches of the spinal nerves.

### What it does
- Lift the ribs slightly when you breathe in.
- Help extend and rotate the spine a little.
- Bend the spine to the same side when one side works.

### In daily life
Taking a deep breath before diving into a pool, a long sigh, and slight turns of the upper back as you reach for something.

### Why it matters
They help the rib cage move with each breath and stay connected to the thoracic spine. That supports free breathing and upper-back mobility.
