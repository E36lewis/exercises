A thick, rounded muscle on the back of the shoulder, just below the teres minor. It runs from the lower outer edge of the shoulder blade to the front of the upper arm bone, in the groove beside the latissimus dorsi tendon.

### What it does
- Extends the arm (pulls it down and back)
- Adducts the arm (brings it toward the body)
- Rotates the arm inward

### In daily life
It works when you pull a rope toward your hip, push yourself up off the arms of a chair or reach into a back pocket.

### Why it matters
It is nicknamed the "lat's little helper" because it follows the latissimus dorsi in nearly every pulling movement. It can help add strength and shape to the back of the shoulder in rows and pull-ups.

### Exercises that train it
Row, single arm bent over row, bent over row dual weight, pull up, pull over, banded kneeling one arm pull
