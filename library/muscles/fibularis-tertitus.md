A small muscle at the lower front of the shin that is really a slip of the extensor digitorum longus. It runs to the base of the fifth metatarsal. Not everyone has one, and it is thought to be absent in a notable minority of people.

### What it does
- Pulls the foot up toward the shin (dorsiflexion).
- Helps turn the sole of the foot outward (eversion).

### In daily life
It helps clear the outer edge of your foot from the ground as you swing the leg forward, and it keeps the foot from dropping inward when you walk downhill.

### Why it matters
It is part of the group that controls the ankle on the way down after a heel strike. The library data places it among the muscles of the outer side of the leg, but it sits in the front compartment. It is supplied by the deep fibular nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Running, Lateral leap
