The middle of the three layers of muscle between the ribs, lying beneath the external intercostals. Their fibres run from the lower edge of one rib to the rib below, slanting downward and backward, at right angles to the layer outside them. The intercostal nerves supply them.

### What it does
- Pull the ribs down and together.
- Assist forceful breathing out.
- Help twist and bend the trunk by tensioning the chest wall.

### In daily life
Singing a long note, blowing out birthday candles, sneezing, and breathing hard during a sprint.

### Why it matters
Along with the external layer, they keep the rib cage working as one unit when you breathe and rotate. That can help with endurance and core stability.

### Exercises that train it
Press sit up, Banded core twist
