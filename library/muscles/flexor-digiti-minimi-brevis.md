A small muscle in the hand, on the pad below the little finger (the hypothenar eminence). It runs from the hook of the hamate bone at the wrist to the base of the little finger.

### What it does
- Bends the little finger at its base joint (knuckle).
- Helps cup the outer edge of the palm around an object.

### In daily life
It tightens your hold when you carry a shopping bag handle with the outer fingers, hold a bat or racket, or play a string instrument.

### Why it matters
The outer fingers give much of the power in a grip, and this muscle gives the little finger its share. Loss of its function can make the hand feel weak. It is supplied by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve.
