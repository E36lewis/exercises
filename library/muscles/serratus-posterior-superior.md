A thin, flat muscle in the upper back, under the rhomboids. It runs from the lower neck and upper thoracic spine out to the second to fifth ribs.

### What it does
- Lifts the upper ribs
- May help breathing in

### In daily life
It may assist when you take a deep breath, such as after a sprint or when you climb a flight of stairs.

### Why it matters
It supports the upper rib cage during breathing. It is small and works with larger muscles, so its effect is subtle.

### Exercises that train it
Twisting spine, turkish get up
