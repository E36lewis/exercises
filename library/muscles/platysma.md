A thin, wide sheet of muscle just under the skin at the front and side of the neck. It runs from the skin over the upper chest and shoulder up across the collarbone and jaw to the lower face.

### What it does
- Pulls the corners of the mouth and the lower lip downward
- Tenses the skin of the neck and shows the cords beneath it
- Gives a small assist in lowering the jaw

### In daily life
It tightens when you grimace, look shocked, strain hard or open your mouth wide, which is why you can see it ripple in the neck during a heavy effort.

### Why it matters
It is mainly an expression muscle, not a neck mover. It can still be a handy sign of how much tension you are carrying in the face and neck during hard work.

### Exercises that train it
Neck pull, neck stretch
