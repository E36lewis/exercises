A long, thin muscle on the front and outer side of the shin. Its tendon passes under the front of the ankle, then splits into four to reach the four lesser toes. It lies in the front (anterior) compartment of the leg, beside the tibialis anterior.

### What it does
- Pulls the foot up toward the shin (dorsiflexion).
- Straightens the four lesser toes.
- Helps turn the sole of the foot outward (eversion).

### In daily life
It lifts your toes so they do not catch on the ground as you walk, and it lowers your foot under control when you walk downhill or down stairs.

### Why it matters
It works with the other shin muscles to hold the ankle steady. Tight or tired shin muscles are a common complaint in runners, and building control here can help. It is supplied by the deep fibular nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Tip toe extension, Upward dog
