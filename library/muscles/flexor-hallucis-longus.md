A long muscle deep in the calf. It starts on the back of the fibula, runs behind the inner ankle bone through a groove under the heel bone, and ends on the tip of the big toe.

### What it does
- Curls the big toe down, giving the last push as you leave the ground.
- Helps point the foot down (plantar flexion) and turn the sole inward.
- Supports the inner arch.

### In daily life
It helps you rise onto your toes, push off to run or jump, and keeps the big toe pressed into the ground on stairs.

### Why it matters
Ballet dancers and runners use it a great deal, and its tendon can be irritated behind the ankle. Strength here helps balance and push-off. It is supplied by the tibial nerve.

### Exercises that train it
High knees, Jump squat, Lateral leap, Twisting jump, Gap jump, Toe flex
