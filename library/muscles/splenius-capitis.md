A flat, strap-like muscle at the back of the neck, lying under the trapezius. It runs from the lower neck and upper chest spine up and outward to the skull behind the ear (the mastoid process).

### What it does
- Extends the head and neck when both sides work (looks up)
- Turns the head to the same side when one side works
- Tilts the head toward the same side

### In daily life
It works when you turn to check your blind spot, tip your head back to look at a ceiling or shelf and keep your head from dropping forward while you read.

### Why it matters
It is one of the main muscles holding the head up against gravity. Strengthening it, and keeping it from getting stiff, can support neck posture and comfort during desk work.

### Exercises that train it
Neck pull, neck stretch, neck banded hold, camel
