The large three-headed muscle on the back of the upper arm. The long head starts on the shoulder blade, and the lateral and medial heads start on the upper arm bone. All three join in one tendon that attaches to the olecranon, the point of the elbow.

### What it does
- Extends the elbow (straightens the arm)
- The long head also extends and adducts the shoulder
- Holds the elbow steady against a load in pressing

### In daily life
It works when you push a door open, press yourself up from a chair or lock your elbow out to put a bag on a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It is the main muscle for straightening the arm, so it supports every press, push-up and throw. A strong triceps also helps protect the elbow joint under load.

### Exercises that train it
Tricep dip, skull crusher, bench press, incline bench press, double press, pull over
