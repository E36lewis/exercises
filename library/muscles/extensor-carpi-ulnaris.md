A muscle on the back of the forearm, on the little-finger side. It starts from the common extensor tendon on the outer elbow and from the ulna, and attaches to the base of the fifth metacarpal, behind the little finger.

### What it does
- Extends the wrist (bends the hand back).
- Tilts the wrist toward the little-finger side (ulnar deviation).

### In daily life
It pulls your hand back and toward the little-finger side when you swing a golf club, wring a cloth or pour from a heavy kettle.

### Why it matters
It balances the pull of the radial wrist extensors so that the hand can extend straight, and it works with the flexor carpi ulnaris to steady the wrist. It is supplied by the posterior interosseous nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Wrist curl palm down, Finger extensions, Lean back forearm, Side to side forearm, Push up fingers, Hammer curl
