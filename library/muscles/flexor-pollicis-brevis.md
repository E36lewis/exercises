A short muscle in the thenar eminence, the fleshy pad at the base of the thumb on the palm side. It has a superficial head and a deep head and ends on the first bone of the thumb.

### What it does
- Bends the thumb at its base joint (the joint nearest the wrist).
- Helps bring the thumb across the palm toward the little finger.

### In daily life
It helps you pinch a coin, press a phone's screen with your thumb and hold a key.

### Why it matters
The thumb makes up a large share of hand function, and this muscle gives it much of its pinch strength. The superficial head is supplied by the recurrent branch of the median nerve; the deep head is often supplied by the ulnar nerve.
