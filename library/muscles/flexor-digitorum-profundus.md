A deep muscle on the front of the forearm. It arises from the upper ulna and runs under the flexor digitorum superficialis. Its four tendons end at the tips of the fingers, and it is the only muscle that bends the end joint of each finger.

### What it does
- Bends the fingertips (the distal joints).
- Helps bend the other finger joints and the wrist.
- Gives you your hold on a bar or rope.

### In daily life
It lets you hang from a bar, climb a rope, grip luggage or hold a rock when climbing.

### Why it matters
It is the muscle that does most of the work in a firm grip, which matters for pulling, carrying and many sports. The index and middle fingers are supplied by the anterior interosseous branch of the median nerve, and the ring and little fingers by the ulnar nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Farmer carry, Wrist curl palm up, Side to side forearm, Lean back forearm, Bicep curl
