A slim muscle on the inner side of the upper arm. It runs from the coracoid process of the shoulder blade to the middle of the humerus, and its tendon is found next to the short head of the biceps.

### What it does
- Lifts the arm forward at the shoulder (flexion).
- Pulls the arm in toward the body (adduction).
- Helps keep the head of the humerus in its socket.

### In daily life
It brings your arm forward and across in a swing, such as when you toss a ball underarm or swing your arms as you walk briskly.

### Why it matters
It is a small stabiliser at the front of the shoulder, and its nerve is a useful landmark: the musculocutaneous nerve passes through the muscle on its way to the biceps and brachialis.

### Exercises that train it
Kettlebell swing, Single arm swing, Halo, Clean from hang, Arm clean, Side lean
