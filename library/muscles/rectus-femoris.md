One of the four quadriceps muscles, on the front of the thigh. It runs from the front of the pelvis (anterior inferior iliac spine) to the kneecap and, through the patellar tendon, to the shin bone. It is the only quadriceps muscle that also crosses the hip.

### What it does
- Extends the knee (straightens the leg)
- Flexes the hip (lifts the thigh)

### In daily life
It works when you stand up from a chair, climb stairs, kick a ball or swing your leg forward as you walk.

### Why it matters
Because it works at both the hip and the knee, it is important for running, kicking and jumping. It can be limited by tight hip flexors, so squats and lunges both train and test it.

### Exercises that train it
Lunge, pistol squat, hack squat, front squat, high knees, leg press
