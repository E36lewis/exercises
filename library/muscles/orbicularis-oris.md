A circular muscle that forms the lips and surrounds the mouth. It blends with the other muscles that pull on the lips, and it is the base the cheek muscle (buccinator) works against.

### What it does
- Closes the lips.
- Purses the lips, as for a whistle or a kiss.
- Shapes the lips for speech sounds such as "p", "b" and "oo".
- Presses the lips against the teeth and seals the mouth.

### In daily life
Whistling, drinking through a straw, blowing out candles, speaking and keeping food in your mouth while you chew.

### Why it matters
It keeps food and drink in the mouth and shapes many sounds. It is important for playing wind instruments.
