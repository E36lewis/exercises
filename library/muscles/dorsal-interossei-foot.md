Four small muscles that sit between the long bones of the foot (the metatarsals). They are bipennate, with each one arising from two neighbouring bones, and they fan out from the second toe.

### What it does
- Spread the toes apart from the second toe (abduction).
- Help bend the base of the toes and straighten their tips.
- Steady the toes against the ground.

### In daily life
They spread and stabilise your toes when you stand on tiptoe, balance in a yoga pose, or push off the ground during a run.

### Why it matters
They hold the forefoot steady and give the toes grip, which can help with balance. They are supplied by the lateral plantar nerve.
