A fan-shaped muscle deep in the web between the thumb and index finger. Its oblique head starts at the wrist and the base of the middle bones of the hand. Its transverse head starts on the third metacarpal. Both insert at the base of the thumb.

### What it does
- Draws the thumb toward the palm and index finger (adduction).
- Powers the squeeze in a pinch and in a grip.

### In daily life
It presses the thumb against the side of the index finger when you turn a key, hold a sheet of paper, or hold a carrier bag by the handle.

### Why it matters
It gives the thumb most of its force in pinching and gripping, so it matters in any task that needs the thumb to hold against the fingers. It is supplied by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve.
