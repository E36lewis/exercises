A group of deep back and neck muscles (capitis, cervicis and thoracis) that run along the spine. Their fibres go from the transverse processes of the vertebrae up to the spinous processes above, and, for the capitis part, to the back of the skull.

### What it does
- Extend the head, neck and upper spine
- Rotate the spine to the opposite side when one side works

### In daily life
They help you look up, hold your head upright when you read, or turn your upper body to look behind you.

### Why it matters
The capitis part is a major muscle that holds the head upright against gravity. Strong, supple semispinalis can help neck posture, especially when you sit for long periods.
