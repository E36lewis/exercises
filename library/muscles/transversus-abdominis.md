The deepest layer of the abdominal wall. Its fibres run straight across the trunk like a corset, from the lower ribs, the lower back fascia and the pelvis to the front midline (the linea alba).

### What it does
- Compresses the abdomen, drawing the belly in
- Raises pressure inside the abdomen to steady the spine and pelvis
- Helps push air out during a forced breath

### In daily life
It tightens before you pick up a shopping bag, cough, laugh or lift a child, giving the trunk a firm base.

### Why it matters
It acts early in many movements, so it supports core control and a steady spine. Training it can help you brace and carry loads safely.

### Exercises that train it
Bird dog, ball slam, single leg deadlift, wood choppers, press sit up, reverse crunches
