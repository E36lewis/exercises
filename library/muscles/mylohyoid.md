A flat, triangular muscle that forms the floor of the mouth. It runs from the inner surface of the lower jaw to the hyoid bone and to a fibrous seam in the midline, where the two sides meet.

### What it does
- Raises the hyoid bone and the floor of the mouth.
- Lifts and pushes the tongue up and back against the roof of the mouth.
- Helps lower the jaw when the hyoid is held steady.
- Starts the swallow.

### In daily life
Swallowing food or water, speaking and opening your mouth wide to yawn.

### Why it matters
Its lift of the hyoid and tongue is one of the first steps of a safe swallow. Healthy swallowing relies on it working with the other muscles under the chin.
