A small, flat muscle at the front of the neck. It runs from the thyroid cartilage (the Adam's apple) up to the hyoid bone, and looks like an upward continuation of the sternothyroid.

### What it does
- Lowers the hyoid bone when the larynx is fixed
- Raises the larynx toward the hyoid when the hyoid is fixed
- Helps shorten the gap between the two during swallowing

### In daily life
It works when you swallow, hum or sing a note, as the voice box slides up and the hyoid shifts to meet it.

### Why it matters
It helps the larynx and hyoid move together so swallowing is smooth and the airway stays protected. It is not a muscle you train, but it shows how many small throat muscles share the work.
