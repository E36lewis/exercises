A flat, four-sided muscle of the chin. It runs up from the lower edge of the jaw to the skin and red margin of the lower lip.

### What it does
- Pulls the lower lip down and slightly out to the side.

### In daily life
It lowers your lower lip when you say a word like "ah", take a sip from a cup, or push your lip out in a pout.

### Why it matters
It helps you open your mouth to eat, drink and speak, and adds to a range of expressions. It is supplied by the marginal mandibular branch of the facial nerve.
