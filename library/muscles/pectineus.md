A short, flat muscle at the top of the inner thigh, in the floor of the femoral triangle (the groin crease). It runs from the pubic bone to a line on the back of the upper thigh bone, and it is the most forward of the adductor group.

### What it does
- Bends the hip (flexion).
- Brings the thigh inward (adduction).
- Turns the thigh slightly outward.
- Helps keep the pelvis level.

### In daily life
Lifting your knee to climb a step, stepping sideways and squeezing your knees together.

### Why it matters
It works in both the hip flexor and adductor groups. Groin strains can involve it, and gentle strengthening and mobility work can help keep the area resilient.

### Exercises that train it
Hip adduction, Squat jack, Frog leap, Lying floor leg raise, Swing kick, Circle run
