Four small muscles in the spaces between the long bones of the hand (the metacarpals), on the back of the hand. They fan out from the middle finger and each arises from two neighbouring bones.

### What it does
- Spread the fingers apart from the middle finger (abduction).
- Help bend the knuckle joints and straighten the finger tips through the extensor tendons.

### In daily life
They let you spread your fingers to stretch over piano keys, catch a basketball, or press your hand flat against a wall.

### Why it matters
They give the hand fine control of finger spacing, which is needed when you hold large objects, and they work with the lumbricals for precise finger movements. They are supplied by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve.
