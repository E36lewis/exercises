A muscle under the jaw with two bellies joined by a tendon that is held to the hyoid bone (a small U-shaped bone in the neck). The front belly attaches to the chin, and the back belly to the skull behind the ear.

### What it does
- Opens the mouth by lowering the jaw.
- Lifts the hyoid bone during swallowing and speaking.

### In daily life
It opens your mouth for a big bite or a yawn, and lifts the hyoid at the start of every swallow.

### Why it matters
It is one of the suprahyoid muscles that coordinate chewing and swallowing. Its two bellies have different nerves: the back belly is supplied by the facial nerve (VII), and the front belly by the nerve to the mylohyoid.
