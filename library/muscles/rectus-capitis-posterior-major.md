A small, deep muscle at the back of the upper neck, one of the four suboccipital muscles. It runs from the spine of the second neck vertebra (axis) up to the base of the skull.

### What it does
- Extends the head (tilts it back)
- Rotates the head to the same side

### In daily life
It helps when you look up at a shelf, tilt your head back to drink or turn to check over your shoulder.

### Why it matters
The suboccipital muscles help fine-tune head position, and they can get overworked when you hold your head forward at a desk. Gentle neck movement can help them stay supple.

### Exercises that train it
Neck banded hold, neck pull, neck stretch
