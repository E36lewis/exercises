A small muscle along the outer edge of the palm, forming the fleshy pad below the little finger. It is one of the hypothenar muscles and runs from the pisiform (a small wrist bone) to the base of the little finger.

### What it does
- Moves the little finger away from the ring finger (abduction).
- Helps bend the base of the little finger.
- Widens the span of the hand when you spread your fingers.

### In daily life
It opens the outer edge of your hand when you reach for a wide jar lid, cup a large ball or stretch an octave on a piano. It also steadies the little finger when you type.

### Why it matters
This muscle gives the hand its outer span and helps the little finger take part in a firm grip. It is supplied by the deep branch of the ulnar nerve, which also serves many other small hand muscles.
