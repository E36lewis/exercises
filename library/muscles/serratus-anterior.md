A fan-shaped muscle on the side of the ribcage, under the arm. It starts on the upper eight or nine ribs and runs back to the inner edge of the shoulder blade (scapula). It is sometimes called the boxer's muscle.

### What it does
- Protracts the scapula (pulls it forward around the ribs)
- Rotates the scapula upward so the arm can lift overhead
- Holds the shoulder blade flat against the ribcage

### In daily life
It helps when you throw a punch, push a heavy door or reach for something on a high shelf.

### Why it matters
It supports shoulder stability and healthy overhead movement, and keeps the shoulder blade from lifting off the ribs when you push.

### Exercises that train it
Bench press, single arm press, seesaw press, shoulder stand, hand stand push up, pushup hands on bosu ball
