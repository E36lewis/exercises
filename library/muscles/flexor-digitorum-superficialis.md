A muscle on the front of the forearm, lying over the flexor digitorum profundus. It starts at the inner elbow and the upper radius and ulna. Its four tendons split at the fingers so the deeper tendons can pass through them, and they end on the middle bones of the fingers.

### What it does
- Bends the middle joints of the four fingers.
- Helps bend the knuckles and the wrist.
- Lets each finger bend somewhat on its own.

### In daily life
It closes your hand around a mug, a steering wheel or a pen, and it bends your fingers when you type or play guitar chords.

### Why it matters
It adds power to your grip beyond the fingertips. Together with the flexor digitorum profundus it makes the grip strong enough for lifting. It is supplied by the median nerve.

### Exercises that train it
Farmer carry, Wrist curl palm up, Side to side forearm, Lean back forearm, Bicep curl, Hammer curl
