A triangular muscle of the lower face. It starts on the lower edge of the jaw and narrows to the corner of the mouth.

### What it does
- Pulls the corner of the mouth down.
- Shapes frowns and expressions of sadness or disapproval.

### In daily life
It pulls your mouth into a downturned shape when you sulk, frown, or feel disappointed, and helps you form some words.

### Why it matters
It is one of the muscles that lets your face show feeling, which is a large part of how people read each other. It is supplied by the facial nerve (VII).
