A thin muscle lying in the skin at the heel of the hand on the little finger side. It runs from the palmar fascia (the tough sheet in the palm) to the skin of the inner edge of the hand. It is not related to palmaris longus despite the similar name.

### What it does
- Wrinkles the skin of the palm's outer edge.
- Pulls the skin towards the middle of the palm and deepens the hollow.
- Gives the skin a firmer hold when gripping.

### In daily life
Clenching the fist hard, gripping a handle and holding a heavy bag.

### Why it matters
It is small and rarely noticed. It may help to protect the ulnar nerve and artery beneath it and to make the edge of the palm a firmer grip surface.
