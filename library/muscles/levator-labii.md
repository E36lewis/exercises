A thin facial muscle group beside the nose and below the eye. It runs from the upper jaw and cheekbone to the skin of the upper lip. In standard anatomy this is the levator labii superioris, with a branch called the levator labii superioris alaeque nasi that also reaches the nostril. The facial nerve supplies it.

### What it does
- Lifts the upper lip.
- Deepens the fold from the nose to the mouth corner.
- Helps flare the nostrils (the alaeque nasi part).

### In daily life
Smiling, curling your lip at a bad smell, sounding out words, and taking food from a spoon.

### Why it matters
It gives the upper lip its movement for speech, eating and expression.
